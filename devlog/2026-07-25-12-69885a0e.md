# Security Update: DevMan RaaS Portal Centralizes Payload Builds, Victim Management, and Affiliate Payouts

_2026-07-25 12:37 UTC_

**DevLog Entry - 2026-07-25**

**Incident:** DevMan RaaS Portal Centralizes Payload Builds and Affiliate Payouts

**Summary:** A newly discovered ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) portal, DevMan, has emerged with a centralized platform for building and deploying payloads, managing victims, and facilitating affiliate payouts. This development indicates a potential increase in the sophistication and accessibility of ransomware attacks, posing a significant threat to organizations with inadequate security measures. As with any RaaS platform, DevMan's existence highlights the importance of proactive threat hunting and incident response strategies.
