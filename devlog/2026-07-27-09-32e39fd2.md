**Summary:** A new cybersecurity threat, TELESHIM, has been identified to exploit Telegram for Command and Control (C2) purposes in attacks targeting Middle East governments. This raises concerns about the potential misuse of popular communication platforms for malicious activities. Developers should remain vigilant and stay up-to-date on the latest security updates and patches for Telegram and other widely-used services.
