**Dev Log Entry - 27 July 2026**

### Cruciferra Crypter Threat Assessment

A recently discovered malware, Cruciferra Crypter, leverages BYOVD (Bring Your Own Vulnerability Detection) and process ghosting techniques to evade detection on Windows systems. This sophisticated threat exploits the BYOVD vulnerability to install malware without triggering the operating system's security mechanisms. Further research is necessary to understand the full scope of this threat and potential mitigation strategies.
