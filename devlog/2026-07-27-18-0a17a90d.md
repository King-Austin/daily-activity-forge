**Devlog Entry: Dysphoria IoT Botnet Update**
=============================================

**Date:** 27 July 2026
**Source:** [https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/dysphoria-iot-botnet-adds-blockchain-c2.html](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/dysphoria-iot-botnet-adds-blockchain-c2.html)

The Dysphoria IoT botnet has evolved to incorporate blockchain-based command and control (C2) mechanisms, enhancing its resilience and evasion capabilities. Furthermore, the botnet now utilizes compromised victim devices as relays, potentially expanding its attack surface and reach. This update underscores the importance of prioritizing IoT security and implementing robust monitoring and mitigation strategies.
