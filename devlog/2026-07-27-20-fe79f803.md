# Security Update: NVIDIA Forms 37-Member Open Secure AI Alliance and Open-Sources NOOA Framework

_2026-07-27 20:03 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/nvidia-forms-37-member-open-secure-ai.html)

**Devlog Entry: NVIDIA Open Secure AI Alliance & NOOA Framework**

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Update:** NVIDIA has formed the Open Secure AI Alliance with 37 member organizations and open-sourced the NOOA (NVIDIA Open Observatory for AI) framework to enhance AI security.

**Insights:**
The NOOA framework is designed to provide a standardized approach to securing AI systems, but its open-source nature may inadvertently expose vulnerabilities. As the AI landscape continues to evolve, the potential for security threats also grows, and the industry's response will be crucial in mitigating these risks. This development warrants close monitoring to assess its potential impact on AI system security.
