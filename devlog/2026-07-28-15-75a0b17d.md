# Security Update: Tengu Botnet Reboots Compromised Linux Devices When Defenders Kill Its Process

_2026-07-28 15:45 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/tengu-botnet-reboots-compromised-linux.html)

### Devlog Entry: Tengu Botnet Threat Assessment

#### Date: 28 July 2026

The Tengu botnet has been identified as a Linux-targeting threat that employs a unique evasion tactic: rebooting compromised devices when defenders attempt to kill its process. This behavior indicates a sophisticated adversary capable of adapting its tactics to evade manual detection and incident response efforts. Developers and security professionals must remain vigilant in monitoring Linux system logs and processes for signs of Tengu botnet activity.
