_2026-07-29 09:27 UTC_

**DevLog Entry: Check Point SmartConsole Authentication Bypass**

* **Date:** 29 July 2026
* **Summary:** A public proof-of-concept (PoC) exploit has been released for a vulnerability in Check Point's SmartConsole, allowing attackers to bypass authentication and potentially gain unauthorized access to network devices. This exploit has been confirmed to be actively exploited in the wild, emphasizing the need for immediate patching and mitigations.
