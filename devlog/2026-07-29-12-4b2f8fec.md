**Devlog Entry: Mythos and Unanswered Questions**
=====================================================

**Date:** 29 July 2026

**Summary:**

According to a recent cybersecurity update, Mythos has been identified as a threat that asks the right questions but fails to provide answers, potentially evading detection by security systems. This clever tactic exploits the assumption that attackers must provide a clear payload or request to be detected. As a result, security teams must reevaluate their detection strategies to account for this subtle yet effective threat vector.
