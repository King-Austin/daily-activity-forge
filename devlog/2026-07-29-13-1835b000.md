**Devlog Entry: Cybersecurity Readiness**

**Date:** July 29, 2026

**Summary:**

A recent survey indicates that 73% of organizations are not fully prepared to handle a major cyberattack, highlighting the pressing need for enhanced cybersecurity measures. This threat underscores the importance of proactive security strategies, including regular vulnerability assessments, employee education, and robust incident response planning. Organizations must prioritize cybersecurity readiness to mitigate the risks of a potential breach.
