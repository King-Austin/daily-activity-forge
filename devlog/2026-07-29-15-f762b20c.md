# Security Update: Nine-Year Fraud Campaign Clones Russian Company Sites to Steal Advance Payments

_2026-07-29 15:08 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/nine-year-fraud-campaign.html)

### DevLog Entry: Potential Threat - Clone-Based Phishing Campaign

**Date:** 29 July 2026
**Reference:** [https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/nine-year-fraud-campaign.html](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/nine-year-fraud-campaign.html)

A sophisticated phishing campaign has been uncovered, using clones of Russian company websites to deceive victims into making advance payments. This clone-based approach allows attackers to exploit trust in legitimate brands and evade basic security checks. Developers should remain vigilant and implement robust security measures to prevent similar phishing attempts.
