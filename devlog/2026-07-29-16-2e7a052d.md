**Devlog Entry: Ruflo MCP Flaw**

*Date:* 29 July 2026
*Summary:*

A critical vulnerability has been discovered in Ruflo MCP, allowing unauthenticated attackers to run arbitrary commands and manipulate AI memory. This flaw poses a significant risk to systems utilizing the Ruflo MCP, emphasizing the importance of prompt patching and secure configuration. Further research is required to fully understand the implications and potential attack vectors associated with this vulnerability.
