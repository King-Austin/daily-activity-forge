_2026-07-30 08:30 UTC_

**Devlog Entry: Cybersecurity Risks in Foreign-Produced Robots and Power Inverters**

**Date:** July 2026
**Summary:**

The FCC has blocked new foreign-produced robots and power inverters over concerns regarding potential cyber risks. This move highlights the growing importance of supply chain security in the face of increasing global interconnectedness. The decision underscores the need for manufacturers to ensure robust security measures in their products, particularly in critical infrastructure components.
