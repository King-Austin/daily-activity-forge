**Devlog Entry: Network-Control Plane Convergence and AI Security Risks**

**Date:** July 30, 2026
**Reference:** https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/the-network-has-become-control-plane.html

The recent shift in AI security, where the network has become the control plane, poses a significant threat as it exposes potential vulnerabilities in the network's architecture. This convergence may allow adversaries to manipulate network traffic and compromise AI decision-making processes, compromising overall system security. Monitoring network traffic and implementing robust security measures will be crucial in mitigating these risks.
