### Devlog Entry: Session Hijacking Flaw in 4G and 5G Cores

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Source:** [https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/researchers-report-84-flaws-in-4g-and.html](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/researchers-report-84-flaws-in-4g-and.html)

Researchers have discovered a critical session hijacking flaw in the 4G and 5G network cores, impacting an estimated 84 vulnerabilities. This flaw enables attackers to intercept and manipulate user sessions, posing a significant threat to network security and user data integrity. Immediate attention is required to patch and upgrade affected network infrastructure to prevent potential exploitation.
