# Security Update: Cheap Android TV Boxes Pose as Phones and Turn Owners’ Broadband Into Proxies

_2026-07-31 15:53 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/cheap-android-tv-boxes-pose-as-phones.html)

**Devlog Entry - 31 July 2026**

**Title:** Android TV Box Security Threats

**Summary:**
Recent reports indicate that cheap Android TV boxes are being repurposed as phones, potentially allowing malicious actors to exploit vulnerabilities and hijack users' broadband connections, converting them into unwitting proxies for malicious activities.

**Key Insight:** This highlights the need for robust security measures in IoT devices, including secure boot mechanisms, regular updates, and strict access controls to prevent unauthorized modifications and malicious exploitation.

**Recommendation:** Review device configurations and security settings to ensure compliance with best practices and consider implementing additional security solutions to mitigate potential risks.
