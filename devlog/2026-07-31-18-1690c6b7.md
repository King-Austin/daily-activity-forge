# Security Update

## HollowFrame Loader Deploys Matryoshka Backdoor

Summary:
A recent spear-phishing attack on a law firm has been linked to a new variant of the HollowFrame loader, which deploys the Matryoshka backdoor. This loader is capable of evading detection by traditional antivirus software and can be used to deliver various types of malware. The attack highlights the ongoing threat of sophisticated spear-phishing campaigns.
