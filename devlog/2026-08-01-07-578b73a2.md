_2026-08-01 07:55 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/hijacked-hotel-wi-fi-pushes-fake.html)

**Devlog Entry: Hotel Wi-Fi Hijacking**

**Date:** August 1, 2026

**Summary:** A recent cybersecurity update highlights a vulnerability where compromised hotel Wi-Fi networks are being hijacked to push fake software updates that deliver surveillance malware to unsuspecting guests. This attack vector is particularly concerning as it leverages the trust that guests often have in hotel-provided internet access, making it easier for attackers to execute malicious code. This threat serves as a reminder of the importance of verifying the authenticity of software updates and using secure, VPN-protected connections in public networks.
