**Date:** August 1, 2026

**Summary:** Hackers have exploited Adform's vendor tag to inject malicious scripts, which swap cryptocurrency wallet addresses on customer sites. This attack demonstrates the potential for supply chain vulnerabilities to be leveraged for financial gain. Developers should prioritize reviewing third-party scripts and libraries for potential security risks.
