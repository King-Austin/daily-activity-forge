**Devlog Entry: Thermo Fisher Patches Vulnerability in DNA Analysis Software**

* **Date:** 03 August 2026
* **Summary:** Thermo Fisher has released a patch to address a severe vulnerability in their DNA analysis software, which could have allowed attackers to tamper with DNA files without being detected. This flaw, if exploited, could have compromised the integrity of sensitive genetic data. The patch is a critical update that users should prioritize to prevent potential security breaches.
