**Devlog Entry: Cybersecurity Update - FOMO in the SOC**

**Date:** August 3, 2026
**Reference:** [https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/fomo-in-soc-where-ai-platforms-like.html](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/fomo-in-soc-where-ai-platforms-like.html)

Recent research suggests that the integration of AI platforms like Claude in Security Operations Centers (SOCs) may lead to a new form of FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) vulnerability, where over-reliance on AI-generated alerts and insights can compromise the effectiveness of human analysts. This highlights the need for a balanced approach to AI adoption in cybersecurity, ensuring that human oversight and validation are maintained.
