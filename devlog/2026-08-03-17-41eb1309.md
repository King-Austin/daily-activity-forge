**Devlog Entry: INC Ransomware Threat**

**Date:** August 3, 2026

The INC Ransomware has emerged as a dominant actor, exploiting vulnerabilities in SonicWall SMA 1000 appliances to gain unauthorized access to networks. This highlights the importance of timely patching and vulnerability management to prevent exploitation of known flaws. Organizations utilizing SonicWall SMA 1000 devices should prioritize updates and implement robust security measures to mitigate potential risks.
