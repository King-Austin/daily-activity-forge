# Security Update: CISA Adds Exploited N-able N-central Flaw to KEV After Customer Compromises

_2026-08-04 08:04 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/cisa-adds-exploited-n-able-n-central.html)

**Cybersecurity Update - N-able N-central Flaw**

*Date*: August 2026
*Source*: CISA, The Hacker News

A critical vulnerability in N-able N-central, a remote monitoring and management (RMM) platform, has been added to the CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) list after reports of customer compromises. The flaw, although unspecified, poses a significant risk to organizations relying on N-able N-central for IT management and monitoring. Monitoring and patching this vulnerability is crucial to prevent potential breaches.
