_2026-08-05 13:07 UTC_

**DevLog Entry: Kali365 Microsoft Authentication Vulnerability**
===========================================================

**Date:** August 5, 2026
**Source:** https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/kali365-weaponizes-microsoft.html

Kali365, a threat actor, has allegedly developed a tool that exploits Microsoft authentication protocols to gain unauthorized access to US companies. This vulnerability potentially allows attackers to bypass multi-factor authentication (MFA) and obtain sensitive information. Investigation and remediation are crucial to mitigate this enterprise risk.
