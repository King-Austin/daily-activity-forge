_2026-08-05 15:08 UTC_

**Devlog Entry: Critical Security Flaws in Veeam, Terraform MCP, and Django**

**Date:** August 2026
**Severity:** High

Critical vulnerabilities have been identified in Veeam, Terraform MCP, and Django, with a CVSS 10.0 cross-tenant bug leading the list. This potentially allows for unauthorized access and data breaches. It is essential to review and apply patches for these vulnerabilities as soon as possible to prevent potential security breaches.
