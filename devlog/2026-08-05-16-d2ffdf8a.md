# Security Update: Poison Claude Sells Discounted Claude Access While Its Operator Sees Every Customer Prompt

_2026-08-05 16:48 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/poison-claude-sells-discounted-claude.html)

**Devlog Entry: Poison Claude Security Threat**

**Date:** August 5, 2026

**Summary:**

A recent cybersecurity update highlights a potential threat from "Poison Claude," a malicious service that sells discounted access to genuine Claude instances while its operator captures and monitors customer input. This compromise may allow attackers to intercept sensitive information, posing a significant risk to users' data security. Further investigation is necessary to understand the full scope of the threat and potential mitigations.
