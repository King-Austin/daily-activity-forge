# Security Update: Snowflake Hacker Pleads Guilty Over Breaches Affecting at Least 100 Million People

_2026-08-06 06:30 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/snowflake-hacker-pleads-guilty-over.html)

**Devlog Entry: Snowflake Hacker Pleads Guilty**

**Date:** August 6, 2026
**Category:** Security

A hacker has pleaded guilty to multiple breaches affecting at least 100 million people, compromising sensitive data stored in Snowflake, a cloud-based data warehousing service. This incident highlights the importance of proper access controls and data encryption in cloud-based storage solutions. The breach serves as a reminder to prioritize secure data handling practices, particularly in sensitive applications.

**Technical Insight:** This incident emphasizes the need for robust security measures, including multi-factor authentication, access controls, and encryption at rest and in transit, to protect against unauthorized access to sensitive data in cloud-based environments.
