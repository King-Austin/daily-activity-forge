# Security Update: CryptoJS Weak RNG Behind $5.7 Million in Drains Affects Five Crypto Wallet Apps

_2026-08-06 12:30 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/cryptojs-weak-rng-behind-57-million-in.html)

**Devlog Entry: CryptoJS Vulnerability**

**Date:** 06 August 2026

**Summary:**

A critical vulnerability in the CryptoJS library, a widely used JavaScript cryptographic library, has been discovered. The issue, which affects five crypto wallet apps, stems from a weak random number generator (RNG) that can lead to predictable and therefore insecure key generation. This vulnerability has resulted in estimated losses of $5.7 million, highlighting the importance of secure RNG implementations in cryptographic libraries.

**Action Items:**

* Review dependencies and libraries for potential usage of CryptoJS
* Assess and update RNG implementations in our applications
* Consider migrating to alternative libraries with stronger RNG guarantees
