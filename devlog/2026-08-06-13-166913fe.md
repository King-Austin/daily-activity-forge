**Devlog Entry: AI Recommendation Poisoning**

* **Date:** 06 August 2026
* **Update:** AI Recommendation Poisoning via "Ask AI" Buttons
* **Risk:** Recent research highlights the potential for AI recommendation poisoning through "Ask AI" buttons in LLMs (Large Language Models). This vulnerability allows malicious actors to silently modify model memory, leading to biased or manipulated responses. This poses a significant threat to the integrity and trustworthiness of AI-driven systems.
