# Security Update: Over 4,400 Rockwell PLCs Exposed Online, 22 Found in Water Attack Cities

_2026-08-06 13:31 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/over-4400-rockwell-plcs-exposed-online.html)

**Security Log Entry - 06 August 2026**

* **Threat Alert:** A recent cybersecurity update revealed that over 4,400 Rockwell PLCs (Programmable Logic Controllers) are exposed online, posing a significant risk to industrial control systems (ICS) and potential exploitation in cities affected by water attacks.
* **Technical Insight:** This vulnerability highlights the critical need for robust network segmentation and secure configuration of industrial devices to prevent lateral movement and potential ransomware attacks. 
* **Recommendation:** Conduct thorough vulnerability assessments and implement security best practices for ICS environments to mitigate potential risks.
