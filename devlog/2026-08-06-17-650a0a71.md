# Security Update: New Interrupt Injection Attack Can Bypass Spectre v2 Defenses on Intel and AMD CPUs

_2026-08-06 17:11 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/new-interrupt-injection-attack-can.html)

**Devlog Entry: Interrupt Injection Attack**

**Date:** August 6, 2026

**Summary:** A newly discovered interrupt injection attack can bypass Spectre v2 defenses on Intel and AMD CPUs, posing a significant threat to system security. This attack exploits the CPU's interrupt handling mechanism to inject malicious data, allowing for potential privilege escalation and information disclosure. Further research is required to fully understand the scope and impact of this vulnerability.
