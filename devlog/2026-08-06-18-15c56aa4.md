# Security Update: Cisco Patches 12 SD-WAN and IOS XE Flaws, Including Three 9.8 CVSS Score Bugs

_2026-08-06 18:51 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/cisco-patches-12-sd-wan-and-ios-xe.html)

**Security Update: Cisco Patches High-Risk Flaws in SD-WAN and IOS XE**

On August 5, 2026, Cisco released patches for 12 vulnerabilities in its SD-WAN and IOS XE software, including three critical flaws with a CVSS score of 9.8. These high-risk bugs pose a significant threat to enterprise networks, potentially allowing attackers to execute arbitrary code, obtain sensitive information, or cause system crashes. It is essential for organizations using Cisco SD-WAN and IOS XE to apply the latest patches to mitigate these risks.
