# Security Update: New NatJack Attacks Hijack TCP Sessions and Spoof DNS by Manipulating NAT Tables

_2026-08-07 09:53 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/new-natjack-attacks-hijack-tcp-sessions.html)

**Devlog Entry: NatJack Attacks**

**Date:** August 7, 2026

**Summary:**

Recent reports of NatJack attacks highlight a significant cybersecurity threat where attackers manipulate NAT (Network Address Translation) tables to hijack TCP sessions and spoof DNS queries. This exploit leverages vulnerabilities in NAT implementations to intercept and redirect sensitive data, posing a substantial risk to network security and integrity. Organizations are advised to review and update their NAT configurations to mitigate potential risks.
