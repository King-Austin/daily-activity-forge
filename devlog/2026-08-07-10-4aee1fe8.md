**DevLog Entry - Potential Windows Hello for Business Security Vulnerability**
==============================================================

**Date:** August 7, 2026
**Source:** [https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/malware-can-abuse-windows-hello-for.html](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/malware-can-abuse-windows-hello-for.html)

*Summary:*

A recently discovered vulnerability in Windows Hello for Business allows malware to potentially abuse keys for persistent Entra ID access, posing a risk to enterprises relying on this authentication method. This exploit could enable unauthorized access to sensitive resources and data. Developers and security teams should review and update their authentication protocols to address this potential threat.
