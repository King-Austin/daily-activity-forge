**Devlog Entry - 2026-08-07**

**Microsoft 365 AitM Phishing Threat**

A recent cybersecurity update warns of a phishing threat targeting Microsoft 365 users, specifically attempting to hijack accounts to collect sensitive payroll and finance emails. This AitM (Automated Investigation and Remediation) phishing campaign exploits vulnerabilities in user behavior, underscoring the importance of robust security measures and employee education in preventing account takeovers. Developers, IT professionals, and end-users alike must remain vigilant in responding to emerging threats like this.
