**Cybersecurity Update: macOS Stealer Malware**

*Date:* August 07, 2026
*Source:* [https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/clickfix-attacks-deliver-macos-stealer.html](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/clickfix-attacks-deliver-macos-stealer.html)

A new macOS malware, known as ClickFix, has been discovered to deliver a stealer that can extract sensitive information from cryptocurrency wallets. This threat highlights the growing concern of macOS-based attacks, emphasizing the need for developers to prioritize security in their applications and software. Developers should remain vigilant and update their systems to protect against this emerging threat.
