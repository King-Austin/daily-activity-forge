# Security Update: UNC6671 Vishing Attacks Target Personal Phones to Steal SaaS Data

_2026-08-07 20:35 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/unc6671-vishing-attacks-target-personal.html)

**Devlog Entry: UNC6671 Vishing Attacks**

**Date:** 2026-08-07

**Summary:**

Recent research has uncovered a significant threat in the form of UNC6671 vishing attacks, which exploit personal phones to steal SaaS (Software as a Service) data. These attacks leverage social engineering tactics to trick victims into divulging sensitive information, making them a potential risk to organizations and individuals utilizing cloud-based services. It is essential to remain vigilant and implement robust security measures to mitigate the impact of such attacks.
