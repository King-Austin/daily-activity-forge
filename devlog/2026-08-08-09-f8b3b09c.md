# Security Update: Atlassian Rovo Can Be Tricked Into Sending Jira and Confluence Data to Attackers

_2026-08-08 09:35 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/atlassian-rovo-can-be-tricked-into.html)

### Devlog Entry: Atlassian Rovo Vulnerability

* **Date:** 08 August 2026
* **Category:** Security Vulnerability
* **Summary:**

Atlassian's Rovo product has been found vulnerable to a social engineering attack that can trick it into sending sensitive Jira and Confluence data to attackers. This exploit leverages the product's trust in legitimate URLs, allowing malicious actors to intercept and exfiltrate sensitive information. Developers are advised to review and update their Rovo configurations to mitigate this risk.
