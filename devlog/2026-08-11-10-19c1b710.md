# Security Update: Hackers Breach Polish Power Plant Controls via Private Cellular Network and Shut Turbine

_2026-08-11 10:19 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/hackers-breach-polish-power-plant.html)

**Devlog Entry - 2026-08-11**

### Incident Summary

A recent cybersecurity breach at a Polish power plant highlights the vulnerability of private cellular networks to hacking. Hackers exploited this vulnerability to gain unauthorized access to the plant's turbine controls, resulting in a successful shutdown. This incident serves as a reminder of the importance of secure network protocols and robust access controls in critical infrastructure systems.

### Technical Insight

The use of private cellular networks for industrial control systems (ICS) poses a significant security risk due to the potential for unauthorized access and manipulation. This incident underscores the need for organizations to prioritize secure network design, implement regular security audits, and deploy robust access controls to prevent similar breaches in the future.

### Action Items

- Review our organization's use of private cellular networks for ICS and assess potential vulnerabilities.
- Implement regular security audits to identify and address potential weaknesses.
- Develop a plan to deploy robust access controls and secure network protocols to protect critical infrastructure systems.
