**Devlog Entry: North Korean IT Workers' Involvement in Fake Crypto Startup**

**Date:** August 11, 2026
**Summary:**

Researchers have successfully created a fake crypto startup, hiring three suspected North Korean IT workers, to demonstrate the potential ease of recruitment by threat actors. This experiment highlights the risks of unknowingly partnering with malicious individuals, potentially compromising sensitive information and assets. The incident underscores the importance of thorough background checks and due diligence in the crypto industry.

**Technical Insight:** This incident showcases the evolving tactics of state-sponsored threat actors, who may employ fake entities and compromised individuals to infiltrate targeted organizations.
