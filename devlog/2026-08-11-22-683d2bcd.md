# Security Update: Kimwolf v7 Android Botnet Makes HTTP/2 DDoS Traffic Look Like Legitimate Browsing

_2026-08-11 22:41 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/kimwolf-v7-android-botnet-makes-http2.html)

**Devlog Entry: Kimwolf v7 Android Botnet**

### Date: 2026-08-11

Summary:
A newly discovered variant of the Kimwolf Android botnet (v7) has been found to manipulate HTTP/2 traffic, making it difficult to distinguish between legitimate browsing activity and DDoS attacks. This sophisticated technique enables the botnet to evade detection by security systems relying on traditional traffic analysis. As a result, organizations should be prepared to adapt their security measures to mitigate the risks associated with this advanced threat.

### Key Takeaways:

* Kimwolf v7 botnet's advanced HTTP/2 manipulation capabilities
* Increased difficulty in detecting DDoS attacks
* Organizations should update their security protocols to address this emerging threat.
