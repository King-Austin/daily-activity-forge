**DevLog Entry: DeadLock Ransomware Exploits Polygon Smart Contracts**

**Date:** August 12, 2026

**Summary:**

Researchers have discovered that the DeadLock ransomware is leveraging Polygon smart contracts to create resilient extortion infrastructure, making it harder to disrupt. This development highlights the growing threat of decentralized ransomware attacks, which can evade traditional takedown methods. As a result, it's essential to prioritize blockchain security and monitor emerging threats in this space.
