_2026-08-12 10:42 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/malicious-litellm-releases-tied-to.html)

**Devlog Entry: Potential Trivy Hack Threat**
=============================================

### Date: 12 August 2026

A recent cybersecurity update warns of malicious LiteLLM releases potentially tied to a Trivy hack, which may have exposed over 2,100 organizations to security vulnerabilities. This incident highlights the importance of maintaining secure dependencies and monitoring third-party package managers for potential threats. Developers are advised to review their dependencies and update Trivy to the latest version to mitigate this risk.
