## VMware vCenter Vulnerability Exploited

A recent cybersecurity update warns of attackers exploiting a vulnerability in VMware vCenter to gain persistent remote access. This exploit allows for unauthorized access to sensitive data and systems, highlighting the importance of timely patching and monitoring of VMware infrastructure. It is crucial to apply the latest security updates and consider implementing additional security measures to mitigate potential risks.
