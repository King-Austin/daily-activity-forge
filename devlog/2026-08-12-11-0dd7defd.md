# Security Update: ShieldBreak Zero-Day PoC Claims Microsoft Defender Patch Bypass With SYSTEM Access

_2026-08-12 11:22 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/shieldbreak-zero-day-poc-claims.html)

**Devlog Entry: ShieldBreak Zero-Day Vulnerability**

**Date:** August 12, 2026

**Summary:**
A recent report from The Hacker News highlights a potential zero-day vulnerability, ShieldBreak, which allegedly bypasses Microsoft Defender's patch with SYSTEM access. This exploit, demonstrated by a proof-of-concept (PoC) claim, may pose a significant threat to Windows systems if verified and exploited. Further research is required to assess the severity and potential impact of this vulnerability.
