### Security Advisory: Cisco ASA and FTD Vulnerability

A recent cybersecurity update indicates that a flaw in Cisco ASA and FTD devices has been exploited in the wild, potentially triggering a remote denial-of-service (DoS) attack. The vulnerability, while not yet fully disclosed, is reportedly being actively exploited by attackers, and users are advised to take immediate action to mitigate the risk. Further information on the vulnerability and recommended remediation steps can be found in the linked article: https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/cisco-asa-and-ftd-flaw-exploited-in.html.
