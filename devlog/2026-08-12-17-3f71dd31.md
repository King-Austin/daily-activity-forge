# Security Update: Adobe Patches Three CVSS 10.0 ColdFusion and Campaign Classic Flaws

_2026-08-12 17:23 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/adobe-patches-three-cvss-100-coldfusion.html)

**Devlog Entry: Adobe Patches Critical ColdFusion Flaws**

**Date:** August 12, 2026

Adobe has released patches for three critical vulnerabilities (CVSS 10.0) in ColdFusion and Adobe Campaign Classic, highlighting the importance of timely software updates to prevent potential exploitation by attackers. These flaws, if exploited, could lead to remote code execution and unauthorized access to sensitive data. It is essential for developers and administrators to apply the latest security patches to mitigate these risks.
