# Security Update: CVE-2026-23104: OpenSSL ASN.1 Integer Decoding Side-Channel

_2026-10-21 06:30 UTC_

[Source](https://www.openssl.org/news/secadv/)

A timing side-channel in OpenSSL's ASN.1 parsing code allowed recovery of private RSA keys during signature verification under specific cache-timing analysis. Fix introduces constant-time integer normalization.
