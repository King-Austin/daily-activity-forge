# Security Update: Com-Affiliated Hackers Hijack Cloud Accounts via Vishing for Data Theft and Extortion

_2026-11-25 22:34 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/com-affiliated-hackers-hijack-cloud.html)

Noted the emergence of China-affiliated actors leveraging voice phishing (vishing) to intercept MFA codes and hijack cloud-administrative accounts, highlighting the critical vulnerability of human factors in multi-layered cloud security. This vector suggests a shift toward high-impact data theft and extortion campaigns that bypass traditional perimeter defenses by compromising identity credentials directly. I should review our internal MFA enforcement policies and consider implementing hardware-based keys or just-in-time access controls to mitigate this social engineering risk.
