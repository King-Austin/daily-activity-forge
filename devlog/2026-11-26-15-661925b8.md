# Security Update: 13 Malicious NuGet Packages Turn Fake .NET Job Assessments Into Malware Delivery Traps

_2026-11-26 15:45 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/13-malicious-nuget-packages-turn-fake.html)

Noted the emergence of 13 malicious NuGet packages leveraging fake .NET job assessments to distribute malware, highlighting a significant supply chain risk for developers engaging with unsolicited technical evaluations. This vector underscores the need for rigorous vetting of third-party dependencies and code artifacts during recruitment processes to prevent potential compromise of the development environment.
