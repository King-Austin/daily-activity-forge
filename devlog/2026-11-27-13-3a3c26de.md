# Security Update: Microsoft Store App Found Delivering Vinci RAT Capable of Remote System Control

_2026-11-27 13:30 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/microsoft-store-app-found-delivering.html)

Noted a report of a malicious Microsoft Store application distributing the Vinci RAT, which grants attackers full remote system control. This incident highlights the critical risk of supply-chain attacks via trusted app stores and reinforces the need for rigorous verification of app signatures and permissions. I will monitor for further details on the specific IOCs and mitigation strategies to update our internal security guidelines.
