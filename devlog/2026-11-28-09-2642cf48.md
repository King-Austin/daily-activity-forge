# Security Update: pCloud Password Manager Hit by Cyber Attack Involving Email Address Exposure and Login Attempts

_2026-11-28 09:14 UTC_

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/pcloud-password-manager-hit-by-cyber.html)

Noted a recent breach affecting pCloud’s password manager, involving the exposure of user email addresses and subsequent targeted login attempts. This incident underscores the critical risk of credential stuffing and the necessity for enforcing multi-factor authentication (MFA) and rate-limiting on authentication endpoints. I will monitor for any vendor patches or additional data leakage details to assess if client-side encryption mitigations were compromised.
