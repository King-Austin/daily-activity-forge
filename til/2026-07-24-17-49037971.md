# TIL: Micro-SaaS may be on the decline, but what's next?

TIL that the concept of Micro-SaaS is being replaced by "Service with a Software", a model where the focus shifts from a standalone, niche software product to a more holistic, service-oriented approach that bundles software with human expertise. This shift implies that developers should now think about building software that creates a recurring revenue stream through subscription-based services, potentially incorporating AI-powered automation, and integrating with existing workflows. As a developer, I'll be keeping an eye on this trend and exploring ways to adapt our software development process to meet the evolving needs of our customers and the market.
