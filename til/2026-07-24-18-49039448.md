**TIL: YC Application Vulnerability Exploitation**
=====================================================

I came across an article detailing a successful exploitation of a vulnerability in the Y Combinator (YC) application process by a determined applicant. The reported issue appears to be related to a SQL injection vulnerability in the application's API, which was used to manipulate the application status and ultimately gain acceptance into the YC program. This incident highlights the importance of robust security audits and secure coding practices in web applications, especially those handling sensitive data and critical business processes. It also underscores the need for developers to consider attack surface optimization and input validation in their applications to prevent similar exploits.
