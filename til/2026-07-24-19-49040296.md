# Today I Learned
TIL that PostgreSQL's LISTEN/NOTIFY mechanism can scale more effectively than previously thought. This is a game-changer for distributed systems that rely on asynchronous, event-driven communication between nodes. The author's experiments demonstrate that with proper configuration and tuning, LISTEN/NOTIFY can handle high-volume notification scenarios, potentially eliminating the need for custom messaging systems or queuing solutions. I'll need to revisit our project's architecture and consider replacing our message broker with PostgreSQL's built-in LISTEN/NOTIFY functionality to simplify our setup and reduce infrastructure overhead.
