### TIL: Sperm Whales' Bubble-Based Sleep Technique

I stumbled upon an interesting article about sperm whales' unique ability to sleep while remaining upright in the water. By blowing bubbles and adjusting their buoyancy, they can effectively achieve a restful, vertical sleep. While this may seem unrelated to software development, I see potential parallels with concurrent programming and distributed system design. Specifically, this technique highlights the importance of adapting to changing environmental conditions (in this case, water pressure and buoyancy) to maintain stability. This can be seen as analogous to how a system might need to dynamically adjust its resource allocation or scheduling to ensure optimal performance under varying loads, making me aware of the need to consider such adaptability when designing concurrent or distributed systems.
