# TIL: Re: Bye Bye Gravatar

_2026-07-25 04:11 UTC_

[Original Source](https://unattributed.cc/re-bye-bye-gravatar)

TIL that Gravatar, a widely-used global avatar service, is shutting down its underlying email-based ID system, potentially affecting numerous applications and services that rely on it for user identity verification. This change highlights the importance of diversifying authentication methods and considering the long-term viability of third-party services in application design, as well as the need for developers to be aware of and plan for potential service outages and deprecations.
