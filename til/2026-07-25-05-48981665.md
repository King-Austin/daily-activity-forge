### TIL: Wasmtime GC and Exceptions Implications

TIL that Wasmtime, a WebAssembly runtime, has a garbage collector (GC) that's designed to work with exceptions, which could significantly impact how I approach WebAssembly development. This means that Wasmtime's GC is not just about memory management, but also about ensuring that exceptions are properly propagated and handled, which could lead to more robust and fault-tolerant WASM applications. This knowledge is particularly important for developers who need to work with languages that compile to WebAssembly, like Rust and C, where manual memory management and exception handling are common practices.
