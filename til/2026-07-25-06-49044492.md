**TIL**: UK's AISI/CaCIS Preliminary Assessment of Kimi K3's Cyber Capabilities
=====================================================

I came across an article from NIST discussing the preliminary assessment of Kimi K3's cyber capabilities by the UK's AISI/CaCIS (Advanced Information Service Intelligence/Cyber Assessment and Investigation Service). What caught my attention is the reported use of 'zero-day exploits' in Kimi K3's toolkit, which could imply that the toolchain may employ unknown vulnerabilities in software and hardware components. As a developer, this raises concerns about the potential for unaddressed security risks in our own systems and the importance of incorporating robust vulnerability scanning and regular software updates into our development workflows.
