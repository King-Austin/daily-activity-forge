**TIL: Android's potential ADB restriction**
==============================================

TIL that Google may soon restrict On-Device ADB access on Android devices, requiring users to opt-in through the Settings app. This change will impact developers who rely on ADB for testing, debugging, and automated builds. The technical implication is that developers will need to update their build pipelines and testing scripts to account for the new permission requirement, potentially introducing additional complexity and overhead. This shift may also prompt a reevaluation of alternative debugging tools, such as the Android Debug Bridge over USB or wireless debugging using tools like Vysor.
