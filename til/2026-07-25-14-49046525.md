### TIL: Potential Implications of Fedora 45 Sausage Factory

TIL that the Fedora 45 sausage factory, a term coined by Supa Keen, refers to the practice of building and rebuilding the same Linux distribution repeatedly. This phenomenon has significant implications for software developers. It highlights the need for efficient and well-engineered package management systems that can handle rapid iteration and rebuild cycles without introducing unnecessary complexity or dependencies. As a developer, it's essential to consider the ' sausage factory' problem when designing build and deployment pipelines, ensuring that they can scale and adapt to the demands of rapid development and testing.
