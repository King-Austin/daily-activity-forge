### TIL: The Reality of AI and Job Market Impact

TIL that despite the hype surrounding AI, a recent policy brief from Stanford's SIEPR suggests that while AI will indeed displace some jobs, it will also create new ones, particularly in fields such as AI development, deployment, and maintenance. This has implications for software developers, as we should consider upskilling and reskilling to adapt to emerging AI-powered technologies and potentially transition into roles focused on AI development and integration, rather than simply relying on traditional programming skills.
