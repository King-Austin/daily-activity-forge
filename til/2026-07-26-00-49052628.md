### TIL: Reliability in Complex Systems is a Long-Term Problem

TIL that despite significant advancements in fields like medicine and technology, clinical failure rates for medical treatments and products have remained relatively high over the past several decades (around 10-40%). This got me thinking about the parallels between medical device reliability and software development. In software, we often focus on short-term metrics like code coverage and test suites, but this article serves as a reminder that complex systems can still fail spectacularly even with extensive testing and validation. This highlights the importance of long-term thinking and continued monitoring of our systems, as well as the need for more robust and resilient design principles to mitigate the risk of failures.
