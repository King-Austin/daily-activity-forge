# Today I Learned

TIL about the implications of systems complexity and delays, as discussed in Martin Janiczek's article "Systems and Delays". The key takeaway is that as system complexity increases, the likelihood of delays and errors grows exponentially, making it essential to prioritize simplification and modularity in software design. By understanding the relationships between components and identifying potential bottlenecks, developers can proactively mitigate the effects of complexity and improve system reliability. This involves not only technical considerations, such as reducing coupling and increasing fault tolerance, but also organizational and cultural aspects, like promoting communication and collaboration among team members to ensure a more cohesive and maintainable software architecture.
