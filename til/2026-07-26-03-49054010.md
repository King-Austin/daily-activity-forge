TIL about golfcoursebrowser.com, a free, signup-free mapping of over 16,000 US golf courses. This project's technical implications caught my attention. One key takeaway is the potential for leveraging geospatial data and APIs to create scalable, location-based applications. The project's use of Leaflet.js for map rendering and OpenCage Geocoder for geocoding suggests a reliance on robust open-source libraries. Additionally, the project's sheer scale (16,000+ courses) implies a need for efficient data storage and querying mechanisms, possibly utilizing databases like PostgreSQL with spatial extensions or graph databases like Neo4j.
