# TIL: GrapheneOS protections against data extraction from locked devices

_2026-07-26 08:59 UTC_

[Original Source](https://discuss.grapheneos.org/d/40700-grapheneos-protections-against-data-extraction-from-locked-devices)

**Today I Learned: GrapheneOS Lockscreen Exploits**

TIL that GrapheneOS, a hardened Android distribution, has implemented several protections against data extraction from locked devices, including kernel lockdown, secure boot verification, and secure storage for sensitive data. This highlights the importance of kernel-level lockdown in preventing exploitation of lockscreen vulnerabilities, such as those demonstrated by the "Checkm8" vulnerability. As a developer, this emphasizes the need to consider the kernel's role in security and the importance of verifying secure boot mechanisms to prevent malicious modifications. Additionally, this showcases the value of secure storage solutions for protecting sensitive data from compromise, even in the event of a successful lockscreen bypass.
