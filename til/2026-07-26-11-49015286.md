# TIL: German Peasants' War

_2026-07-26 11:44 UTC_

[Original Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Peasants%27_War)

### Today I Learned

I came across an interesting article on Hacker News about the German Peasants' War, a 16th-century uprising of peasants against the nobility. While it may seem unrelated to software development at first glance, I noticed a few parallels with modern distributed systems. In 1525, the peasants' decentralized, peer-to-peer network of messengers and communication channels facilitated the rapid dissemination of information across the region. This echoes the principles of modern distributed systems, where nodes communicate with each other to achieve a common goal. Notably, the peasants' use of a decentralized network also highlighted the importance of robust communication protocols, data redundancy, and error handling - all crucial considerations for developing resilient distributed systems.
