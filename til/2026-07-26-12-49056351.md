**TIL**: Systemd Linger
=====================

I recently came across a disturbing trend on Hacker News where various users have reported that their systems are being unexpectedly awakened from suspend/hibernate mode due to a seemingly innocuous `systemd-logind` service. The issue, dubbed "Systemd Linger", arises when `systemd-logind` leaves a zombie process behind after a user logs out, which can then cause the system to wake up from suspend/hibernate mode due to a periodic timer. This behavior highlights the importance of monitoring system services and their potential interactions, as well as the need to carefully configure and manage systemd units to avoid such unexpected side effects. I'll be keeping a closer eye on this issue and exploring ways to mitigate its impact on my own systems.
