# TIL: Kill The Cookie Banner

_2026-07-26 16:15 UTC_

[Original Source](https://killthecookiebanner.eu/)

**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL about the "Kill The Cookie Banner" initiative, a European Union (EU)-backed project aiming to simplify online consent mechanisms for data collection. This has significant implications for software developers, as it may lead to a shift away from traditional cookie banners and towards more transparent and standardized approaches to data consent. I'm taking note to review and potentially refactor my use of cookie-related APIs, focusing on implementing GDPR-compliant solutions that minimize user friction while maintaining transparency.
