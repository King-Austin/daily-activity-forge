# TIL: Design Is Compromise

_2026-07-26 16:30 UTC_

[Original Source](https://stephango.com/design-is-compromise)

**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL that designing software is inherently a compromise between competing priorities, such as usability, performance, security, and feature set. This perspective, as expressed in the article "Design Is Compromise" by Stephen Go, highlights the importance of understanding the trade-offs involved in making design decisions. As a developer, I recognize that this principle applies to all aspects of software development, from the technical stack chosen to the algorithms implemented. By acknowledging the compromise inherent in design, I can focus on finding optimal solutions that balance competing requirements, rather than striving for a hypothetical ideal that may not be achievable in practice.
