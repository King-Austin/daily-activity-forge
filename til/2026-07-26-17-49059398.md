### Today I Learned

TIL about CheapSecurity, an open-source, self-hosted CCTV solution for Linux Single Board Computers (SBCs). This project demonstrates the potential of repurposing low-cost, low-power hardware (e.g., Raspberry Pi) for IoT applications. From a technical perspective, it highlights the importance of optimizing software for low-resource devices, including minimalistic designs, efficient resource utilization, and potentially custom kernels. The use of Linux SBCs for CCTV applications also underscores the growing trend of leveraging commodity hardware for specific, use-case-driven tasks, thereby reducing costs and promoting DIY innovations.
