### TIL: Lidl Introduces Wero Payment Method in Germany

TIL that Lidl is introducing the European payment method Wero in Germany, marking a potential shift in payment ecosystem adoption. This could imply a rise in support for 3DS 2.0 (3D Secure 2.0) protocol, a two-factor authentication standard, which Wero seems to be built upon. As a developer, this development highlights the importance of staying up-to-date with emerging standards and protocols in the payment industry, particularly in regions with a high adoption rate of new technologies like Europe.
