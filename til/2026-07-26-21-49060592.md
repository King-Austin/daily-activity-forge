# TIL: It's not empowering to hand off the details

_2026-07-26 21:15 UTC_

[Original Source](https://davidnicholaswilliams.com/its-not-empowering-to-hand-off-the-details/)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

I came across an insightful article on Hacker News, "It's not empowering to hand off the details" by David Nicholas Williams. The piece highlights the importance of engineers taking ownership of the technical decisions and implementation details in their work, rather than relying on others, such as managers or external experts, to provide these details. This resonates with me in the context of large-scale software development, where over-reliance on others can lead to knowledge silos and hinder team autonomy. I've come to realize that, as a developer, I should strive to be more self-sufficient in understanding and implementing technical solutions, rather than relying on others to dictate the "what" and "how". This mindset shift will help me make more informed decisions and contribute more effectively to team projects.
