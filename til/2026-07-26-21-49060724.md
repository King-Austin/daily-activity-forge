# Today I Learned

TIL about Data-Oriented Design (DOD) and its potential to revolutionize how we approach software architecture. After reading the 'Introduction to Data-Oriented Design' article, I'm intrigued by the idea of decoupling data and operations to improve cache locality, reduce memory traffic, and enhance overall system performance. By arranging data in a contiguous, cache-friendly manner and using data structures that allow for efficient access and manipulation, developers can create more scalable and efficient systems. I'll need to explore this concept further and consider its implications for our team's future projects, particularly in areas where memory constraints are a major concern.
