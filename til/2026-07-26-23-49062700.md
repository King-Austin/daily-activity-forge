### TIL
TIL about the benefits of teaching children the Forth programming language, as discussed in the article 'Teaching Kids Forth' by Chris Pressey. This caught my attention because it highlights a key aspect of programming that's often overlooked: the importance of simplicity and minimalism. Forth's concise syntax and unique stack-based paradigm can make it an ideal language for beginners, allowing them to focus on fundamental programming concepts without being overwhelmed by verbose syntax. As a developer, I appreciate the insight into how a deliberately simple language can accelerate learning and foster a stronger understanding of programming principles.
