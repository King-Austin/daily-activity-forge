### Today I Learned
#### [PGSimCity](https://nikolays.github.io/PGSimCity/) - Understanding PostgreSQL Internals

TIL that [PGSimCity](https://nikolays.github.io/PGSimCity/), an interactive simulation of PostgreSQL, can be a powerful tool for developers. This visualization helps illustrate the complexities of PostgreSQL's architecture, including query planning, execution, and storage management. By exploring how the system handles concurrent connections, locking, and caching, I gained a deeper appreciation for the intricacies of a high-performance database. Specifically, I now better understand the importance of indexing, query optimizations, and the impact of transaction isolation levels on database performance, which will inform my approach to database design and optimization in future projects.
