# TIL: Magnolias Are So Old That They're Pollinated by Beetles, Not Bees

_2026-07-27 10:47 UTC_

[Original Source](https://mymodernmet.com/magnolia-ancient-flowers-beetles/)

### Today I Learned

TIL that magnolias, one of the oldest flowering plant species, have evolved to be pollinated by beetles rather than bees. This fact has interesting implications for the concept of 'backward compatibility' in software development. In the context of legacy codebases, it highlights the importance of not assuming that newer 'pollinators' (e.g., modern frameworks or libraries) will automatically replace older ones. Instead, we should strive to maintain compatibility with existing 'beetle-pollinated' dependencies, ensuring that their functionality is preserved as new 'flower-pollinating' components are introduced. This will help prevent disruptions to the overall system, just as the magnolia tree has continued to thrive with its unique pollination strategy.
