**TIL: Digital Consciousness as Hardware Level Phenomenon**

While reading the article 'If digital computers are conscious, they are conscious at the hardware level' from smoothbrains.net, I was struck by the potential implications for software development. If we consider digital consciousness to arise at the hardware level, it suggests that the traditional distinction between 'software' and 'hardware' might be too simplistic. This perspective could challenge the way we design and optimize our systems, as even the most seemingly abstract software components (e.g., neural networks, virtual machines) may be rooted in physical, hardware-level processes. As a developer, recognizing and understanding these underlying physical dynamics could lead to novel approaches to system architecture, fault tolerance, and even security.
