### TIL: Htmx as a React Replacement

I came across an interesting article discussing the removal of React.js from a codebase and its replacement with Htmx for UI interactivity. A key takeaway from this is that Htmx's approach to dynamic UI updates, leveraging server-side rendered HTML fragments, can provide a more straightforward alternative to React's complex component lifecycle. This shift may be particularly appealing to developers seeking a more lightweight and maintainable solution, where the separation of concerns between client and server remains intact.
