### TIL: AI companies and the risk of data-driven book destruction

TIL that AI companies, in their pursuit of training high-quality datasets, may be using techniques like deconstruction and shredding of rare books to extract text data. This raises concerns about the potential misuse of AI for destructive purposes and highlights the importance of considering the source and provenance of training data in AI development. As a developer, this serves as a reminder to design systems that prioritize data acquisition methods that are transparent, accountable, and environmentally sustainable.
