#### TIL: Efficient Ray Tracing of Animated Geometry via Tetrahedral Cages

TIL that researchers have developed a technique to accelerate ray tracing of massive amounts of animated geometry by utilizing tetrahedral cages. This method leverages the fact that complex models can be approximated by a union of simpler tetrahedra, allowing for efficient culling and ray intersection calculations. The potential implications of this research for software developers are significant: it could enable real-time rendering of complex, dynamic scenes in fields like computer-aided design (CAD), video games, and simulation. By applying this technique, developers may be able to push the boundaries of what is possible in terms of scene complexity and interactivity, ultimately creating more immersive and engaging experiences for users.
