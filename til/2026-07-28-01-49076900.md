**Today I Learned**
===============

TIL that astronauts returning from 6-month missions often describe a persistent "observer" sensation, feeling as if they're watching their own lives from outside the frame. This phenomenon, while intriguing, has potential implications for our understanding of cognitive biases in long-duration spaceflight. For software developers, it highlights the importance of considering the psychological effects of prolonged exposure to data visualization and feedback loops. In our own work, we may inadvertently create systems that induce a similar "observer" effect, where users become detached from their own experiences. By acknowledging this risk, we can design more empathetic and engaging interfaces that avoid exacerbating this phenomenon.
