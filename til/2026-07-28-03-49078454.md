# TIL: A $500 RL fine-tune of a 9B open model beat frontier models on catalog review

_2026-07-28 03:04 UTC_

[Original Source](https://fermisense.com/when-machines-take-the-wheel/)

**Today I Learned**
================

TIL that a $500 fine-tuning of a 9B-parameter open model can outperform state-of-the-art models on catalog review tasks. This has significant implications for the cost-effectiveness of leveraging pre-trained language models in production. It highlights the potential for significant cost savings by using open models and fine-tuning them on specific tasks, rather than developing and training new models from scratch. This approach not only reduces costs but also accelerates deployment times, making it a promising strategy for companies looking to adopt AI-driven solutions.
