# TIL: Residential Proxies Are a National Security Threat

_2026-07-28 04:49 UTC_

[Original Source](https://jacob.gold/posts/residential-proxies-are-a-national-security-threat/)

### Today I Learned

TIL that residential proxies are being widely exploited for malicious purposes, posing a significant national security threat. As a developer, this highlights the importance of scrutinizing third-party IP address services and proxy providers. I'll be adding residential proxy services to our security audit checklist, considering factors such as IP geolocation accuracy, rotation speed, and the provider's stance on abuse and compliance. Furthermore, I'll be looking into implementing IP verification and abuse detection mechanisms in our applications to mitigate potential risks associated with proxy-based attacks.
