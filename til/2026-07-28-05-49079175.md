# TIL: Why do we think we understand the world more than we actually do?

_2026-07-28 05:19 UTC_

[Original Source](https://thedecisionlab.com/biases/the-illusion-of-explanatory-depth)

**Today I Learned**

TIL about the **Illusion of Explanatory Depth**, a concept where people tend to overestimate their understanding of complex systems. This bias has implications for software development, where we often struggle to fully comprehend the intricacies of our own codebases. As a developer, I'll be more mindful of the limitations of my own understanding and the potential for unseen interactions between components. This awareness can help me design more modular, testable systems and avoid over-engineering solutions. I'll also prioritize documenting assumptions and edge cases to encourage collaboration and critique from colleagues, ultimately fostering a more accurate understanding of our software's complexities.
