# Today I Learned (TIL)
### 2024-02-20

TIL about the "Ars Astronomica" project, a collection of English translations of rare Hebrew and Latin astronomy texts. As a developer, I found an interesting connection to the history of programming and mathematics in ancient cultures. The project highlights the contributions of medieval scholars to the fields of astronomy and mathematics, which have since been forgotten or overlooked. Notably, the Arabic numeral system, which revolutionized mathematics and paved the way for modern computing, was introduced to Europe through translations of Arabic and Latin texts during this period. This historical context adds nuance to the development of modern programming languages and mathematical concepts, emphasizing the importance of interdisciplinary knowledge and cultural exchange in shaping the technological advancements we enjoy today.
