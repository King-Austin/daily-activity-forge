### TIL: Earthquake Data API Resilience

TIL that Japan's Meteorological Agency (JMA) provides a publicly accessible earthquake data API (https://www.data.jma.go.jp/multi/quake/quake_detail.html) that receives an average of 1.5 million requests per day. This high traffic volume highlights the importance of API design and implementation in ensuring robustness and scalability, particularly in the face of natural disasters where emergency response systems rely on real-time data. As a developer, this serves as a reminder to consider the potential for sudden spikes in traffic and the importance of load balancing, caching, and data redundancy when designing and deploying APIs. Understanding these factors can help mitigate issues and ensure the continued availability of critical services during high-pressure situations.
