_2026-07-28 10:19 UTC_

### TIL: macOS Tahoe 26.6 Security Content Implications

TIL that Apple's macOS Tahoe 26.6 update addresses a total of 39 security vulnerabilities, including 2 that have been actively exploited in the wild. Notable is `CVE-2023-41061`, a remote code execution vulnerability in the `libxslt` library, which has a CVSS score of 8.8. This highlights the importance of keeping third-party libraries up-to-date, even in closed-source ecosystems like macOS. It also underscores the need for developers to stay vigilant in their use of open-source dependencies, as these can often be the weakest link in a system's security posture.
