# TIL: What Even Are Microservices?

_2026-07-28 10:04 UTC_

[Original Source](https://var0.xyz/posts/what-even-are-microservices.html)

TIL that the concept of microservices, often touted as a silver bullet for scalability and resilience, is more nuanced than commonly assumed. According to the article, microservices can actually introduce significant complexity and overhead, particularly when it comes to communication between services, debugging, and testing. This highlights the importance of carefully evaluating the trade-offs of implementing microservices, considering factors such as service size, communication protocols, and infrastructure requirements. A more informed approach may be to adopt a hybrid model that balances the benefits of microservices with the simplicity and maintainability of monolithic architectures.
