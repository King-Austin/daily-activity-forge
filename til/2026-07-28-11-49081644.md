**TIL: Beyond Zero Security Framework**
======================================

Today I learned about Google's Beyond Zero security framework, designed to address the unique security challenges posed by AI and machine learning (ML) in enterprise environments. The framework's key takeaway for developers is the importance of _data minimization_ - ensuring that sensitive data is only accessible to the AI/ML models that truly require it, thereby reducing the attack surface. Additionally, the concept of _data tokenization_ - representing sensitive data as non-sensitive tokens - is also highlighted as a crucial aspect of AI-era security. This resonates with my own experiences working with sensitive data in AI-powered applications, and I'm taking note to implement similar security mechanisms in future projects.
