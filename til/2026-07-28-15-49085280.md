# Today I Learned: Harmony and Audio Signal Processing

TIL that music theory, specifically the concept of harmony, has a mathematical underpinning. The paper "Harmony Explained: Progress Towards a Scientific Theory of Music" by Dmitri Tymoczko (2012) proposes a framework for understanding musical harmony using geometric concepts. This work has implications for audio signal processing, particularly in music information retrieval (MIR) and audio analysis. The idea of representing musical harmony as a geometric space could be leveraged to develop more effective algorithms for music classification, similarity search, and feature extraction in audio signals.
