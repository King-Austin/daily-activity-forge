### Today I Learned

TIL about "KimiDelta Attention", a novel attention mechanism that has garnered attention in the deep learning community. According to the article, KimiDelta Attention is a reimplementation of the existing "Delta Attention" mechanism, which is a more efficient alternative to traditional self-attention in transformer architectures. What caught my attention is that KimiDelta Attention achieves similar performance to Delta Attention while using a fraction of the memory and computations. This could have significant implications for developers working on large-scale NLP models, as it opens up possibilities for deploying more complex models on memory-constrained hardware, such as edge devices. I'll have to explore this further and consider whether it could be a good fit for our upcoming project.
