# Today I Learned

TIL that having a personal website is becoming increasingly important for Substack writers, as it provides a dedicated space for showcasing their portfolio, expertise, and brand identity. This article highlights the technical takeaway that a website can serve as a hub for linking to one's Substack newsletters, allowing readers to easily access their content and fostering a more direct connection with their audience. Furthermore, it implies that developers should consider building a simple website or blog to complement their newsletter content, using tools like Jekyll, Hugo, or even Next.js for rapid prototyping and deployment.
