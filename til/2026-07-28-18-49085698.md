### Today I Learned
#### Kimi K3 Architecture
TIL about the Kimi K3 architecture, a novel approach to building scalable and fault-tolerant systems. From reading Sebastian Raschka's article, I'm intrigued by the use of a "control plane" to manage clusters, which allows for decentralized decision-making and improved system resilience. The concept of "service clusters" also caught my attention, where each cluster is responsible for a specific function, making it easier to isolate and update individual components without affecting the entire system. This modular design could potentially make it easier to develop and deploy complex distributed systems, and I'm interested in exploring this architecture further to learn more about its practical applications and potential pitfalls.
