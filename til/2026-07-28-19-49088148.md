### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL about Deflock Casa Grande (https://deflockcg.com/), a community-driven project that has taken down a notorious Casa Grande surveillance system. This raises several technical takeaways: it highlights the importance of code review and auditing in identifying vulnerabilities, and demonstrates the effectiveness of open-source collaboration in uncovering and exploiting those weaknesses. Specifically, the use of fuzz testing and binary analysis in identifying the system's flaws showcases the value of creative and meticulous bug hunting. As a developer, this serves as a reminder to prioritize code quality, security, and openness in our own projects.
