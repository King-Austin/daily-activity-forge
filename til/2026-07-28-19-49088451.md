# TIL - GrapheneOS Donation Campaign

Today I learned about the donation campaign for GrapheneOS, a custom Android distribution prioritizing user privacy and security. This news highlights the growing demand for secure mobile devices and the importance of open-source development. Notably, the GrapheneOS project is built on top of AOSP (Android Open Source Project), demonstrating the potential of community-driven contributions to secure and customize Android. This has implications for software developers who work with Android, emphasizing the need for secure coding practices and the importance of contributing back to the community to ensure the continued development and maintenance of secure open-source projects.
