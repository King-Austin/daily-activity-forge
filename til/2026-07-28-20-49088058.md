### TIL: MCP (Model Context Protocol) Specification Update

TIL that the latest MCP specification (2026-07-28) introduces a stateless transport model, potentially shifting the paradigm for remote procedure calls (RPCs). This change could lead to reduced server load, improved scalability, and simplified deployment architectures. The stateless transport also implies a shift in session management and authentication strategies, as clients will need to handle session state independently. As a developer, this update warrants closer inspection of existing RPC implementations and consideration of how to adapt to this new specification, potentially involving the use of caching, token-based authentication, or alternative session management techniques.
