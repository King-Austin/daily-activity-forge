# TIL: Apple's iPhone Upgrade Program has been replaced by Apple Upgrade

I noticed that Apple has replaced their iPhone Upgrade Program with Apple Upgrade, which implies a shift from a leasing model to a more traditional upgrade program. From a technical standpoint, this change may lead to increased demand for device-specific software and hardware support, potentially affecting the development of software compatible with various iPhone models. Furthermore, this transition may have implications for the way developers handle device compatibility, upgrade paths, and user experience, particularly in the context of enterprise and business applications.
