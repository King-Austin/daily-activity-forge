# Today I Learned
TIL that it's possible to run Kimi K3, a lightweight Linux distribution, on an M1 Mac using the deltafin project. This got me thinking about the potential implications for cross-platform development and the future of ARM-based macOS. The fact that deltafin can run a Linux distro on an M1 Mac suggests that it may be possible to leverage the power of ARM-based Apple silicon for Linux development and deployment. This could have significant implications for developers who rely on Linux for their workflows, and may even enable a new wave of ARM-based development tools and services.
