### TIL: 'Teach yourself programming in ten years (1998)'
 
I just read Peter Norvig's seminal article 'Teach yourself programming in ten years (1998)', which outlines a practical approach to learning programming. A key takeaway I got from this article is the importance of consistent, long-term effort in mastering a programming skillset. Norvig's advice to spend 20 minutes a day learning, and to focus on building a strong foundation in fundamental concepts (e.g., recursion, closures) rather than jumping into complex frameworks or tools, resonates with my own experience as a developer. This article serves as a reminder to prioritize gradual, deliberate learning over hasty attempts to stay up-to-date with the latest trends, and to always focus on building a deep understanding of the underlying principles of software development.
