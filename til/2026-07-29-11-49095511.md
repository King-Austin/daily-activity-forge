### Today I Learned (TIL)

**TIL about the Amiga Graphics Archive**

I came across the Amiga Graphics Archive (https://amiga.lychesis.net/index.html) on Hacker News, which reminded me of the rich history of computer graphics. The archive showcases a vast collection of Amiga-era graphics assets, including demos, logos, and icons. This got me thinking about the potential implications for modern game development and graphics rendering. The Amiga's custom chipset, the OCS (Original Chip Set), and ECS (Enhanced Chip Set), were capable of producing high-quality graphics with limited resources, making them a great example of efficient graphics rendering. This nostalgia trip has inspired me to learn more about the technical details of the Amiga's graphics architecture and how its design principles can be applied to modern graphics pipelines, potentially informing more efficient graphics rendering techniques in the future.
