### TIL: FCC National Security Determination on Foreign-Produced Robotic Devices

TIL that the US Federal Communications Commission (FCC) has issued a national security determination warning about the potential threats posed by foreign-produced robotic devices. This revelation raises concerns about the security of supply chains, particularly in the context of AI-powered robotics and IoT devices. As a software developer, I'm reminded to consider the potential risks of using foreign-made components in our projects, including issues like backdoors, data exfiltration, and intellectual property theft. This highlights the importance of assessing the security posture of third-party libraries and dependencies, and exploring alternatives sourced from trusted suppliers.
