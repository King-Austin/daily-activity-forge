**TIL: E-Book Reading Platform Built on Top of Android**

Today I learned about KOReader (https://koreader.rocks/), an open-source, multi-platform e-book reader that utilizes the Android Open Source Project (AOSP) as its base. This caught my attention because it highlights the potential for repurposing Android's underlying architecture for niche use cases beyond traditional mobile devices. The project's focus on customization and modularity also underscores the power of AOSP as a versatile, adaptable framework for building innovative applications. I'll be keeping an eye on KOReader's development to see how it leverages and extends Android's capabilities in the e-book reading domain.
