**Today I Learned**
TIL that the concept of an 'AI Crash' might not be a far-fetched scenario, as discussed in [After the AI Crash](https://potsandpansbyccg.com/2026/07/29/after-the-ai-crash/). This hypothetical event raises concerns about the fragility of large language models (LLMs) and their reliance on complex hyperparameter tuning, data curation, and computational resources. If such a crash were to occur, it could lead to significant disruptions in AI-powered systems, revealing the need for more robust and adaptive architectures, as well as the importance of investigating Explainable AI (XAI) techniques to improve model interpretability and trustworthiness.
