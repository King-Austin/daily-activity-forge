### TIL: Consider DuckDB over SQLite

TIL that DuckDB, a columnar in-memory database, may be a viable alternative to SQLite for certain use cases. Its performance advantages stem from its ability to execute aggregation queries much faster, thanks to its columnar storage and query optimization techniques. This makes it particularly well-suited for applications with complex analytics or machine learning workloads. While SQLite's simplicity and ease of use remain attractive, DuckDB's potential for significant performance gains and its ability to scale with larger datasets warrant consideration in projects where query performance is critical.
