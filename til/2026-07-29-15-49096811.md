### TIL (Today I Learned)
#### Godot VR and PSVR2 Porting: Optimizing for Performance and Compatibility

TIL that optimizing VR experiences for console devices like PSVR2 requires careful consideration of hardware constraints, particularly in terms of render target resolution, frame rate, and motion smoothing. According to the [Shipping Godot VR and Porting to PSVR2: A Partial Post Mortem](https://www.claire-blackshaw.com/blog/2026/07/shipping-godot-vr-and-porting-to-psvr2-a-partial-post-mortem/) article, one of the key challenges encountered was ensuring a stable 120 FPS on the PSVR2, which involved tweaking render target resolutions, disabling motion interpolation, and implementing a robust occlusion culling system. This experience highlights the importance of thorough testing and iteration to achieve optimal performance and compatibility when porting VR experiences to console platforms.
