# TIL: TokenTown: A visual way to understand how LLMs work

_2026-07-29 15:08 UTC_

[Original Source](https://laurentiugabriel.github.io/token-town/)

**TIL**: TokenTown (https://laurentiugabriel.github.io/token-town/) provides an interactive visualization of how Large Language Models (LLMs) process and generate text. This interactive model highlights the importance of tokenization, attention mechanisms, and the role of context in LLM decision-making. By breaking down the complexities of LLMs into an intuitive visual representation, TokenTown demonstrates how developers can leverage these components to improve the performance and interpretability of their own NLP applications, such as more effective language encoding and decoding techniques.
