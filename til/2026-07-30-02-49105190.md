# TIL: A pharmacy chain in Vermont implemented AI for efficiency

_2026-07-30 02:39 UTC_

[Original Source](https://vtdigger.org/2026/07/29/a-pharmacy-chain-in-vermont-implemented-ai-for-efficiency-its-led-to-delays-incorrect-information-and-privacy-concerns/)

**TIL**: When integrating AI into business-critical systems, it's crucial to not only consider technological efficiency, but also the potential impact on human processes and data handling. The reported implementation of AI at a Vermont pharmacy chain has led to delays, incorrect information, and privacy concerns, highlighting the risks of over-reliance on automated systems without adequate human oversight and data validation mechanisms. This serves as a reminder to developers to prioritize robust testing, data quality checks, and human-in-the-loop feedback mechanisms when building AI-driven systems to ensure they align with business goals and user needs.
