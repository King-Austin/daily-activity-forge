# TIL: NSF pilots 4-year PhDs with industry research placements

_2026-07-30 03:24 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.nsf.gov/news/nsf-partners-universities-industry-pilot-initiative-four)

**TIL: Industry-Academia Collaborative PhDs**
=====================================================

Today, I learned about the National Science Foundation's pilot initiative to offer 4-year PhD programs with industry research placements. This collaboration has the potential to bridge the gap between theoretical research and practical application in software development. The integration of industry expertise into PhD programs could lead to more industry-relevant research and a stronger pipeline of software engineers with a deeper understanding of the intersection of research and industry needs. This, in turn, may result in more impactful open-source projects and innovative solutions developed in collaboration between academia and industry.
