# TIL: 1,741 "informed" consents with one click? GDPR complaint filed

_2026-07-30 06:39 UTC_

[Original Source](https://noyb.eu/en/1741-informed-consents-one-click-gdpr-complaint-against-dictcc-filed)

**TIL: GDPR Consent Mechanisms May Be More Stringent Than Expected**
Today I learned that the Noyb initiative has filed a GDPR complaint against Dict.cc for allegedly obtaining 1,741 "informed" consents with a single click, potentially violating Article 7(2) of the GDPR. This highlights the importance of implementing explicit and granular consent mechanisms in web applications. As a developer, I should ensure that any consent requests are not buried in lengthy terms of service or made accessible only after a prolonged interaction. Instead, I should focus on designing user-centric interfaces that clearly explain the purpose of data collection and provide users with an easy-to-use opt-in process, ideally with separate checkboxes for each type of data being collected.
