### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL about the concept of finding the "most equidistant pub" in London through a clever algorithmic approach. The implementation uses a technique called " Voronoi diagrams" to divide the city's pubs into distinct regions, each containing the nearest pub to every point within its boundary. This has me thinking about potential applications in real-world problems, such as optimizing delivery routes for companies or identifying optimal locations for new infrastructure projects. The challenge of scaling this approach for larger cities or areas with dense pub distributions is also interesting to consider, and I'm curious to explore how this could be implemented using spatial data structures and geospatial libraries like PostGIS or Geopy.
