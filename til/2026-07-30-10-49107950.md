# TIL: Google will expand age checks on Android worldwide till the end of the year

_2026-07-30 10:40 UTC_

[Original Source](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/07/google-play-age-signals-api-safer-experiences.html)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that Google is expanding age checks on Android worldwide, requiring developers to implement the Google Play Age Signals API or face potential app removal from the Play Store by the end of the year. This means I'll need to ensure that my apps, especially those targeting minors, comply with Google's new policies by integrating this API, which will likely involve verifying user age, handling parental consent, and potentially implementing content restrictions. This shift highlights the increasing importance of implementing robust age verification and parental consent mechanisms to meet evolving regulatory requirements and maintain app store compliance.
