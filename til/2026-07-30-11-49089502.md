# Today I Learned
TIL that Azulejo, a traditional Portuguese art form, involves creating intricate, geometric patterns on blue and white tiles. This technique can be seen as analogous to the way we approach software development, particularly in the realm of GUI design. The use of repeating patterns, symmetry, and modular components can be applied to UI/UX design, allowing developers to create visually appealing and scalable interfaces. This art form's emphasis on repetition and standardization can also be seen as an early example of the modularity and reuse principles that underlie many modern software development methodologies, making it a fascinating historical precedent for our industry.
