### TIL: SOFR Interest Compounding on Unused Prepaid Credits

I came across an interesting article on Hacker News highlighting Carolina Cloud's practice of paying SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate) interest on unused prepaid credits. As a developer, this has implications for how we design and implement billing systems, especially when it comes to prepaid models. It's a good reminder to consider interest compounding when designing credit-based systems, which can result in significant accruals over time. This got me thinking about how we can build more accurate and nuanced billing systems that account for these types of scenarios, potentially leading to better user experiences and more realistic revenue projections.
