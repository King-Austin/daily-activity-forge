**TIL: GCC Steering Committee Announces AI Policy**
=====================================================

TIL that the GCC steering committee has established a policy for the use of AI in developing the GNU Compiler Collection (GCC). I'm curious to see how this will impact the community, but one potential implication is that it may lead to increased adoption of AI-assisted tools and automation for tasks such as code generation and optimization. This could bring significant performance improvements and speed up the development process, but it may also raise concerns about the potential for AI-generated code to become part of the GCC source base. I'll be keeping an eye on this development to see how it unfolds.
