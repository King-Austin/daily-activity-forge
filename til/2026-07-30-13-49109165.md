**2024-05-15: TIL about OpenJDK's Generative AI Policy Implications**

TIL that the OpenJDK Interim Policy on Generative AI introduces restrictions on the use of AI-generated code in OpenJDK projects, effective immediately. This policy has significant implications for developers who rely on tools like GitHub Copilot or other AI-powered code completion utilities. According to the policy, AI-generated code must be manually reviewed and validated before being submitted to OpenJDK repositories. This is expected to add an extra layer of complexity to the development workflow, particularly for large-scale projects. It's essential for developers to understand these new guidelines and adjust their development processes accordingly to avoid potential licensing or IP issues.
