# TIL: 'VPNs are lawful technical tools,' says EU Court in landmark copyright ruling

### Today I Learned
#### VPNs and EU Court Ruling

TIL that the EU Court has ruled VPNs as lawful technical tools, which may have significant implications for software developers. This ruling could lead to a shift in how we design and implement geo-restricted content access in our applications. With VPNs no longer considered a grey area, we may need to revisit our content delivery networks (CDNs) and geo-blocking strategies to ensure compliance with EU regulations. This could involve using more sophisticated IP address detection and location services, as well as implementing more flexible content serving mechanisms that can accommodate VPN users.
