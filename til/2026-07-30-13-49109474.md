# TIL: Internet Design Implications from RFC 8890

Today, I came across the IETF RFC 8890, "The Internet is for End Users", which highlights the need for internet protocols to prioritize end-user needs over engineering expediency. As a developer, thisRFC serves as a reminder that our design choices should be guided by the ultimate users of our systems, not just technical feasibility or engineering simplicity. This mindset shift has implications for how we approach protocol development, API design, and even the development of user-facing applications - prioritizing usability, accessibility, and reliability over raw performance or theoretical maximums.
