# TIL: Why Don't People Use Formal Methods?

_2026-07-30 14:41 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.hillelwayne.com/post/why-dont-people-use-formal-methods/)

Today I Learned
===============

TIL that the main reasons people don't use formal methods are not necessarily the lack of tools or complexity of the methods themselves, but rather the high overhead of manually writing and maintaining formal specifications, the difficulty of transitioning from informal code reviews to formal verification, and the tension between the 'just enough' nature of informal code reviews and the 'all-encompassing' nature of formal verification. This highlights the need for better tooling and processes that can bridge the gap between informal and formal verification, making it more practical and accessible for developers to incorporate formal methods into their workflow.
