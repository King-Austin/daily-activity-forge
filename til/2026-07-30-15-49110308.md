**Today I Learned: Lost Civic Life of Movie Rental Stores and the Value of Human Interaction**

TIL that the rise of digital storefronts has led to the decline of physical movie rental stores, highlighting the importance of human interaction in civic experiences. As a software developer, I realized that the erosion of these physical spaces has also led to a loss of community-driven recommendations, which could be replicated through more engaging and social features in digital platforms. This got me thinking about how we can reimplement the social aspects of physical stores, such as personalized recommendations, community-driven reviews, and in-store events, into our own software products, making them more enjoyable and interactive for users.
