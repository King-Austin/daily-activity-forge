# TIL: Why DNA damage from smoking and UV rays cause cancer in some but not others

_2026-07-30 15:11 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.cam.ac.uk/research/news/study-reveals-why-dna-damage-from-smoking-and-uv-rays-may-cause-cancer-in-some-people-but-not-others)

**Today I Learned**
===============

TIL that DNA damage from smoking and UV rays can cause cancer in some individuals due to the presence of a mutation in the ERCC2 gene, which affects the repair mechanism of damaged DNA. The study's findings highlight the complex interplay between genetics and environmental factors in disease manifestation. From a software development perspective, this reinforces the importance of considering the inherent variability in system inputs (in this case, genetic predisposition) and how it can lead to non-deterministic outcomes.
