# TIL: 2x, not 10x: coding with LLMs in 2026

_2026-07-30 20:12 UTC_

[Original Source](https://obryant.dev/p/2x-not-10x/)

**TIL: LLMs might not revolutionize coding as much as expected**

I came across an article on Hacker News, "2x, not 10x: coding with LLMs in 2026" by Owen Bryant, which presents a nuanced view on the potential impact of Large Language Models (LLMs) on coding productivity. The author argues that, while LLMs can certainly improve coding efficiency (2x), they might not lead to the often-expected 10x productivity boost. According to the article, this is because LLMs can automate routine tasks, but may not be able to replace human creativity and problem-solving skills. From a technical standpoint, this means that developers should focus on leveraging LLMs as tools to augment their workflow, rather than relying on them to replace key aspects of coding.
