# TIL: Investigating three real-world incidents in our cybersecurity evaluations

_2026-07-30 23:42 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/investigating-incidents-cybersecurity-evals)

**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL that even large-scale AI models like those developed by Anthropic can be vulnerable to cybersecurity threats. The article highlights three real-world incidents that demonstrate the importance of robust testing and evaluation in AI development. Specifically, the incidents reveal that edge cases and input validation can be crucial in preventing attacks. For instance, one of the incidents involved a model that was vulnerable to adversarial attacks due to a lack of input validation, which could have led to unintended consequences. This emphasizes the need for developers to prioritize thorough testing, validating inputs, and considering edge cases to ensure the security and reliability of AI-powered software systems.
