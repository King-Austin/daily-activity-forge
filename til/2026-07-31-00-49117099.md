### TIL: The AI Aesthetic

TIL that the increasing prevalence of AI-generated content may lead to the emergence of a new 'AI Aesthetic' - a set of stylistic conventions and tropes that are inadvertently codified by AI algorithms. As a developer, this trend has implications for our work in areas like design, user interface (UI) development, and even code generation. For instance, it's essential to consider the potential for AI-generated components to influence the visual and interactive design of software applications, and to ensure that our development processes account for this emerging aesthetic. By understanding the AI Aesthetic, we can better anticipate and mitigate its effects, and create more effective and user-friendly software products that balance AI-generated elements with human intuition and taste.
