# TIL: A California aquifer may have crossed the point of no return

_2026-07-31 03:42 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.science.org/content/article/california-aquifer-may-have-crossed-point-no-return)

# Today I Learned

TIL that the collapse of a massive California aquifer, which supplies ~30% of the state's water, could have significant implications for water resource management systems and potentially inform software design for sustainable resource allocation. The concept of a "point of no return" in this context, where the aquifer's collapse becomes irreversible, highlights the importance of monitoring and predictive modeling in water management. As a developer, I'm reminded of the need for robust data integration, real-time monitoring, and probabilistic modeling to anticipate and mitigate the effects of such environmental tipping points, potentially informing the design of more resilient and adaptive systems for resource management.
