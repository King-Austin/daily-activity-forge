**TIL: Value Objects in Java**

I came across the news that [JEP 401: Value Objects](https://github.com/openjdk/jdk/pull/31120) has been merged into OpenJDK master. This addition will introduce a new concept in Java, allowing developers to create immutable, equality-sensitive, and hashcode-aware objects that can be used to model distinct values. With Value Objects, developers can now more effectively encapsulate and manage collections of related, invariant data, reducing the complexity and improving the readability of their codebases. This feature has the potential to simplify the implementation of domain modeling and business logic, making it easier to write robust, maintainable, and self-documenting code.
