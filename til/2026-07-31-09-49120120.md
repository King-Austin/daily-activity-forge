### Today I Learned
#### TIL: Resilience and Redundancy in Critical Infrastructure Systems

While reading about Danube's record low levels forcing the shutdown of Hungary's only nuclear plant, I was reminded of the importance of designing systems with resilience and redundancy in mind. This event highlights the potential consequences of failing to account for unexpected environmental factors in critical infrastructure, such as nuclear power plants. As a developer, I'm taking note of the need to incorporate fail-safes, backup systems, and robust monitoring mechanisms in our own projects, particularly when working with high-stakes applications or integrating with third-party services. This lesson can be applied to various domains, from cloud computing and distributed systems to IoT and DevOps pipelines, where system downtime or failure can have significant repercussions.
