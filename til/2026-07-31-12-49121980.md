### TIL: Implications of Smashwords' Closure on eBook Distribution

TIL that Smashwords, a major eBook distributor, has been acquired by Apple and will be shutting down its services. As a developer, this news has significant implications for eBook distribution and the potential shift towards more direct relationships between authors and platforms. The closure of Smashwords may lead to increased adoption of alternative eBook distribution channels, such as Amazon's KDP Direct or Apple Books' own publishing platform. This development could also influence the way authors and developers approach eBook formatting, metadata management, and DRM (Digital Rights Management) solutions, as these platforms may have different requirements and constraints.
