# TIL: Severance

_2026-07-31 20:59 UTC_

[Original Source](https://lcamtuf.substack.com/p/severance)

TIL that the concept of "Severance" - a hypothetical future-proofing measure for online services - is gaining traction. In essence, Severance proposes that software developers embed metadata into their codebases to provide a clear, automated path for services to "sever" their connections to dependencies that become problematic, malicious, or abandoned. This could potentially mitigate the impact of third-party API deprecation, security vulnerabilities, or even the catastrophic failure of entire ecosystems, by allowing services to automatically disconnect and redirect users to more reliable alternatives.
