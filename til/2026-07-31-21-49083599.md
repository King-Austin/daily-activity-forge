**TIL: Model Weights as a New Form of Intellectual Property**
I came across an insightful article highlighting the shift in sensitive data from cryptographic keys to AI model weights. This has significant implications for software developers: just as cryptographic keys can be thought of as trade secrets, model weights can now be seen as a new form of intellectual property. This means that companies may view their AI models as proprietary assets, and protecting them from theft or reverse-engineering becomes crucial. As a developer, I'll need to consider the security implications of model weight storage, transmission, and sharing, potentially using techniques like encryption, secure enclaves, or secure multi-party computation to safeguard these valuable assets.
