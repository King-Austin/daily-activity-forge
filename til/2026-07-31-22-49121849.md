# Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that the law of diminishing returns applies to more than just software optimization. The article on "Increasing the lifespan of a bulb makes it worse in every other way" highlights the trade-offs involved in engineering design. This concept can be applied to software development when trying to optimize system performance or resource utilization. For instance, a system optimized for low latency may sacrifice database query complexity, while a system optimized for high availability may compromise on data consistency. Understanding these trade-offs and the law of diminishing returns can help developers make more informed design decisions when faced with conflicting requirements.
