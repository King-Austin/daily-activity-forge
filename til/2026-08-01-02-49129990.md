# Today I Learned
TIL about the importance of existing over constantly striving for productivity and efficiency, as discussed in the article "How to Exist" (https://www.raptitude.com/2026/07/how-to-exist/). In the context of software development, this concept resonated with me as I realized the potential downsides of an over-reliance on automation and optimization. While these tools can certainly streamline workflows, they can also lead to a sense of disconnection and burnout. As a developer, I need to be mindful of striking a balance between using technology to augment my work and making time for the 'non-existent' moments that allow me to recharge and maintain a healthy sense of purpose.
