# TIL: AI doesn't generate working products, that's still your job

_2026-08-01 09:30 UTC_

[Original Source](https://weeraman.com/the-prototype-isnt-the-product/)

### TIL (Today I Learned)
#### AI-generated code is not a replacement for human expertise

After reading the article "The prototype isn't the product" on Hacker News, I was reminded that AI tools, despite their rapid advancements, still lack the nuance and problem-solving skills of human developers. This means that while AI can generate code snippets, it's up to us to ensure that the generated code is properly integrated, tested, and refined to meet real-world requirements. I'm taking away the importance of critically evaluating AI-generated code and focusing on what I do best - writing high-quality, maintainable software that solves real problems.
