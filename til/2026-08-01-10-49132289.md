# TIL: Focus on Depth Over Breadth
Today I learned about the importance of making significant contributions in software development, as outlined in Paul Graham's 'How to Do Great Work'. One key takeaway is the need to focus on depth over breadth in our projects. Rather than spreading ourselves too thin by working on multiple, shallow features, we should aim to tackle fewer, but more complex and impactful tasks. This approach can lead to more substantial and meaningful contributions, as well as increased efficiency and reduced cognitive load. By prioritizing depth, we can create a lasting impact and make our work stand out in the industry.
