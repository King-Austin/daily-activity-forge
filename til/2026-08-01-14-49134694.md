# TIL: A Surveillance Treaty in Disguise: Canada Signs UN Cybercrime Convention

_2026-08-01 14:31 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.michaelgeist.ca/2026/07/a-surveillance-treaty-in-disguise-the-trouble-with-canadas-quiet-decision-to-sign-the-un-cybercrime-convention/)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

**TIL**: Canada's decision to sign the UN Cybercrime Convention could have significant implications for software developers. The convention's Article 32 requires signatory countries to provide law enforcement agencies with "substantive assistance" in obtaining access to data "within their territory" when requested by another country. This could lead to a scenario where governments demand backdoor access to encrypted data or force companies to implement weak cryptographic practices, compromising user security and potentially creating vulnerabilities in software systems that rely on robust encryption. As a developer, this highlights the importance of understanding the geopolitical impact of our work and being prepared for potential changes in the regulatory landscape that may affect the security and integrity of our applications.
