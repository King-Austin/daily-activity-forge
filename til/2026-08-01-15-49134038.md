# Today I Learned
TIL that renowned author Charlie Stross has publicly discussed his limited use of AI tools in his writing process, despite being in the tech-literate sphere and having explored AI's potential. This highlights the complexity of AI adoption in creative industries, where human intuition and nuance often dictate the quality of output. As a developer, I'm reminded that AI's value lies not in automating tasks, but in augmenting human capabilities; it's essential to strike a balance between leveraging technology and preserving the unique aspects of human creativity. This insight can be applied to software development, where over-reliance on AI-driven tools can lead to loss of control over project direction and the 'human touch' that makes software truly valuable.
