# Today I Learned

This design decision implies a paradigm shift from cost-centric to token-centric thinking, potentially influencing how developers approach analytics and pricing strategies. It also highlights the importance of considering the user's perspective and the potential for token-based economies to become a dominant force in software development and resource utilization.
