### TIL: Explorative Modeling for Improved Model Performance

I came across an interesting blog post on Hacker News titled "Explorative modeling: Train on the best of K guesses" which discusses a novel approach to model training. The core idea is to train a model on the top K predictions across multiple iterations of the same model, leveraging the benefits of both ensemble methods and self-supervised learning. This technique seems to offer a promising way to improve model performance by learning from the best outcomes of multiple runs, allowing for the capture of more nuanced and diverse patterns in the data. I'm intrigued by the potential for this method to be applied to our machine learning pipelines, particularly in cases where traditional ensemble techniques fall short.
