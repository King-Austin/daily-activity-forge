# TIL: Kaisel – Routes as Values. Dart 3 Native Router for Flutter

_2026-08-01 17:31 UTC_

[Original Source](https://kaisel.dev/)

**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL about Kaisel, a native router for Flutter built using Dart 3. This caught my attention as it proposes a novel approach to routing by embedding routes directly into the codebase as values. This concept could potentially simplify navigation and reduce the boilerplate associated with traditional route management approaches. I'm intrigued by the possibility of improved code organization, maintainability, and developer productivity that Kaisel might offer. However, I'll need to explore its implementation and performance characteristics further to truly understand its implications and potential adoption in Flutter projects.
