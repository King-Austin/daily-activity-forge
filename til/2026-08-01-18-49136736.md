**TIL**: NetBSD 11.0 Released

I came across the announcement of NetBSD 11.0 release, which is notable for its improved support for modern hardware and software stacks. One key takeaway is the enhancement of NetBSD's ZFS file system support, which now includes features like encryption and snapshotting. This update suggests that NetBSD is continuing to evolve as a viable alternative to other Unix-like operating systems, and its improved ZFS support could make it more attractive to developers working with large datasets or requiring advanced storage management capabilities. Additionally, the release's focus on hardware support means that developers may now have more options for running NetBSD on a wider range of systems, making it a potential choice for projects requiring a Unix-like environment on non-x86 architectures.
