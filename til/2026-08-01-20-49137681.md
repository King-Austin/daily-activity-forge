# TIL: Google News is just Forrest Gump's shrimp boat now

_2026-08-01 20:01 UTC_

[Original Source](https://elgan.com/google-news-is-just-forrest-gumps-shrimp-boat-now)

**TIL:** Google News' aggregation approach might be similar to Forrest Gump's shrimp boat, implying a "catch-all" strategy where relevance is secondary to sheer volume. This approach can be seen as a trade-off between comprehensiveness and accuracy in news aggregation. As a developer, this analogy highlights the importance of considering the trade-offs between breadth and depth in data ingestion and processing, particularly when dealing with large datasets. This could inform the design of data pipelines, where prioritizing completeness might come at the cost of nuanced filtering or contextual understanding.
