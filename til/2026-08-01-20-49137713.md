### TIL: Embracing the spirit of 'But can your calculator run Linux?'

TIL that the concept of Linux on calculators can be extended to more complex systems, pushing the boundaries of what is considered a 'traditional' computing platform. This idea is not just a novelty, but rather a thought experiment that exposes the inherent portability and flexibility of the Linux operating system. It challenges developers to consider the minimum requirements for running a full-fledged OS on unconventional hardware, and the potential implications for low-power, embedded systems development. This perspective can inspire innovative approaches to hardware abstraction, device drivers, and system software design, ultimately enabling the creation of more efficient and versatile computing systems.
