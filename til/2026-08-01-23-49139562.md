# TIL: Simplify database interactions to reduce complexity
I came across an interesting article on Hacker News 'Stop fucking around with database commits and transactions' which highlights the importance of keeping database interactions as simple and straightforward as possible. The author argues that over-engineering database commits and transactions can lead to unnecessary complexity, increased error rates, and decreased performance. Key takeaways include: (1) use auto-commit mode when possible, (2) minimize the use of manual transactions, (3) focus on atomic operations and error handling, and (4) avoid mixing business logic with database operations. By applying these principles, developers can write more maintainable, efficient, and scalable database code.
