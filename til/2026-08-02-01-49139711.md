### TIL: Deprecation of Obsolete TLS Key Exchange Methods

TIL that RFC 10015 proposes deprecation of outdated key exchange methods in TLS 1.2 and DTLS 1.2, including ECDHE_PSK, ECDHE_PSK_WITH_3DES_EDE_CBC_SHA, and PSK_DHE_PSK_WITH_3DES_EDE_CBC_SHA. This change may require updates to cryptographic libraries and TLS implementations to comply with future standards. In practice, this means we should start exploring alternative key exchange methods, such as those using elliptic curves or modern symmetric ciphers, and consider implementing TLS 1.3 or DTLS 1.3 which have already introduced more secure default configurations.
