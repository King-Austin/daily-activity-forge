### TIL: Grid-Scale Solar Inverters and Home Battery Systems May Soon be Integrated

TIL that grid-scale solar inverters, which currently allow homeowners to sell excess energy back to the grid, may soon be integrated with home battery systems to create a seamless plug-and-play solar-battery hybrid. This development implies that software developers working on IoT and home automation projects may soon need to consider integrating battery management systems (BMS) with existing smart home infrastructure, requiring a deeper understanding of power electronics, grid dynamics, and communication protocols such as Modbus or IEC 61850.
