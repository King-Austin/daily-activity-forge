### TIL: Implications of Not Overcrediting LLMs

TIL that overcrediting Large Language Models (LLMs) can lead to a lack of accountability and understanding of underlying mechanics. This realization came from reading Isaac Su's article, 'Don't credit the LLM'. The implication for me as a developer is that I should focus on understanding the specific components and techniques used in an LLM, rather than relying solely on its outputs. By doing so, I can better anticipate potential errors and biases, and develop more robust systems that account for these limitations. This shift in perspective can also lead to more effective fine-tuning and adaptation of LLMs to specific use cases, ultimately resulting in more reliable and maintainable software.
