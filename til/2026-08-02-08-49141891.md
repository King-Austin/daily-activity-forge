### TIL - Potential Risks of Over-Reliance on Utility-First CSS Frameworks

After reading the article "Why I don't recommend Tailwind CSS" on Hacker News, I've gained a new perspective on the potential risks of relying heavily on utility-first CSS frameworks like Tailwind. One key takeaway is that over-reliance on such frameworks can lead to a lack of understanding of fundamental CSS concepts, making it challenging to refactor or maintain complex styles when the framework is not available or is modified. This highlights the importance of striking a balance between utilizing productivity tools and ensuring that developers have a solid grasp of underlying technologies.
