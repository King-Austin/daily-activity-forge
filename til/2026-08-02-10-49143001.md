### AI, Instant Knowledge, and the Ars Notoria

TIL that the concept of 'Ars Notoria', a medieval manuscript promising instant knowledge, has parallels in modern AI research, particularly in areas like knowledge graph construction and information retrieval. The idea of a single, unified repository of knowledge, where complex concepts can be linked and queried, resonates with the goals of AI systems like Semantic Knowledge Graphs and graph-based neural networks. This concept has implications for how we design knowledge representation models, potentially enabling more efficient and accurate information retrieval, and paving the way for more sophisticated AI applications that can tap into vast, interconnected knowledge bases.
