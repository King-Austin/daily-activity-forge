# TIL: Is the Industrial Revolution a good precedent for explosive growth today?

_2026-08-02 11:03 UTC_

[Original Source](https://mattsclancy.github.io/2026/07/27/industrial-revolution-growth.html)

**TIL: Reevaluating Scalability through Historical Analogies**
===========================================================

TIL that the Industrial Revolution's explosive growth may not be a suitable precedent for today's tech scaling challenges. The article highlights the vastly different factors contributing to growth in the 18th-19th centuries versus our current era, including global population, resource availability, and technological advancements. This got me thinking about the limitations of analogously applying historical growth patterns to our modern software development problems. For instance, the 'scaling up' of software systems might not follow the same exponential growth curve as industrial production, and we need to consider more nuanced factors such as network effects, resource constraints, and the role of automation in modern scalability. This nuanced understanding can help us make more informed decisions when designing and optimizing our systems for growth.
