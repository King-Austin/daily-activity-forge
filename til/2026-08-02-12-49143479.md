**TIL: Meshdiff's Approach to Client-Side STL Comparison Offers Insights into Efficient Binary Data Processing**
Today I learned about Meshdiff, a browser-based tool for visually comparing two STL versions. Its ability to handle large binary data sets and perform comparisons in the browser, without relying on server-side processing, highlights the potential of modern browser capabilities and Web Assembly. The technical takeaway is that Web Assembly can be used to offload computationally intensive tasks, like mesh comparison, from the server, enabling faster and more scalable solutions. This approach also underscores the importance of exploring and leveraging browser-side capabilities for data processing and visualization, especially when dealing with large datasets.
