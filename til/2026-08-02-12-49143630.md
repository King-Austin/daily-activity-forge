# TIL: Only 8.9% of sites block AI crawlers, but 94.8% are never cited in AI answers

_2026-08-02 12:33 UTC_

[Original Source](https://website-auditor.io/ai-visibility-index)

### TIL: AI-Driven Visibility and SEO Implications

TIL that despite a significant number of sites blocking AI crawlers, most websites are never cited in AI-driven answers, suggesting that AI-driven content generation may not be heavily reliant on crawling the entire web. This has implications for developers, as it means that even if a site is not crawled by AI crawlers, it may still be at risk of having its content scraped or used in AI-generated responses through other means, highlighting the need for a comprehensive content protection strategy that goes beyond traditional SEO measures and AI crawler blocking.
