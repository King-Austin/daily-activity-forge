### TIL: RISC OS Open's 20th Anniversary

TIL that RISC OS Open has been maintaining the legacy of Acorn's RISC OS for 20 years. This news may seem nostalgic, but it has a few tech-savvy takeaways for developers. RISC OS, known for its simplicity and efficiency, has been a significant influence on modern operating systems. Its unique architecture and minimalistic design make it an interesting case study for system programming and OS development. Furthermore, RISC OS Open's commitment to open-source and community-driven development may inspire a new wave of OS innovation, highlighting the potential benefits of collaboration and transparency in software development.
