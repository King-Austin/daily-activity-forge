## TIL: Critique of Unfettered Technological Progress

TIL that Ursula K. Le Guin's 2005 essay "A Rant About 'Technology'" advocates for a more nuanced approach to technological advancement, underscoring the importance of considering the broader societal and environmental implications of our innovations. As developers, this resonates with the need to adopt responsible AI/ML practices, prioritize data privacy, and incorporate principles of sustainability and environmental responsibility into our design decisions, lest we create systems that exacerbate existing problems rather than solving them.
