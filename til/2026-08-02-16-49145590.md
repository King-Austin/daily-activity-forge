# TIL: How the words we teach English language learners changed

_2026-08-02 16:18 UTC_

[Original Source](https://pudding.cool/2026/07/essential-words/)

**TIL (Today I Learned)**

I came across a fascinating article on Hacker News highlighting how the words taught to English language learners have shifted over time. The article suggests that the most commonly taught vocabulary has changed significantly since the 1970s, with a greater emphasis on technology-related terms and a decrease in traditional vocabulary. As a developer, I'm aware of the importance of clear and concise communication in software development, and this shift in vocabulary has implications for how we write documentation, comments, and even user interface text. For example, a growing number of users may not be familiar with traditional idioms or colloquialisms, which could impact the effectiveness of onboarding processes or error messages, motivating me to consider using more modern and technology-focused language in my code and documentation.
