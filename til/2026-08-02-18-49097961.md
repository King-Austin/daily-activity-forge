**TIL: The Psychological Aspect of Tool Adoption**

Today I learned that the trust developers place in tools is not just about their functionality, but also about the security, reliability, and consistency they provide. This concept, coined as "tools encode trust", highlights how our attachment to tools can be a result of the emotional safety they offer. As a developer, I should be aware that the tools I choose to use often reflect my own values and biases, and can influence my decisions on other aspects of software development. This means that I should actively evaluate not only the technical merits of a tool, but also its potential impact on my workflow, decision-making, and the overall quality of the software I produce.
