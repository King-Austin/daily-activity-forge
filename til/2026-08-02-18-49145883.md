**TIL: Insecure IoT Devices Pose a Risk to Network Security**

Today, I read about the TP-Link TL-841N router being vulnerable to rooting and firmware analysis, leading to the exposure of persistent credentials. This highlights the importance of secure development practices and supply chain security in IoT devices. As a developer, I learned that a device's firmware can be a critical vulnerability point, and even seemingly secure devices can have hidden backdoors. This serves as a reminder to consider the security implications of using third-party hardware and software components in our projects, and to include security testing and analysis in our development workflows.
