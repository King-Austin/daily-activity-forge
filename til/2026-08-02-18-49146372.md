### TIL: Importance of Secure Hardware Abstraction Layers in Cryptography

TIL that the recent Coldcard hack, which may have resulted in losses of up to $88.6M, highlights the critical importance of secure hardware abstraction layers (HALs) in cryptographic systems. The issue appears to be related to the use of an insecure HAL, allowing an attacker to inject malicious code into the system. This vulnerability demonstrates the need for robust and thoroughly audited HALs in cryptographic applications, which can be particularly vulnerable to side-channel attacks. As a developer working with cryptocurrency or secure systems, this serves as a reminder to prioritize the security of underlying hardware interfaces and to carefully evaluate the trustworthiness of any third-party libraries or components used in the development process.
