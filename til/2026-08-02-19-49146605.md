**TIL: SSH Credential Harvesting Risks**
=====================================

After reading "Harvesting SSH Credentials: Insights from My Honeypot Network", I've taken away a crucial reminder of the importance of secure SSH key management. The article highlights the alarming rate at which attackers are harvesting SSH credentials, often through automated scripts and brute-force attacks. This got me thinking about the potential security implications for our own codebase. Specifically, I'll be revisiting our deployment scripts to ensure that sensitive SSH keys are properly secured, rotated regularly, and not hard-coded in plain text. Additionally, I'll be exploring ways to implement SSH key revocation and monitoring for suspicious login activity to quickly detect and mitigate any potential breaches.
