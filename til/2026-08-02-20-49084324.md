**TIL:** I came across an insightful article on note-taking and personal knowledge management strategies. One key takeaway is the emphasis on the importance of creating a robust, searchable, and customizable note-taking system. For a software developer, this means considering the implementation of a knowledge graph or graph database to efficiently store, link, and query notes. This can be particularly useful for tracking dependencies between projects, recording decisions, and preserving context during the development process.
