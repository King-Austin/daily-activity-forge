### TIL: Tiny Language Models on Tiny Hardware

TIL that Matt Berton has successfully implemented an autoregressive language model on the 6502 processor, a 1970s-era 8-bit microcontroller. This achievement demonstrates the possibility of running complex AI models on extremely resource-constrained hardware, pushing the boundaries of what's thought possible with tiny machines. The implications are fascinating: it opens up possibilities for building AI-powered devices with minimal power consumption and low memory requirements, making them suitable for IoT applications, robotics, or even edge computing scenarios where resources are limited. This work highlights the importance of rethinking traditional assumptions about the relationship between model complexity and hardware requirements.
