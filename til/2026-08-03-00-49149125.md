**TIL: Computational Theory of Mind**

I just read the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy's entry on the Computational Theory of Mind (2015) and I'm struck by the parallels between its theoretical frameworks and software development principles. According to this theory, the mind can be understood as a computational system, processing information through symbolic manipulation. This has implications for our approaches to artificial intelligence and machine learning, as it suggests that cognition can be reduced to a set of computational processes that can be modeled and optimized. In practice, this means that we can draw inspiration from cognitive architectures when designing complex software systems, such as using modular and hierarchical structures to facilitate information processing and knowledge representation.
