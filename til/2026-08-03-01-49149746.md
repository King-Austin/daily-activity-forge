# TIL: Why Book Corners won't sync contributions back to OpenStreetMap

_2026-08-03 01:04 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.andreagrandi.it/posts/why-book-corners-wont-sync-contributions-back-to-openstreetmap/)

**Today I Learned**
TIL the concept of 'impossible' or 'undesirable' data synchronization, as highlighted by the OpenStreetMap (OSM) Book Corners controversy. The article demonstrates that even well-intentioned projects can have unforeseen consequences when attempting to integrate multiple data sources. Specifically, it shows how the 'source of truth' problem can arise when trying to sync data between OSM and Book Corners, where data is either lost or inconsistent. This serves as a reminder for developers to consider the potential implications of data synchronization, including data loss, inconsistencies, or 'impossible' state transitions, and to carefully evaluate the trade-offs when integrating multiple data sources.
