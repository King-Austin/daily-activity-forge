# TIL: CP/M-386 – CP/M for 386 protected mode, derived from CP/M‑68K

_2026-08-03 01:34 UTC_

[Original Source](https://github.com/johnsonjh/cpm386)

### Today I Learned

TIL that the CP/M-386 project, a revival of the classic CP/M operating system for 386 protected mode, exists. The implications of this project are multifaceted: it demonstrates that modern CPUs can support legacy operating systems, and that the architecture of 80s-era systems can still be exploited for educational or nostalgic purposes. This project also highlights the importance of understanding the evolution of operating systems and the impact of CPU architecture on system design. As a developer, I'm reminded to appreciate the roots of modern computing and to consider the trade-offs between legacy systems and modern architectures when tackling complex projects.
