**Today I Learned (TIL) - Isopolis**

TIL about the potential of using 3D rendering libraries to generate isometric pixel art maps. The Isopolis project uses WebGL to render a stunning isometric pixel map of San Francisco. This demonstrates the feasibility of leveraging 3D rendering techniques for 2D isometric art, which could be particularly useful for game development, map-making, or even web-based visualization tools. The approach also opens up opportunities for exploring new levels of detail and interactivity in isometric art, such as dynamic lighting, animations, and data visualization overlays.
