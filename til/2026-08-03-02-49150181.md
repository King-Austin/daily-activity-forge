**TIL: TLS 1.2 Feature Freeze**
================================

RFC 9851 marks the feature freeze for TLS 1.2, indicating that no new features or changes will be made to this protocol variant. This has significant implications for our projects: we should begin planning for eventual TLS 1.2 deprecation in favor of TLS 1.3 or later. Key takeaways include reviewing all dependencies for TLS 1.2 support, considering upgrading to a compatible TLS version, and developing a strategy for supporting older clients that may not be compatible with newer TLS versions. With this change, it's essential to prioritize secure communication protocols and stay up-to-date with TLS best practices to ensure our applications remain secure and compatible with evolving standards.
