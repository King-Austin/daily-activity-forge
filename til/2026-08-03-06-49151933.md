# TIL: Don't Be a Meat Proxy

_2026-08-03 06:35 UTC_

[Original Source](https://gruhn.me/blog/2026-08-03/)

**TIL: Avoiding the Pitfalls of External Dependencies**

Just read the thought-provoking article "Don't Be a Meat Proxy" by a fellow developer, which highlights the importance of avoiding tightly coupled systems and external dependencies. The author emphasizes that when we expose internal implementation details to external services, we risk creating a "meat proxy" - a system that's dependent on the implementation specifics of its external counterpart, making it brittle and hard to maintain. This got me thinking about the implications for our own codebases: we should strive to use APIs and services in a way that decouples our internal logic from external dependencies, and instead focus on abstracting away the underlying implementation to ensure our systems remain flexible, resilient, and easier to evolve over time.
