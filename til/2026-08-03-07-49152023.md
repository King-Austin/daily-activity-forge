### Today I Learned
#### Immobile Types and Guaranteed Destructors in Rust

TIL that Rust's project goals include introducing immobile types and guaranteed destructors, which could significantly impact how we design and manage resources in Rust. This change aims to provide a way to ensure that types cannot be moved, effectively making them "immobile," and guaranteeing that destructors are always called, even in the presence of panics. This could lead to improved resource management, reduced risk of resource leaks, and more predictable behavior in error scenarios, making Rust an even more attractive choice for systems programming and high-reliability applications.
