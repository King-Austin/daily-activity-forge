**TIL: Rethinking the Role of LLMs in Development**

I came across a thought-provoking article today suggesting that manually retyping LLM (Large Language Model)-generated code can prevent cognitive debt. While this might seem counterintuitive, it highlights the importance of understanding and reviewing the output of AI-assisted tools to avoid blindly adopting potentially flawed or over-engineered solutions. This got me thinking about the potential risks of relying too heavily on LLMs for code generation, including decreased developer comprehension of the codebase and reduced ability to troubleshoot complex issues. As a developer, I'll be keeping this in mind when utilizing LLMs in the future and making a conscious effort to review and understand the code they generate, rather than simply accepting it at face value.
