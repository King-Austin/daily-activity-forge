# TIL: What DMARC Protects You From, and What It Does Not

_2026-08-03 11:07 UTC_

[Original Source](https://senderledger.com/articles/what-dmarc-actually-protects-you-from)

**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL that DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance) policies do not protect against the most common type of phishing attacks: domain spoofing. Despite its name, DMARC is primarily focused on preventing email spoofing where the sender's domain is not authorized to send emails on behalf of another domain, but does not prevent attackers from sending emails that appear to come from a legitimate domain. This means that developers should not rely solely on DMARC to prevent phishing attacks, and instead focus on implementing additional security measures such as DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail) and SPF (Sender Policy Framework) to provide more robust email authentication and protection.
