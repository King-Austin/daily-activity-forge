### TIL: SPF Record Syntax Gotchas

TIL that SPF record syntax is more complex than I thought, with multiple mechanisms (e.g., a, mx, ip4, ip6), qualifiers (e.g., include, exists, redirect), and modifiers (e.g., all, ~, -). Macros (e.g., %s, %p) also offer flexible lookup and substitution options. This nuanced syntax can have significant implications for email deliverability, especially when incorrectly configured. For instance, a typo in the SPF record can lead to mail being incorrectly flagged as spam or rejected altogether. I'll need to be more meticulous when implementing email validation in our application to avoid these gotchas.
