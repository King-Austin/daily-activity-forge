### TIL: C-Kermit Release Highlights the Challenges of Maintaining Legacy Codebases

Today I learned that the release of C-Kermit after a 15-year hiatus highlights the difficulties of maintaining long-lived, complex codebases. With over 40 years of development history, C-Kermit's codebase is likely a testament to the importance of modular design, refactoring, and incremental updates to ensure continued maintainability. The fact that a new release was made possible suggests that careful stewardship and incremental modernization efforts were invested in the codebase, underscoring the value of gradual updates to legacy systems rather than attempting a comprehensive rewrite. This serves as a reminder that even the most seemingly archaic systems can be revitalized with attention to their underlying structure and incremental updates.
