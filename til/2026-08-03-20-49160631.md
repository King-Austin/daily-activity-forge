# TIL: Importance of DDoS Mitigation and Incident Response Planning

TIL that a DDoS attack against Norway's government IT infrastructure highlights the need for robust security measures and incident response planning. This incident serves as a reminder that even well-resourced organizations can be vulnerable to targeted attacks. A key takeaway is the importance of having a DDoS mitigation strategy in place, which may involve techniques like traffic filtering, rate limiting, and content delivery network (CDN) services. Additionally, a well-documented incident response plan can help organizations minimize downtime and quickly recover from such attacks. As a developer, this serves as a reminder to prioritize security in our designs and consider the potential impact of a DDoS attack on our systems.
