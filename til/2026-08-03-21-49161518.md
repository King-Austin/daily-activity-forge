# TIL: LLMs reward expertise

_2026-08-03 21:23 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.seangoedecke.com/llms-reward-expertise/)

Today I Learned: 
TIL that LLMs (Large Language Models) may exhibit a bias towards rewarding expertise in text-based evaluations, potentially creating an uneven playing field for individuals with varying levels of domain knowledge. This phenomenon could have significant implications for software development, as it may influence the way we design and interact with AI-powered tools, such as chatbots and documentation generators. Specifically, I'm considering the potential need to mitigate these biases in our own applications, and exploring ways to ensure that LLMs are evaluated on a more level playing field, perhaps by incorporating more nuanced evaluation metrics or augmenting LLMs with human feedback loops.
