### TIL: Interview Preparation Requires More Than Technical Knowledge

I came across an insightful article on Hacker News detailing the author's experience failing a Microsoft interview. While not directly related to a technical takeaway, it highlights the importance of preparation beyond just knowing the intricacies of algorithms and data structures. The author's failure was attributed to a lack of understanding of the company culture and the types of problems they typically solve, as well as an inability to articulate their own experiences and motivations. This serves as a reminder that software development is not solely about writing code, but also about effective communication, teamwork, and problem-solving in a business context.
