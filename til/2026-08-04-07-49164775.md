# Today I Learned
### EV Adoption Trends and Implications for Software Developers

TIL that Denmark has reached a new milestone with 97% of new private car sales being electric vehicles (EVs) in July. This trend has significant implications for software developers working on automotive-related projects, particularly those involving IoT, mobility, and sustainability. As EVs become increasingly prevalent, we can expect to see more demand for software solutions that cater to their unique characteristics, such as real-time charging management, route optimization for EV-friendly charging stations, and integration with smart home systems for seamless energy management. Developers should be prepared to adapt to these emerging requirements and stay up-to-date with the latest advancements in EV technology to remain competitive in this rapidly evolving field.
