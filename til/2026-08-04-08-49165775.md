### TIL - Palantir's Access to Identifiable Patient Data

TIL that the NHS has admitted Palantir engineers have access to identifiable patient data, raising concerns about data protection and access controls. As a developer, I'm reminded of the importance of implementing least privilege access and ensuring data anonymization or pseudonymization when sharing sensitive information with third-party vendors. This incident highlights the need for robust data governance frameworks, including clear roles and responsibilities, access logs, and regular audits to detect and prevent unauthorized access. It's also a reminder to scrutinize the terms and conditions of vendor contracts, ensuring they align with organizational security policies and regulatory requirements.
