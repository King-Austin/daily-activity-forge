### TIL: Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement

TIL that I can leverage the principles of software engineering to drive personal growth and self-improvement, as outlined in the article "Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement" by Lilian Weng (https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/). Specifically, I found the concept of "Technical Debt" being applied to personal growth to be particularly insightful, where I can acknowledge and actively manage my own "emotional debt" by tracking and addressing areas where I'm holding onto negative emotions or self-doubt. This has the potential to improve my mental well-being and increase my productivity, much like how refactoring technical debt improves the maintainability and reliability of code.
