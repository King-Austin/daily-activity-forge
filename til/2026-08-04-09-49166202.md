### TIL: FFmpeg 9.0 Release Notes

TIL that FFmpeg 9.0 introduces significant improvements in performance, including a 20-30% boost in decoding speed due to the adoption of AV1 and AVX-512 accelerations, as well as improved support for 8K and 12-bit video encoding. Additionally, the new release includes a major refactor of the `libavfilter` API, making it easier to develop and integrate custom video filters. These advancements imply that developers working with multimedia processing and transcoding can expect faster execution times and more efficient memory usage, allowing for more complex tasks to be handled within existing resource constraints.
