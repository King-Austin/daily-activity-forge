### TIL: System Thinking and Holistic Optimization

TIL that Buckminster Fuller's philosophy of "doing more with less" and "synergetics" can be applied to software development in the form of system thinking and holistic optimization. By considering the entire system as a whole, rather than individual components, developers can identify and eliminate inefficiencies, reducing complexity and increasing overall performance. This mindset shift can be seen in practices like microservices architecture, where the entire system is designed to work together to achieve a common goal, rather than individual services being optimized in isolation. By adopting this approach, developers can create more robust, scalable, and maintainable software systems.
