### TIL: Resilience and Automation in the Face of Human Extinction
Today I came across a sci-fi short story by Ray Bradbury, "There Will Come Soft Rains", which eerily depicts a world where humanity has been wiped out, yet technology persists, operating with eerie precision. This haunting tale sparks a thought experiment: what would happen to our own systems and infrastructure if human extinction were to occur? As a developer, I realize that we often design systems for resilience and fault tolerance, but rarely consider the long-term sustainability of our creations in a world devoid of human intervention. This exercise in imagination reminds me to think critically about the potential consequences of our code and the importance of considering long-term maintenance and evolution of our systems, especially when incorporating automation and AI.
