# TIL: Xbox goes down. You can't play games you own on disc

_2026-08-04 12:24 UTC_

[Original Source](https://birchtree.me/blog/xbox-goes-down-you-cant-play-games-you-own-on-disc/)

**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL that the Xbox outage incident highlights the fragility of digital game ownership and the importance of considering content delivery mechanisms in software architecture. The fact that users were unable to play physical games due to a server-side issue demonstrates the potential risks of relying on cloud-based authentication and game delivery systems, where a single point of failure can impact the entire user experience. This serves as a reminder to prioritize robust and resilient design, considering alternatives such as local authentication, offline play modes, and content caching to ensure a more reliable and seamless experience for users.
