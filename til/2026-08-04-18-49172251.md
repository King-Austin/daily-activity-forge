### TIL: Vlt 1.0 and Hosted Package Registries

TIL about Vlt 1.0, a new package registry that's been gaining traction on Hacker News. From what I gather, Vlt is a hosted registry that allows developers to host their own packages and dependencies, reducing the need for third-party registries like npm or PyPI. This has implications for dependency management and security, as developers can now have more control over their dependencies and ensure they're not relying on outdated or malicious packages. It's also an interesting development in the package registry space, which could potentially disrupt the established players. I'll be keeping an eye on Vlt and its implications for the dev community.
