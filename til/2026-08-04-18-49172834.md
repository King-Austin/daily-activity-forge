### TIL: Security is Hard and Ephemeral

TIL that the concept of security is inherently fragile and susceptible to exploitation due to its reliance on human error and the limitations of current technology. The article discusses the idea that security is not just about implementing the latest security protocols, but also about understanding the context and nuances of the systems we're trying to protect. This realization highlights the importance of continuous learning, vigilance, and a willingness to revise and refine our security strategies as new threats and vulnerabilities emerge. As a developer, this serves as a reminder to prioritize security in all aspects of our work, from code review and testing to deployment and maintenance, and to stay up-to-date with the latest developments in the field.
