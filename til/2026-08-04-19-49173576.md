**Today I Learned**
===============

TIL that the recent downfall of situational awareness startups highlights the importance of **separation of concerns** in software design. These companies' attempts to integrate multiple, unrelated data feeds into a single platform ultimately led to their demise. This serves as a reminder to prioritize **modularity** and **interfaces** when building complex systems, allowing for easier maintenance, updates, and scalability. By encapsulating distinct components and providing well-defined APIs, developers can avoid the pitfalls of tightly coupled, monolithic architectures that are prone to catastrophic failures.
