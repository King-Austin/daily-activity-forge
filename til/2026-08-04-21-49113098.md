# TIL: Ever-Bigger Cars Pose Interesting Problems for Software Developers

I came across an article about the increasing size of modern cars, with a remarkable trend of growing 1.2cm longer and 0.5cm taller each year. As a developer, this got me thinking about the implications for automotive software systems, particularly those related to safety and collision avoidance. For instance, advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) like lane departure warning and automatic emergency braking rely heavily on accurate sensor data and mapping of the vehicle's surroundings. The growing size of cars could lead to increased computational complexities, as software developers will need to account for the changing geometry and sensor ranges to maintain reliable performance and prevent false alarms or missed warnings. This highlights the importance of considering the interplay between hardware and software in automotive development, and the need for developers to stay up-to-date with the evolving landscape of vehicle design and technology.
