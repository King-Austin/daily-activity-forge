### TIL: Centering a Div is Not a Fixed Problem

TIL that centering a `div` element, a long-standing CSS challenge, is not a solved problem due to browser quirks. The article highlights how modern browsers, in an effort to improve accessibility and usability, have introduced sidebars and other features that can disrupt traditional centering methods. This gotcha serves as a reminder that CSS is not just about styling, but also about understanding the nuances of browser behavior and being prepared to adapt to changing browser features. As a developer, I'll need to stay vigilant and consider the potential impact of evolving browser features on my CSS solutions.
