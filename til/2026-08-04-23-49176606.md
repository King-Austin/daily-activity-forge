# TIL: libexpat now funded by the City of Munich for up to 6 months

_2026-08-04 23:41 UTC_

[Original Source](https://blog.hartwork.org/posts/libexpat-city-of-munich-open-source-sabbatical/)

### Today I Learned

TIL that the City of Munich is funding libexpat, an open-source XML parser, for up to 6 months, allowing its maintainer to take an open-source sabbatical. This news has significant implications for software developers relying on libexpat, as it reduces the risk of project abandonment and ensures the library's continued maintenance and improvement. The funding model could serve as a blueprint for supporting other open-source projects, potentially leading to more sustainable and reliable dependencies in our software ecosystems.
