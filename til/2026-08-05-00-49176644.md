**TIL: Potential Risks in Passwordless Authentication**

I just came across a Unit 42 article highlighting a novel attack surface in passwordless authentication - 'Pass the Passkey' (PTP). In a nutshell, PTP exploits the use of passkeys in passwordless authentication systems, where an attacker can intercept and reuse a passkey to gain unauthorized access. The key takeaway is that relying solely on passkeys without proper validation and secure storage can be just as vulnerable as traditional password-based systems. This got me thinking about the importance of ensuring secure key management, validating passkey integrity, and implementing additional authentication factors to prevent such attacks. I'll be exploring ways to integrate these security considerations into our passwordless authentication implementation to ensure the robustness of our system.
