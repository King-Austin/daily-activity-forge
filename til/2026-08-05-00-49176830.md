# TIL: Eight Myths on Software Engineering and GenAI

_2026-08-05 00:41 UTC_

[Original Source](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3807963)

**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL about the potential pitfalls of adopting GenAI in software engineering, as highlighted in the article "Eight Myths on Software Engineering and GenAI". One key takeaway is the risk of **over-reliance on AI-generated code**, which can lead to a loss of understanding of the underlying technology and reduced ability to debug and maintain complex systems. This has implications for the importance of **human oversight and code review**, ensuring that AI-generated code is thoroughly evaluated and validated before deployment. Additionally, the article emphasizes the need for **contextual understanding** of GenAI models, highlighting the limitations of these systems in understanding nuances and edge cases. I'll be keeping these considerations in mind when exploring the potential applications of GenAI in my future projects.
