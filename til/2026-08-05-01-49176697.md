# TIL: IP and DNS Leaks in WebKit Affecting Proxy Browsers and iCloud Private Relay

_2026-08-05 01:41 UTC_

[Original Source](https://mysk.blog/2026/08/04/webkit-proxy-icloud-private-relay-ip-leak/)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that a recent WebKit vulnerability, affecting proxy browsers and iCloud Private Relay, can lead to IP and DNS leaks. This discovery highlights the importance of thoroughly testing and auditing our implementation of private browsing features, particularly when relying on third-party libraries or frameworks like WebKit. Furthermore, it underscores the need to consider the potential interactions between our application's networking stack and external services like iCloud Private Relay, which may introduce unforeseen security risks. This serves as a reminder to carefully review and validate the security implications of our technology choices, especially when ensuring user privacy and security is a top priority.
