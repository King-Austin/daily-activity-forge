**TIL:** "A recent article on 'The Golden Age of British Ice Cream' caught my attention, particularly the discussion on traditional ice cream vendors' use of hand-cranked ice cream makers. This nostalgic approach to ice cream production relies on an age-old mechanism leveraging thermodynamics and mechanical advantage to freeze and aerate mixtures, a process that can be likened to the efficient use of caching in software development. By preloading and pre-processing data (in this case, the ice cream mixture) before the main computation (freezing and aerating), the hand-cranked ice cream maker can achieve near-optimal results with minimal computational overhead, a principle that can be applied to optimize caching strategies in our own codebases."
