**TIL: Ancient Pneumatic Systems Can Inform Modern Automation**

I came across an article on Hacker News highlighting the works of Hero of Alexandria, an ancient Greek mathematician and engineer who described intricate pneumatic systems in his book 'Pneumatica'. While this may seem unrelated to software development at first glance, I was struck by the parallels between Hero's designs and modern automation systems. His use of valve networks, fluidic logic gates, and feedback loops to control the flow of air and water in his machines can be seen as precursors to the digital control systems used in modern robotics and IoT devices. This got me thinking about how understanding the fundamental principles of fluid dynamics and control systems could inform the design of more efficient and scalable automation architectures.
