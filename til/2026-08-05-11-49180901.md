### TIL: Community Resistance to Data Center Expansion

Today I learned that the recent trend of community resistance to data center expansion, as highlighted in the article "No Data Centers in My Backyard", may have significant implications for software developers. The article suggests that data center operators are facing increased scrutiny and pushback from local communities, driven by concerns over power consumption, land usage, and environmental impact. As a developer, this could lead to increased costs and complexity associated with data center operations, potentially driving innovation in areas like edge computing, green data centers, and more resilient supply chains.
