**TIL: Limitations of LLMs**

I came across the "Position: LLMs Can't Jump" paper, which highlights the potential limitations of large language models (LLMs) in understanding and generating text that requires common sense, context, and nuanced understanding of the physical world. The paper presents evidence that LLMs struggle to generate coherent and accurate descriptions of physical scenarios, such as a ball bouncing, or even simple tasks like predicting the outcome of a jump. This got me thinking about the potential implications for software development: while LLMs are incredibly powerful for tasks like code completion, text summarization, and content generation, their limitations in understanding the physical world or complex scenarios might necessitate the development of more specialized models or hybrid approaches that combine the strengths of LLMs with other AI techniques, such as computer vision or physics engines. This is an important reminder to carefully evaluate the capabilities and limitations of LLMs when designing AI-powered software systems.
