# TIL: Civilian plane crash in New Mexico tied to military GPS blocking

_2026-08-05 11:27 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.wired.com/story/a-civilian-plane-crashed-in-new-mexico-was-the-militarys-tech-to-blame/)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that the US military's GPS jamming technology may have contributed to a recent civilian plane crash in New Mexico. From a technical perspective, this incident highlights the potential risks and unintended consequences of jamming or interfering with critical infrastructure like GPS systems. As a developer working with geolocation and navigation APIs, this serves as a sobering reminder of the importance of considering edge cases and fallback mechanisms in our designs, such as using alternative positioning methods (e.g., cellular-based locationing) or implementing redundancy in critical systems.
