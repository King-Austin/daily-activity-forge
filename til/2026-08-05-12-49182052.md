# TIL: Iowa asks OpenAI to keep their bots sandboxed

_2026-08-05 12:57 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.iowaattorneygeneral.gov/newsroom/attorney-general-brenna-bird-leads-coalition-demanding-transparency-from-openai-after-ai-breach-and)

**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL that the Iowa Attorney General's request for OpenAI to keep their bots sandboxed suggests concerns around AI model deployment and potential risk to users. This news highlights the need for developers to consider implementing robust isolation and testing strategies for AI/ML models to prevent unintended consequences. The concept of sandboxing in this context implies a clear distinction between the model's development, testing, and production environments, helping to prevent uncontrolled or untested AI behavior from impacting end-users. As developers, it's essential to prioritize model testing, validation, and isolation techniques to mitigate potential risks associated with AI model deployment.
