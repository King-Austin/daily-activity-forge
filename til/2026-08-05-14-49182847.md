### TIL: Critical Vulnerabilities in Modern Web Applications

TIL that the 'A Sick People' article highlights critical vulnerabilities in modern web applications, including the abuse of `tel:` and `mailto:` links to inject malicious payloads. As a developer, I realize that even seemingly innocuous features like link preview and click-to-email functionality can be exploited by attackers. This vulnerability is particularly concerning given the widespread adoption of modern web frameworks and the reliance on third-party libraries, which may not always be patched or vetted thoroughly. To mitigate such risks, I'll be reviewing my project's link handling and email integration components to ensure they adhere to secure coding practices and handle potential edge cases effectively.
