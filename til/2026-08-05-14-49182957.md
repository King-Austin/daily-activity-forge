**TIL: The Consequences of Ignoring Junior Talent**

I came across a thought-provoking article on Hacker News arguing that not hiring junior engineers is not a viable solution to address the industry's challenges. While this may seem counterintuitive, it highlights the importance of cultivating a diverse and inclusive talent pool. As a developer, I realize that junior engineers bring a unique set of skills and perspectives, including a willingness to learn and take risks, which are essential for driving innovation and tackling complex problems. By ignoring junior talent, companies risk stagnating their ability to adapt to changing technologies and market demands, ultimately hindering their competitiveness in the long run.
