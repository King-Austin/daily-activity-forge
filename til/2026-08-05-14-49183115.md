### TIL: Aristotle's Philosophy on Virtue, Knowledge, and Happiness

While reading through Aristotle's quotes on virtue, knowledge, and happiness, I came across a few insights that resonated with my approach to software development. Specifically, Aristotle's notion that "we are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act, but a habit" ([1](https://www.campion.edu.au/blog/top-25-aristotle-quotes-on-virtue-knowledge-and-happiness/)) reminded me of the importance of consistently writing clean, well-documented code. It's not a one-time task, but a habit that requires dedication and practice. This quote also echoes the concept of code review and continuous improvement in agile development methodologies. Furthermore, Aristotle's emphasis on knowledge as a means to achieve happiness ([2](https://www.campion.edu.au/blog/top-25-aristotle-quotes-on-virtue-knowledge-and-happiness/)) resonates with my personal goal of staying up-to-date with the latest technologies and best practices in software development. I believe that by cultivating a habit of continuous learning and improvement, I can not only write better code but also increase my overall job satisfaction and well-being.
