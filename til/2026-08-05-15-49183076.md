**TIL: Intelligence Is Not the Main Bottleneck**

I came across an insightful article on Hacker News, "Intelligence Is Not the Main Bottleneck", where the author makes a compelling case that our brains are more capable than we think, and that it's often our habits and environments that hold us back from realizing our full potential. As a software developer, I took away the importance of recognizing that our technical limitations are often self-imposed. This realization led me to re-evaluate my own workflow and identify areas where I can optimize my productivity by adopting better habits, such as breaking down complex tasks into smaller, manageable chunks, practicing focused coding sessions, and leveraging tools and automation to streamline repetitive tasks. By acknowledging that intelligence is not the main bottleneck, I aim to shift my focus from "I'm not smart enough" to "I need to work smarter" and make incremental improvements to my development process.
