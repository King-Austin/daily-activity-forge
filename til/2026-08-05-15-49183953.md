# TIL: Coordinated Attacks on Individuals Can Involve Multiple Parties and Exploit Weaknesses in Identity Verification

TIL that a coordinated attack involving a major bank (Citigroup), a government agency (Idaho), and a retail store (Build-a-Bear) was launched against an individual. This incident highlights the need for developers to consider the potential for multiple parties to collaborate on attacks, especially when it comes to identity verification and authentication systems. This scenario underscores the importance of designing robust systems that can withstand coordinated attacks and verifying identities through multiple factors, rather than relying on single-point authentication methods.
