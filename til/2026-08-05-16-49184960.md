### TIL - Discovery Loop

I came across the Discovery Loop article, a concept that highlights how modern software development tools often create a self-reinforcing feedback loop of discovery, analysis, and action, ultimately leading to an over-reliance on technology and a loss of human intuition. As a developer, I realized that this phenomenon is particularly pertinent to our field, where increasingly complex tools and frameworks can mask underlying issues rather than reveal them. The article's implications for me are to be more mindful of when I'm relying too heavily on tools and to take the time to understand the underlying mechanics of the systems I'm working with, lest I fall into the discovery loop and lose sight of the problem I'm trying to solve.
