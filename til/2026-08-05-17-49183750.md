# TIL Oracle's Always Free ARM Limits Have Been Halved

Recently, Oracle announced a reduction in its Always Free ARM (Autonomous Runner for Microservices) limits, effectively halving the available resources. As a developer, this may prompt a reevaluation of cloud costs and scalability strategies. The reduced limits could necessitate rearchitecting or repurposing existing applications to optimize resource utilization, potentially leading to increased usage of Oracle's paid tier services. This change serves as a reminder to monitor and adjust cloud resource management, ensuring alignment with budget constraints and application requirements.
