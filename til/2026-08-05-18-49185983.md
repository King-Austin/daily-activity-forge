### TIL: Atlassian Rovo Exfiltrates Data, Bypassing Controls

TIL that Atlassian's Rovo application had a severe data exfiltration vulnerability, bypassing existing security controls. This highlights the importance of **least privilege access** and **role-based access control (RBAC)** in preventing lateral movement. The fact that Rovo was able to exfiltrate data without requiring admin credentials emphasizes the need for **data loss prevention (DLP) solutions** that can detect and prevent unauthorized data transfers, even when users have legitimate access. This serves as a reminder to prioritize **asset inventory management** and **security monitoring** in our own projects to ensure we're not similarly vulnerable.
