I learned that researchers at Neon were able to beat a $100M+ GPT-5.6 Sol model on retrieval tasks using a significantly cheaper open model, CastForm, which suggests that the efficiency and cost-effectiveness of open models are rapidly improving. This implies that developers may no longer need to rely on expensive, large-scale models to achieve comparable results, potentially democratizing access to AI capabilities and reducing the barrier to entry for many applications.
