# TIL: The Title Cards in Blade Runner Are Fucking Amazing

_2026-08-05 22:59 UTC_

[Original Source](https://randsinrepose.com/archives/blade-runner-title-cards/)

### Today I Learned

TIL about the innovative title card design in Blade Runner (1982) by Douglas Trumbull, which uses a combination of analog and digital techniques to produce a mesmerizing effect. The article highlights how Trumbull's team employed a technique called "optical compositing" to create the title cards, which involves manually layering and adjusting film elements using an optical printer. As a developer, I find this technique fascinating because it predates modern digital compositing tools like Adobe After Effects. The emphasis on manual, iterative processes in the title card creation process reminds me of the value of "human in the loop" design and the importance of considering the limitations and strengths of analog techniques in the pursuit of creative expression.
