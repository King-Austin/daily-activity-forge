# TIL: LLMs won't break symmetric crypto

_2026-08-06 02:14 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.bfswa.blog/p/llms-wont-break-symmetric-crypto)

### TIL

TIL that LLMs (Large Language Models) are unlikely to break symmetric crypto due to their linguistic nature and the fundamental security properties of symmetric encryption. This insight is based on the argument that an LLM, no matter how advanced, will eventually rely on linguistic patterns and probabilities to generate potential keys or attacks, which symmetric encryption's random key space and avalanche effect can effectively mitigate. This conclusion has significant implications for the development of secure systems, as it suggests that symmetric crypto remains a reliable choice for protecting sensitive data, even in the face of increasingly powerful AI adversaries.
