### TIL: Eminent Domain and Data Center Relocation Implications

TIL that the city of Nashville used eminent domain to block a data center near a zoo. The implications of this event for software developers could be significant, particularly in the realm of disaster recovery and business continuity planning. If a data center is halted or relocated due to unforeseen circumstances, the ripple effects on dependent systems and applications could be substantial, requiring rapid replanning and redeployment of infrastructure. This serves as a reminder of the importance of having a well-defined disaster recovery plan, including considerations for data center relocation, to minimize downtime and ensure business continuity in the face of unexpected disruptions.
