**TIL (2023-08-06)**

I came across an article on making a Nintendo 64 game in 2026, which highlights the work of Andreas Gustafsson's Xibalba64 project. TIL that it's technically possible to create a 64-bit N64 game using modern C and a custom emulator, leveraging the Linux kernel and a modified version of the N64's RSP (RISC Processor) to achieve near-native performance. This has implications for our understanding of emulator development and the potential for retro-style game development on modern platforms, demonstrating that even decades-old console architectures can be reverse-engineered and utilized with contemporary tools.
