**TIL: Potential for Y2K-Style Vulnerability in Modern Systems**

Today I read about the "Y2K" meetup initiative, a tongue-in-cheek call to test the resilience of modern systems in the face of a widespread clock rollover from 99 to 00. While the article is lighthearted, it highlights a legitimate concern about the potential for Y2K-style vulnerabilities in modern codebases. As a developer, I'm reminded to review our system's date-handling routines, especially those involving time-sensitive operations like payment processing, scheduling, and security token expiration. It's also a good opportunity to verify our code's compliance with modern standards and best practices for handling date and time data, ensuring that we don't inadvertently create a ticking time bomb that could cause issues when the clock strikes 00:00:00 on January 1, 2030.
