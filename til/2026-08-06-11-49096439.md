### TIL - Pareto Front Optimization

TIL that the Pareto Front is a concept in multi-objective optimization that can be leveraged in software development for decision-making and trade-off analysis. In essence, it represents the optimal trade-off between two or more conflicting objectives, where further improvements in one objective necessarily degrade the others. This principle can be applied to scenarios such as performance vs. memory usage, code complexity vs. maintainability, or even feature addition vs. resource allocation, allowing developers to identify the optimal balance between competing priorities and make data-driven decisions.
