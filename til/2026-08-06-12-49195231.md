### TIL: The Pareto Principle in Software Development

TIL about the Pareto Principle in the context of the classic Mario game from [Mario Meets Pareto](https://www.mayerowitz.io/blog/mario-meets-pareto). The article presents an interesting analysis of the game's level design and how it inadvertently demonstrates the 80/20 rule, where 20% of the level's content (power-ups and coins) contribute to 80% of the player's score. This observation has implications for software development, where understanding the Pareto Principle can help developers focus on the most critical features and functionality that provide the greatest returns on investment. By identifying and optimizing these high-impact areas, developers can create more efficient and effective software solutions, and allocate resources more wisely.
