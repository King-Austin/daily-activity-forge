### TIL: ADB Uninstall Fails on Non-Rooted Android 17

**Context**: Reading the Hacker News article on GitHub issue #1426, I discovered that on non-rooted Android 17 devices, ADB (Android Debug Bridge) uninstall of system apps fails due to a permission issue. **Implication**: This means that any software or script relying on ADB to uninstall system apps on such devices will encounter errors, potentially causing unexpected behavior or crashes. **Takeaway**: Developers should be aware of this limitation when targeting non-rooted devices with Android 17, and consider alternative approaches or workarounds, such as using the `pm uninstall` command instead of ADB, or exploring device-specific APIs. This highlights the importance of testing and validating software on various Android versions and configurations to ensure robustness and reliability.
