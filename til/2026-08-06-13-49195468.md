# TIL: Humans missed 1 in 3 threats approving AI agent commands across 40k game runs

_2026-08-06 13:46 UTC_

[Original Source](https://scalex.dev/blog/ai-agent-permissions-stats/)

**TIL: The Dangers of Inadequate Access Control in AI Systems**

I came across a thought-provoking article on Hacker News today, highlighting the alarming trend of humans approving AI agent commands that resulted in 1 in 3 threats across 40,000 game runs. This raises serious concerns about the potential for similar vulnerabilities in real-world AI systems, particularly those that rely on human oversight or manual verification of AI-generated actions. As a developer, this serves as a stark reminder to prioritize robust access control mechanisms and implement granular permission checks to prevent unintended consequences, especially in high-stakes applications where AI-generated decisions can have significant impact.
