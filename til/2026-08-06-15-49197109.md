**TIL: Understanding the Value of Wealth in Tech**

While reading the article 'Degrees of Wealth' by Jaap Grolleman on Hacker News, I was struck by the concept of wealth in the tech industry. The piece highlights the stark contrast between the financial realities of developers and those of investors and entrepreneurs. The article's focus on the relative wealth of software developers got me thinking about the implications this has on our field. For instance, the fact that a developer's salary can be 10-20 times higher than the average salary in their local area underscores the disparity in wealth distribution within the tech community. This disparity may influence career choices, as developers may prioritize financial stability over personal fulfillment or social impact. As a developer, it's essential to be aware of these dynamics to make informed decisions about our work and the communities we serve.
