### TIL: Analogies in Complex Systems

Today I learned that the process of cooking a steak can be surprisingly analogous to optimizing software systems. The article "Almost no skill required to cook a steak" highlights the importance of understanding the underlying physics and chemistry involved in searing a steak, which can be seen as a complex system with non-linear interactions. Similarly, in software development, complex systems often exhibit emergent behavior that can be challenging to predict or control. By recognizing these analogies and applying the principles of understanding underlying dynamics, we can develop more effective strategies for debugging, testing, and optimizing our software systems, just as a skilled cook applies their knowledge of physics and chemistry to achieve the perfect steak.
