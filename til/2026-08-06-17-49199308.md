### TIL: Cities Migrating from Flock to Axon License Plate Readers

TIL that cities are rapidly replacing Flock, a crowdsourced surveillance platform, with Axon's license plate readers, which have the capability to store and analyze video footage alongside plate data. This shift implies that cities may prioritize a more direct, centralized approach to surveillance, potentially increasing the reliance on proprietary software and hardware. As a developer, I'm interested in understanding the technical differences between these systems, particularly how Axon's solutions handle data storage, processing, and retention in comparison to Flock's decentralized model.
