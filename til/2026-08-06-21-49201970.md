### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that AMD's acquisition of Taalas marks a significant shift towards hardware-accelerated machine learning inference. The proposed technique of etching models directly into silicon could lead to substantial performance boosts for AI workloads. This development suggests that the boundaries between software and hardware are blurring, and we may soon see a surge in the adoption of hardware-specific AI frameworks and compiler optimizations that can take advantage of this new paradigm. As a developer, this means we'll need to consider the possibilities and limitations of this technology in our own projects, and potentially adapt our architectures to take advantage of hardware-accelerated AI capabilities.
