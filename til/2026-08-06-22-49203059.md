_2026-08-06 22:47 UTC_

[Original Source](https://github.com/mattdeeds/pokeemerald-rp2350)

### Today I Learned

TIL that the Pokémon Emerald game has been successfully ported to the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 using RP2x35 chip, demonstrating the feasibility of emulating complex systems on low-power, embedded devices. This achievement highlights the potential of modern microcontrollers and their ability to run demanding applications, potentially paving the way for the development of embedded systems that can execute full-fledged games or even operating systems. The technical implications of this porting project are significant, as they demonstrate the effectiveness of modern toolchains, such as the RP2x35 chip's support for C/C++ development, and the potential for leveraging these technologies in novel applications.
