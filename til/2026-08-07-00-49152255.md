# TIL - USB-C Cable Labeling May Not Be Reliable
When verifying the reliability of USB-C cable labeling, I learned that the labels may not accurately represent the cable's capabilities. In a recent article, the author used a USB probe meter to test a selection of USB-C cables, discovering that many cables claiming to support Power Delivery (PD) actually did not. This finding highlights the importance of verifying technical specifications through direct testing rather than solely relying on labeling or marketing claims. As a developer, this serves as a reminder to be cautious when integrating third-party components or cables into our projects, and to consider implementing validation checks to ensure that components meet their advertised capabilities.
