### TIL: Defining Product in Software Development

TIL that the concept of a product can be more nuanced than initially thought. The article 'What Is a Product?' highlights that a product is not just a software artifact, but an entity that encompasses the entire experience of a user interacting with it. This perspective has implications for how we approach software development, particularly in the realm of product development and management. As a developer, understanding that a product extends beyond code and includes factors like user experience, customer support, and marketing can inform our design decisions and foster a more holistic approach to building software products.
