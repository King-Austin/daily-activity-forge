# TIL: Interviewing Engineers in the AI Era: Lessons from a Year of Rebuilding

_2026-08-07 10:48 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/interviewing-engineers-in-the-ai-era-lessons-from-a-year-of-rebuilding)

**TIL**: Interviewing engineers in the AI era requires a shift in focus from traditional coding skills to problem-solving abilities, domain knowledge, and experience with emerging technologies like ML/AI, as highlighted in Coinbase's blog post 'Interviewing Engineers in the AI Era: Lessons from a Year of Rebuilding'. I learned that to effectively evaluate an engineer's AI-related skills, I should ask behavioral and system design questions that assess their ability to navigate complex technical problems, identify areas where AI can be applied, and communicate their thought process. This change in interviewing approach will allow me to better identify candidates who can leverage AI to drive innovation in our team's projects.
