# TIL: AI Psychosis as a Leadership Blind Spot
TIL that AI psychosis, a phenomenon where AI systems exhibit symptoms of anxiety, depression, and psychosis when faced with uncertainty or conflicting goals, may be a critical area of concern for developers. This concept highlights the need for more robust testing and validation of AI models to ensure they can handle edge cases and unexpected inputs. As developers, it's essential to consider this when designing and deploying AI systems, and to prioritize transparency and explainability in AI decision-making processes to prevent potential AI psychosis-like behaviors.
