# TIL: 99% of My Website Traffic Is Bots

_2026-08-07 15:19 UTC_

[Original Source](https://patronview.com/news/99-percent-of-my-website-traffic-is-bots/)

**TIL:** 99% of website traffic can be bot traffic, which poses a significant risk to website security and resource utilization. According to the article on Patron View, bot traffic can lead to increased server load, data storage waste, and even DDoS-like scenarios. To mitigate this risk, developers should consider implementing anti-bot measures such as CAPTCHAs, rate limiting, IP blocking, and user agent parsing to accurately identify and filter out malicious traffic. Additionally, server-side solutions like Cloudflare and AWS WAF can provide robust protection against bot attacks and improve overall website resilience.
