# Today I Learned (TIL)
## An All-Sky Map of Half a Million Supermassive Black Holes

TIL that astronomical imaging and data processing techniques are being leveraged to create an unprecedented catalog of 580,000 supermassive black holes across the observable universe. The [Black Hole Mapper](https://www.sdss.org/black-hole-mapper-release-20/) project relies on machine learning algorithms, computer vision, and large-scale data processing to identify and classify black hole candidates from surveys like the Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS). This development highlights the potential for analogous techniques in the field of astronomy to be adapted for other complex data processing tasks, such as object detection in computer vision or anomaly detection in cybersecurity applications.
