### TIL: Economic Theory Implications for Systems Design

TIL that tax policies targeting the wealthy often disproportionately benefit the rich, debunking the notion of trickle-down economics. From a system design perspective, this highlights the potential for economic inequality to exacerbate in systems where wealth concentration is a key factor. For example, in designing a cryptocurrency or social network, we should consider the potential for wealth concentration to lead to unequal distribution of resources, influence, or power. This might inform the implementation of mechanisms like progressive tax systems, wealth redistribution protocols, or robust governance models to mitigate such effects and promote a more equitable outcome.
