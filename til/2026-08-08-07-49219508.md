### Hardware Backdoors in x86 CPUs

TIL that the `Rosenbridge` proof-of-concept by xoreaxeaxeax demonstrates a method for exploiting a potential hardware backdoor in some x86 CPUs. This raises concerns for developers working in fields involving high-security or sensitive data, as it highlights the possibility of a malicious actor controlling system behavior through undocumented CPU instructions. The technical takeaway is that developers should be cautious when using CPU-specific features or relying on the security of CPU-based primitives, such as random number generators or cryptographic accelerators, as they may be vulnerable to these types of backdoors. This serves as a reminder to always consider the potential for hardware-based attacks when designing secure systems.
