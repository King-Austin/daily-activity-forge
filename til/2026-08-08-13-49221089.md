### The CPU-GPU Split is Under Review for LLM Inference

I came across an interesting article on Red Hat's blog discussing the reevaluation of the CPU-GPU split for Large Language Model (LLM) inference. It highlights the resurgence of CPU performance, driven by advancements in silicon design, and the increasing efficiency of CPU architectures. This shift has significant implications for LLM inference workloads, which may no longer be relegated solely to GPUs. As a developer, this means we may need to revisit our choice of hardware and consider using CPUs for certain LLM inference tasks, potentially improving performance, power efficiency, and cost-effectiveness. I'll keep an eye on this development and explore the feasibility of CPU-based LLM inference in our projects.
