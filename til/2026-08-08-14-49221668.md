### Today I Learned (TIL)

#### [2023-12-01] Domain for Sale Indicator in DNS

TIL that a new DNS specification (draft-ietf-dnsop-fs-dns-04) allows domains to indicate they are for sale by publishing a specific record (`FS` or "For Sale" record). This development has implications for domain name management, as registrars and registrants can now signal to interested parties when a domain is available for purchase. As a developer, this change may impact my work in the following ways: (1) I'll need to consider the potential for domain squatting or speculation when developing applications that rely on domain name resolution; (2) I may need to update DNS-related code to handle this new record type; (3) This change could also lead to new opportunities for developing innovative solutions that take advantage of this feature. I'll keep an eye on the evolution of this specification and its adoption in the industry.
