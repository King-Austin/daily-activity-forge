### TIL: Gateway 2000's Ad Missteps Reveal the Importance of User Experience and Technical Understanding

TIL that the hilariously bad advertising efforts of Gateway 2000 in the 90s were not only comedic but also a reflection of the company's lack of understanding of user needs and technical capabilities. This serves as a reminder that even in the early days of computing, user experience and technical expertise were crucial factors in a product's success. As a software developer, this highlights the importance of considering the target audience and their potential pain points when designing and marketing software, as well as the need to ensure that the product's technical capabilities are accurately represented to avoid confusing or misleading potential customers.
