# TIL: Fastmail offers EU data region

_2026-08-08 17:36 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.fastmail.com/blog/fastmail-offers-eu-data-region/)

# Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that Fastmail, an email service provider, is now offering an EU data region, which means user data will be stored within the European Union. This development has implications for software developers working with EU data, particularly those building applications that involve email services or require compliance with GDPR regulations. It highlights the importance of data sovereignty and regional data hosting for EU-based users, and it may be worth considering alternative email providers or data storage solutions that cater to specific regional requirements when designing or integrating external services into our applications.
