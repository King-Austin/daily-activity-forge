### TIL: LinkedIn Feed Blocker Implications

TIL that a user has created a browser extension, `linkedin-feed-blocker`, to bypass LinkedIn's algorithm-driven feed by loading the full list of connections and their posts. This implementation leverages the `chrome.tabs.executeScript` API to inject a custom script into a LinkedIn tab, effectively circumventing the platform's content restrictions. This hack demonstrates the power of browser extensions and web automation, highlighting the limitations of client-side content filtering and the importance of considering user experience when designing data-driven feeds. It also underscores the potential for users to bypass platform restrictions, emphasizing the need for developers to balance user experience with content moderation and algorithmic control.
