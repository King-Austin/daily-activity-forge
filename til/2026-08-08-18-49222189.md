# TIL: "Code was never the hard part" is an insult to all programmers

_2026-08-08 18:21 UTC_

[Original Source](https://blog.senko.net/code-was-never-the-hard-part-is-an-insult-to-all-programmers)

### TIL: Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving in Programming

TIL that the phrase "Code was never the hard part" underestimates the complexity and nuance involved in programming, particularly in tackling real-world problems. This mindset can be detrimental as it overlooks the intricacies of software design, debugging, and optimization. In reality, programming is about crafting solutions that balance competing constraints, such as performance, maintainability, and scalability. A more accurate perspective might be that "code is just the beginning," emphasizing the importance of critical thinking, problem-solving, and collaboration in delivering high-quality software. This shift in perspective can lead to more effective communication with stakeholders, better project planning, and ultimately, higher-quality software.
