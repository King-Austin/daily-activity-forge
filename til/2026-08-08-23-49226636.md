### TIL: Phone as a Server

TIL that a server can be repurposed into a phone, thanks to advancements in cloud computing and software-defined networking. The article showcases a clever implementation where a server is converted into a phone by using a VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol) stack and a web-based interface to manage call setup and teardown. This concept highlights the blurring of lines between physical devices and their software-defined counterparts, forcing us to rethink our assumptions about hardware requirements and infrastructure design. As a developer, I see opportunities to apply this thinking to other areas, such as repurposing underutilized resources or exploring novel uses for existing infrastructure.
