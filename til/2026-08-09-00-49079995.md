**TIL: Differential Heuristics Improve A\* Pathfinding Efficiency**
===============

TIL that differential heuristics can significantly enhance the performance of A\* pathfinding algorithms. According to the article on Red Blob Games, traditional heuristics like Manhattan distance and Euclidean distance can lead to suboptimal results, especially in scenarios with obstacles and complex terrain. By using a differential heuristic, which measures the difference in the distance to each node, we can converge to the optimal solution more efficiently. This technique has the potential to improve the performance of navigation and pathfinding systems in games, autonomous vehicles, and other applications, making it a valuable optimization technique for developers to consider.
