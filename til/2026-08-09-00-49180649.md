### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL about the concept of difficulty curves in games and their application to software development. According to the article "Making Difficulty Curves in Games" by Dave Turner, a well-designed difficulty curve can enhance player engagement and satisfaction by providing a sense of progression and challenge. This insight can be applied to software development by considering the concept of "difficulty curves" in the context of user onboarding, error handling, and feature adoption. By strategically introducing increasing levels of complexity and challenge, developers can create a more engaging and intuitive user experience, making complex systems feel more approachable and rewarding to use. This can be achieved through techniques such as gradual feature rollout, adaptive error messaging, and skill-based progression.
