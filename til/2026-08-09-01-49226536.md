# TIL: Shopify replaced Redis with MySQL for inventory reservations–and it scaled

_2026-08-09 01:22 UTC_

[Original Source](https://shopify.engineering/scaling-inventory-reservations)

**TIL: Avoiding Early Optimization Pitfalls**

TIL that Shopify's decision to replace Redis with MySQL for inventory reservations demonstrates the importance of iterating on database schema design, even in high-traffic systems. The realization that inventory reservations can be efficiently managed using MySQL, rather than relying on a separate caching layer like Redis, highlights the potential for premature optimization. This anecdote serves as a reminder to focus on understanding the fundamental requirements of an application before investing in complex caching or NoSQL solutions, and to regularly re-evaluate assumptions about system performance.
