**TIL: Dithered QR Codes and Error Correction**

I came across an interesting article on Hacker News discussing 'dithered QR codes': a technique that intentionally introduces pixelation to QR code images, leveraging the human visual system to maintain readability. This approach has implications for error correction in QR code generation. Traditionally, QR code generators aim to minimize distortion, but dithering exploits the tolerance of the human eye to imperfections. By applying error correction methods to the dithered output, developers can potentially create more resilient QR codes with increased robustness to degradation and noise, making them more suitable for use cases where image quality may be compromised.
