**TIL: The Impermanence of URLs**

I came across an article from 2011 where a prediction was made that the original URL for the prediction would no longer be available in 11 years. Visiting the URL https://longbets.org/601/, I was reminded of the importance of URL persistence and the potential implications for software developers. This highlights the need to consider URL longevity when designing APIs, web services, or even data storage, as URLs can become inaccessible due to various reasons such as domain expiration, server migration, or even a simple change in URL structure. This got me thinking about strategies for mitigating this issue, such as using UUIDs or hashes for unique identifiers, and implementing redirects or canonical URLs to ensure that resources remain accessible even when their original URLs are no longer valid.
