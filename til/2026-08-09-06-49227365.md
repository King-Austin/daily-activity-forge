# TIL: Melatonin impairs morning cognition in healthy young adults

_2026-08-09 06:37 UTC_

[Original Source](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article/46/Supplement_1/A34/7181621)

# Today I Learned
TIL that melatonin, a common sleep aid, can impair morning cognition in healthy young adults. This finding has implications for software development teams that work non-traditional hours or across time zones, as it suggests that melatonin use may negatively impact productivity and cognitive performance during the critical morning hours when most coding and problem-solving tasks are tackled. As a developer, I'll consider this when planning team schedules and consider suggesting alternative sleep aids or strategies to mitigate potential melatonin-related cognitive impairment during critical work periods.
