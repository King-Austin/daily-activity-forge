### TIL: Alpha 21264 CPU Design Implications

TIL that the Alpha 21264 CPU, released in 1998, was a revolutionary 64-bit RISC processor designed by Digital Equipment Corporation (DEC). One technical takeaway is that this CPU's use of a load/store architecture with a unified instruction set (EVL - Execute, Vector, and Load/Store) highlights the potential benefits of decoupling memory access from execution, which can lead to improved performance and reduced power consumption in modern CPU designs. This design choice also underscores the importance of optimizing memory access patterns, a consideration that remains relevant in software development today, particularly in systems programming and low-level optimizations.
