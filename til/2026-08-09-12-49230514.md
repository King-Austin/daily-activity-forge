### TIL ReMarkable 2 SSH Reverse Engineering

TIL that it's possible to revive a four-year-old reMarkable 2 e-reader by reverse engineering the SSH protocol used by the device. This exploit involves identifying a vulnerability in the device's firmware that allows for SSH connections over a reverse tunnel, effectively bypassing the device's intended security measures. As a developer, this highlights the importance of implementing robust security measures and regularly updating dependencies to prevent similar vulnerabilities from being exploited in production codebases.
