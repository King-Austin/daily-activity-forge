### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL about the importance of disaster recovery planning from the recent 'Mea Culpa – Dark Hours' blog post by Terry Godier. The post outlines a catastrophic failure of a critical system due to a series of unfortunate events, including a faulty backup strategy, inadequate monitoring, and human error. The key takeaway for me is the need to prioritize disaster recovery planning and implement a robust backup strategy that includes regular testing and verification. This involves not only setting up automated backups but also ensuring that these backups are stored securely, can be restored quickly, and are easily accessible in case of a disaster.
