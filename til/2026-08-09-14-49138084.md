**TIL (Today I Learned)**

While reading about Uber's SubmitQueue, a high-performance speculative merge queue, I was struck by the potential implications for software developers. Specifically, I learned that a well-designed queue can significantly improve the performance of distributed systems by reducing contention and increasing throughput. The key takeaway is that speculative merge queues, like SubmitQueue, can provide a scalable solution for handling high-volume, low-latency data processing workloads, making them a valuable addition to the toolkit for developers building highly concurrent and fault-tolerant systems.
