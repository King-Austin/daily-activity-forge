# TIL: Honey, I shrunk the embeddings: Matryoshka vs. PCA

[Original Source](https://dylancastillo.co/posts/matryoshka-vs-pca)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL about the comparison of `Matryoshka` and PCA (Principal Component Analysis) in dimensionality reduction. `Matryoshka` appears to be a novel approach that leverages hierarchical clustering to shrink embeddings, whereas PCA relies on singular value decomposition (SVD). Notably, `Matryoshka` is able to preserve local structure in data, which can be beneficial for tasks like clustering and nearest neighbors, whereas PCA focuses on retaining global variance. This difference in approach highlights the importance of considering the specific use case when choosing a dimensionality reduction technique.
