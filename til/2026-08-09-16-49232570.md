_2026-08-09 16:23 UTC_

**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL that the author of the article `Tech sucks: You have to vote with your wallet, or nothing will change` suggests that consumers have the power to drive change in the tech industry by voting with their wallets, abandoning products and services from companies that prioritize profit over users' well-being. As a developer, this got me thinking about the technical debt I've accumulated in my projects. I realize that I might have inadvertently contributed to this problem by using tools and libraries that prioritize convenience over maintainability, or those that have poor security practices. This article has motivated me to review my tech stack and consider alternatives that align with my values, such as using open-source software, prioritizing data ownership, and adopting more secure coding practices.
