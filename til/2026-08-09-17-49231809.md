_2026-08-09 17:08 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.w3.org/Provider/Style/URI)

**TIL: Avoid Fragile URIs**
I learned that URIs should be designed to be persistent and unchanging, as outlined in the W3C's "Cool URIs Don't Change" article (1998). This means that software developers should avoid using query parameters or fragment identifiers for critical resource identification, as they can easily break when updated or migrated. Instead, use path-based URIs or consider using more robust identification methods, such as UUIDs or ETags, to ensure that resources can be reliably referenced and accessed over time. This consideration is especially important for applications with long-term data storage and retrieval requirements, like APIs, databases, and content management systems.
