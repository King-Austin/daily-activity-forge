**Today I Learned (TIL)**
========================

TIL from the recent Hacker News discussion that various open-source projects and startups are actively exploring the use of WebAssembly (WASM) as a way to deploy and run legacy software on modern cloud infrastructure. This trend highlights the potential for WASM to serve as a binary-compatible layer for reviving and maintaining older codebases, enabling them to take advantage of modern operating system and hardware features. Notably, some projects are leveraging the WASM runtime to run .NET and JVM-based applications on Linux and other non-Windows environments, which could have significant implications for migration and maintenance efforts in the industry.
