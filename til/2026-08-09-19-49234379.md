# TIL: The Power of Saying No

While reading the article "Saying No" by [Author], I was struck by the parallels between saying no in personal and professional contexts, particularly for software developers. As developers, we often find ourselves in situations where we're asked to take on too much, or implement a feature that doesn't align with the project's goals. This article reminded me of the importance of politely saying no, not just to protect our time and energy, but also to maintain code quality and prevent technical debt. By saying no to non-essential features or requests, we can focus on delivering high-quality solutions that meet the project's core requirements, ultimately making our codebase more maintainable and efficient.
