# TIL: New Zealand lost its music media, and what we're building to replace it

_2026-08-09 21:39 UTC_

[Original Source](https://propelmusic.co.nz/articles/the-sound-went-quiet-nz-music-media)

### Today I Learned

TIL that the loss of a national music media outlet can have significant implications for the distribution and promotion of local content, potentially leading to a reliance on decentralized platforms and community-driven initiatives to fill the gap. As a developer, this highlights the importance of considering the impact of technological changes on the ecosystems we build and the potential need for grassroots solutions to support niche or underserved communities. The example of Propeller's efforts to rebuild a digital music media scene in New Zealand underscores the potential for technology to facilitate community-driven revitalization and the need for adaptable, community-focused development strategies in response to changing market landscapes.
