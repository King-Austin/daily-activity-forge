### TIL: The Importance of Transparency in Tech Promises

TIL that over-promising and under-delivering is a common pitfall in tech, as exemplified by the article "Is it all just vapourware?" by Kira Howe. This got me thinking about the technical takeaways from this phenomenon. A key implication is the importance of fact-checking and due diligence when evaluating the feasibility of emerging technologies, especially those touted as revolutionary or game-changing. This includes considering the technical roadmaps, milestones, and proof-of-concepts to separate genuine innovation from hype. As a developer, I'll be more mindful of these red flags when assessing new technologies and their claims, and will prioritize transparency and open communication with stakeholders in my own projects to avoid similar pitfalls.
