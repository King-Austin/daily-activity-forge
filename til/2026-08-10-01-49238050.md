# TIL: How We Pushed CDC into Postgres

_2026-08-10 01:39 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/engineering/postgres-to-snowflake-replication-mirroring/)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that the CDC (Change Data Capture) mechanism implemented by Snowflake for replicating Postgres data can potentially disrupt traditional database replication approaches. By leveraging a CDC-like mechanism, Snowflake was able to replicate Postgres data without requiring changes to the source database, effectively decoupling the replication process from the source database's operation. This approach highlights the importance of considering alternative replication strategies when dealing with legacy databases or databases not designed with high-availability in mind.
