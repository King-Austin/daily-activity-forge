# TIL: What Happened to HackerOne?

_2026-08-10 02:54 UTC_

[Original Source](https://blog.teknogeek.io/posts/what-happened-to-hackerone/)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that HackerOne, a prominent bug bounty platform, has been experiencing declining growth and a reduced user base, potentially due to the increasing popularity of self-managed bug bounty programs and the rise of competing platforms. This news highlights the importance of staying adaptable in the rapidly evolving cybersecurity industry and the need for bug bounty platforms to continuously innovate and improve their offerings to maintain user trust and engagement. As a software developer, I'm taking note to keep an eye on industry trends and consider potential shifts in the demand for bug bounty services and the technologies that support them, such as custom bug tracking and reporting systems.
