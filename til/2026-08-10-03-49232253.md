### TIL: Cognitive Abilities & Mental Mapping

TIL that taxi drivers, who have extensively developed complex mental maps and spatial reasoning skills, are less likely to suffer from Alzheimer's disease. This got me thinking about the potential implications for software development. Specifically, this highlights the importance of designing systems that leverage cognitive abilities such as mental mapping and spatial reasoning. For instance, this could inform the design of more intuitive user interfaces that rely on visual and spatial cues, rather than purely text-based input. It also suggests that incorporating more interactive, exploratory learning experiences into software could have a positive impact on user engagement and retention, ultimately leading to more effective and user-friendly software.
