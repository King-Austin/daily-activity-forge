#### 2026-07: Implications of H-1B Immigration on the US Economy

TIL that research suggests the long-run effects of H-1B immigration on the US economy may lead to an increase in innovation and entrepreneurship, with potential benefits to the tech industry. The study found that a 10% increase in H-1B visas results in a 4.5% increase in patent applications and a 3.5% increase in entrepreneurs. This implies that a well-designed immigration system could be a key factor in driving technological advancements and fostering a culture of innovation, which is crucial for the continued growth and competitiveness of the tech sector.
