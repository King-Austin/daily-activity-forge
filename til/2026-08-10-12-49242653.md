### TIL: Squeak/Smalltalk 6.1 Release Notes

TIL that Squeak/Smalltalk 6.1 introduces several significant improvements, including a new compiler that can automatically inline methods, reducing memory usage and improving performance. This feature, combined with the existing support for just-in-time (JIT) compilation, highlights the potential for Smalltalk to excel in areas where dynamic typing and functional programming paradigms can provide significant benefits. The 6.1 release also includes enhancements to the Croquet platform, which enables scalable, collaborative, and persistent environments - a feature that could be particularly interesting for developers exploring distributed systems and multi-user applications. These advancements demonstrate the continued innovation in the Smalltalk ecosystem, emphasizing its potential as a powerful tool for building high-performance, scalable, and concurrent software systems.
