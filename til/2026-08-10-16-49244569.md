# Today I Learned (TIL)
### TIL: Importance of Handling Edge Cases in Conversational AI

After reading the article about Kinney Drugs' AI phone assistant debacle, I learned a valuable lesson about the importance of thorough testing and edge case handling in conversational AI development. The fact that hundreds of customers complained about the AI's inability to resolve simple issues, such as cancelling prescriptions, highlights the need for robust testing and validation of AI-powered systems. As developers, we must consider the 'worst-case scenario' and ensure that our AI systems can handle unexpected user input, misinterpretations, and other edge cases to prevent similar issues in our own projects. This includes investing time in testing and refining our AI systems to provide a seamless user experience and build trust with our customers.
