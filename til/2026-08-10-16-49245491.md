**Today I Learned: SMM Attack Vector via Long Interrupt**

TIL that a long interrupt can be used to exploit System Management Mode (SMM), a powerful and privileged execution environment on modern CPUs. This vulnerability, demonstrated by the `smiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii` proof-of-concept, leverages a previously unknown interaction between SMM and the interrupt handler, allowing an attacker to gain elevated privileges. As a developer, this highlights the importance of considering the interactions between different CPU modes and the potential for novel attack vectors, even in well-established architectural components like SMM. I'll need to review our project's code and consider potential SMM-related security implications, especially in scenarios involving interrupt handling and system firmware interactions.
