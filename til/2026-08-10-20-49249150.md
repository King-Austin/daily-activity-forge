_2026-08-10 20:52 UTC_

**TIL 2023-08-10**
I came across an interesting article on Hacker News about Illinois' new law (HB 5511) requiring operating systems to implement age verification for digital goods. This might seem like a non-technical issue, but it has potential implications for developers working on Linux distributions, which could be forced to comply with this new legislation. The law's focus on operating systems as a whole might lead to interesting technical challenges, such as how to verify the age of a user on a Linux system without compromising user anonymity or freedom. This raises questions about how developers will implement and handle age verification in Linux, and what the potential consequences might be for user privacy and freedom.
