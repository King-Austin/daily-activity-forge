# TIL: Early Adoption of Open Source Principles in Hardware Development
I came across an interesting article about Akizuki Denshi, a Japanese electronics company that published schematics of their products as early as the 1970s. This practice, which predates the concept of open-source software, highlights the importance of transparency and collaboration in innovation. As a software developer, I realized that this approach can be applied to our own work, emphasizing the value of sharing knowledge and designs to accelerate progress and reduce duplication of effort. By adopting a similar mindset, we can foster a more open and cooperative community, driving innovation and improving the quality of our projects.
