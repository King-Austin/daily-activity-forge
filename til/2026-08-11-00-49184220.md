# TIL: The "mechanical miracle" that ruined Mark Twain's life

_2026-08-11 00:23 UTC_

[Original Source](https://resobscura.substack.com/p/the-mechanical-miracle-that-ruined)

**TIL: The Overemphasis on Technology Can Have Unintended Consequences**

I came across an article detailing how Mark Twain's life was significantly impacted by the introduction of the Mergenthaler typesetting machine, which replaced human typesetters and disrupted the printing industry. This 'mechanical miracle' led to a decline in employment for typesetters and printers, ultimately affecting Twain's personal finances. As a software developer, this story highlights the importance of considering the human element in the development of automation and AI technologies. It's essential to weigh the benefits of technological advancements against the potential social and economic impacts on those who may be displaced or affected by such changes. This reminds me to always consider the broader implications of the projects I work on and strive to mitigate any negative consequences.
