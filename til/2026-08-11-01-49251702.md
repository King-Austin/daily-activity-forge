# TIL: Chicken Scheme 6.0

_2026-08-11 01:23 UTC_

[Original Source](https://code.call-cc.org/releases/6.0.0/NEWS)

TIL about Chicken Scheme 6.0, a significant update to the R7RS Scheme implementation. This release includes notable improvements such as performance enhancements, better Unicode support, and improved compatibility with various platforms. Notably, Chicken Scheme 6.0 now supports the R7RS standard, providing a more consistent and predictable language experience. As a developer, this is interesting because it offers a chance to leverage the strengths of a functional programming paradigm, potentially leading to more concise and composable code.
