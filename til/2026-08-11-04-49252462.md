### TIL - Floppy Disk Recycling

TIL about the Floppy Disk Recycling initiative by Floppydisk.com. This project highlights the surprising number of floppy disks still in use today, particularly in legacy systems and industrial applications. As a developer, this got me thinking about the potential impact on our digital heritage and the need for sustainable software development practices. I realized that many of the old systems and components we're still using today are likely to become increasingly obsolete and difficult to maintain, making it essential to consider the long-term implications of our technical choices and strive for more environmentally friendly and forward-thinking approaches to software development.
