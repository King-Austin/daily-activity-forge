### Today I Learned

TIL that demographic shifts, such as the decline of rural populations, can have significant implications for software development, particularly in areas like geospatial mapping, urban planning, and resource allocation. The fact that half of Europe's towns and villages have fewer residents than 60 years ago highlights the need for developers to consider the effects of changing population dynamics on services like address validation, geographic information systems (GIS), and data-driven decision-making in urban planning. This shift also underscores the importance of maintaining and updating legacy infrastructure, such as mapping data and geocoding systems, to ensure accuracy and relevance in an increasingly decentralized population landscape.
