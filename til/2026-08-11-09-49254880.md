# TIL: France's Anti-Telemarketing Law Implications for Developers

Today, I learned that France plans to ban unsolicited telemarketing calls from August 11th, 2026. As a developer, this has implications for our industry, particularly in terms of data protection and compliance. With the rise of automated dialing systems and AI-powered telemarketing tools, developers will need to adapt their solutions to comply with the new regulations. This may involve implementing additional authentication and consent mechanisms for users, as well as regular auditing to ensure that our systems are not inadvertently facilitating non-compliant telemarketing activities.
