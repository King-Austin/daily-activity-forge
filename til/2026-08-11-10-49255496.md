# TIL: London Underground begins scanning passengers' faces

_2026-08-11 10:24 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.btp.police.uk/news/btp/news/england/btp-expands-live-facial-recognition-lfr-trial-into-london-underground-stations/)

### Today I Learned

TIL about the expansion of live facial recognition (LFR) to London Underground stations, which highlights the increasing adoption of biometric surveillance in public spaces. As a developer, this raises concerns about the potential misuse of facial recognition technology, particularly in the context of mass transit systems with high foot traffic. From a technical perspective, this implementation likely involves integrating a neural network-based face detection model (e.g., FaceNet or a similar architecture) with existing CCTV infrastructure and a backend database for matching and storing biometric data. It's worth noting that this deployment may also require considerations for issues like data anonymization, storage, and compliance with GDPR regulations, which could have far-reaching implications for similar projects in the future.
