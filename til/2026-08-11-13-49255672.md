### TIL: The Value of Social Media Data

TIL that multiple firms are paying up to $100k/month for access to Truth Social posts, highlighting the immense value of social media data in the post-2020 social media landscape. This news should serve as a reminder to developers that social media platforms now consider user-generated content a valuable asset, and access to this data can come at a significant cost. As a developer, understanding this shift in perspective can inform design decisions around data storage, access control, and monetization in applications that handle user-generated content. It may also prompt consideration of data anonymization techniques to maintain user trust and comply with data protection regulations.
