_2026-08-11 14:39 UTC_

## Today I Learned (TIL)
### Leaking Real Email Address in curl User-Agent String

I came across a concerning issue in the Claude Code project where a real email address is being leaked in the User-Agent string of a `curl` command. This vulnerability can expose sensitive information to servers or proxy servers along the request path. As a developer, I learned that it's crucial to inspect and sanitize User-Agent strings to prevent such leaks, especially when making outgoing requests to untrusted services or APIs. This highlights the importance of carefully managing and masking sensitive information, even in seemingly innocuous headers like User-Agent, to maintain user privacy and security.
