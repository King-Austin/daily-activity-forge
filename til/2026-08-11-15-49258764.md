# Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that Manus, a company that offers a suite of developer productivity tools, is regaining its independence. This event highlights the importance of monitoring the ecosystem and understanding the implications of companies changing ownership or structure. For a developer, the return of Manus as an independent company may lead to a shift in their product roadmap, potentially resulting in new features or changes to their existing tools that can impact workflows and integrations. This serves as a reminder to stay informed about the companies behind the tools we use, as these changes can have a ripple effect on our development processes and the technologies we integrate into our projects.
