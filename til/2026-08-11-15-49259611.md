**Today I Learned (TIL)**

TIL about `git-knife`, a tool that enables mass editing of Git commit messages, authors, and dates in a spreadsheet-like interface. This caught my attention as it highlights the growing demand for more user-friendly Git management tools. From a technical perspective, I'm intrigued by the fact that `git-knife` appears to utilize `git filter-branch` under the hood, which is a powerful but resource-intensive command. This implementation choice raises questions about the tool's performance and potential impact on large, complex Git repositories. I'll likely dig deeper into `git filter-branch` and its usage in `git-knife` to better understand the trade-offs involved.
