### TIL: The Decline of Physical Job Boards and Implications for Software Development

TIL that newspaper classifieds, once a primary means of finding employment, have largely been replaced by digital platforms. This shift highlights the importance of maintaining a strong online presence, particularly for software developers. As the majority of job searches now occur online, having an up-to-date personal website or profile on platforms like GitHub or LinkedIn becomes crucial for visibility and discoverability. This trend reinforces the need for developers to invest time in curating their digital footprint and optimizing it for search engines to increase their chances of being found by potential employers.
