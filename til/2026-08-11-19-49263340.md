# TIL: Nvidia Nemotron 3.5 lightning and Nemo Switchyard

_2026-08-11 19:55 UTC_

[Original Source](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-lightning-switchyard-rtx-dgx/)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL about the recent advancements in Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5, specifically the introduction of "Nemotron Lightning" and the "Nemo Switchyard". This new technology allows for a significant reduction in latency when switching between different workloads on an RTX-enabled DGX machine. As a developer, this has potential implications for applications requiring low-latency multi-tasking or real-time processing, such as scientific simulations, machine learning, or gaming. The Nemotron Lightning architecture enables more efficient utilization of the GPU, allowing for faster context switching and improved overall system performance. This could lead to the development of more responsive and scalable applications, making it easier to take advantage of the massive compute capabilities of modern GPUs.
