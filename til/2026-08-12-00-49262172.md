# TIL: Ethical Cold Outreach

_2026-08-12 00:26 UTC_

[Original Source](https://blog.val.town/ethical-cold-outreach)

**TIL (12 August 2026)**

After reading the article "Ethical Cold Outreach" on Hacker News, I've gained insight into the potential downsides of unsolicited outreach. As a developer, this resonated with me, given the prevalence of cold emails and LinkedIn messages we often receive. The article highlights the importance of considering the recipient's time and boundaries when initiating contact. This has implications for our own outreach strategies, such as when reaching out to potential collaborators or contributors. A key takeaway is the need to personalize and research our approach, rather than sending generic emails or requests. This can be achieved by leveraging APIs like Hunter for email address extraction or using tools like LinkedIn's Sales Navigator for targeted outreach. By taking a more considerate approach, we can establish stronger connections and build more meaningful relationships with others in our industry.
