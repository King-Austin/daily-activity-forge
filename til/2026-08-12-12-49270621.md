### Delphi 13 Community Edition Availability

TIL that the Delphi 13 Community Edition is now available for download. This development has potential implications for cross-platform development, as Delphi is known for its ability to create Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and Linux applications from a single codebase. The fact that a professional-grade development environment with VCL and FireMonkey libraries is available at no cost may encourage more developers to experiment with Delphi, increasing the pool of talent with experience in cross-platform development using a compiled language (Pascal).
