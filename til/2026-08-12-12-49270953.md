_2026-08-12 12:12 UTC_

**TIL 2026-08-12**

I stumbled upon a fascinating Hacker News article, "2026 Eclipse Webcams" ([https://jonty.github.io/2026_eclipse_webcams/](https://jonty.github.io/2026_eclipse_webcams/)), that highlights the effort to coordinate and provide live webcams for the upcoming 2026 solar eclipse. As a developer, I find it intriguing that the author leveraged APIs, such as NASA's APOD (Astronomy Picture of the Day) and OpenWeatherMap, to fetch real-time data and display it on a map. This got me thinking about the potential use cases for API aggregation and real-time data visualization in our own projects. I'll keep this in mind for future development, especially when working on large-scale or geographically distributed applications where real-time data is crucial.
