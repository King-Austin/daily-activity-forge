# TIL: AI is removing the middle class of software engineering

_2026-08-12 14:42 UTC_

[Original Source](https://blog.florianherrengt.com/ai-removing-middle-class-software-engineering.html)

**TIL: AI's Impact on Software Engineering Career Trajectories**

I came across an article suggesting that AI might be displacing the middle class of software engineers, implying that junior engineers may struggle to find work due to automation and AI-powered tools, while senior engineers continue to thrive due to their ability to adapt, innovate, and provide high-level guidance. This raises concerns about the future of skill acquisition and retention in our industry, as well as the need for continuous upskilling and reskilling to remain relevant. It's essential for developers to start thinking about how to future-proof their careers by focusing on high-level problem-solving, creative thinking, and expertise in areas that are less likely to be automated.
