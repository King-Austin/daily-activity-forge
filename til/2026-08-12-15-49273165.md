# TIL: License Plate Reader Searches Should Require a Warrant

_2026-08-12 15:13 UTC_

[Original Source](https://andrewpwheeler.com/2026/08/12/license-plate-reader-searches-should-require-a-warrant/)

**TIL**: I came across an article suggesting that license plate reader searches should require a warrant, which has me thinking about the technical implications for data collection and storage. If such searches become warrant-requiring, it could lead to more stringent requirements for logging and retention of LPR data, potentially forcing developers to implement more robust audit trails and ensure data minimization practices when handling PII (Personally Identifiable Information) from LPR feeds. This could also prompt a reevaluation of data processing pipelines to ensure that they can adapt to changing regulatory requirements and provide transparent logging and explainability for any automated decision-making processes involved in LPR data analysis.
