TIL about the Met Office's Climate Dashboard feature on glaciers, which serves as a reminder of the importance of data visualization and accessibility in climate science. The interactive dashboard, available at https://climate.metoffice.cloud/glaciers.html, uses web technologies like HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to present complex glacier data in an engaging and easy-to-understand format. This example highlights the potential for web development to make scientific research more accessible to a broader audience, and suggests that similar approaches could be applied to other domains, such as data-driven storytelling and scientific communication.
