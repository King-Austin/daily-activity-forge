**TIL: Supply Chain Disruptions Can Expose Systemic Vulnerabilities**

While reading the article "Thanks to social media, canned sardines are a scarcity on the supermarket shelf" (https://corneroffifth.studio/why-cant-you-find-canned-sardines-right-now/), I was reminded that supply chain disruptions can have far-reaching implications, even affecting seemingly unrelated systems. As a developer, this highlights the importance of considering external factors when designing and implementing software systems, especially those that interact with or rely on external services. For example, if a food production facility experiences a shortage of a specific ingredient, it could lead to downstream effects on inventory management systems, supply chain logistics, and even customer-facing applications. This serves as a reminder to design systems with flexibility and fault tolerance in mind, and to proactively monitor and adapt to potential disruptions that may impact our software and the businesses it serves.
