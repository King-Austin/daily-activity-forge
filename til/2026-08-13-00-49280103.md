### TIL: IBM PC's 45th Birthday & Model F/XT

TIL that the IBM PC, released 45 years ago, had a significant impact on modern computing. The Model F/XT keyboards, widely used in the industry during the 1980s and 1990s, were known for their durability and tactile feedback. For software developers, this news highlights the importance of ergonomic design in keyboard layout and the value of tactile feedback in typing. Additionally, the IBM PC's widespread adoption led to the development of the x86 architecture, which remains a dominant platform for software development today. This serves as a reminder to consider the long-term implications of design choices and the importance of compatibility when building software systems.
