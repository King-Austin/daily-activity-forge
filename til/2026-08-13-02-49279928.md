### Today I Learned

TIL about the potential relevance of Bertrand Russell's "Principia Mathematica" to modern software development, thanks to a thought-provoking article by Oleg Mazonka. This classic work in mathematical logic, initially published in the early 20th century, may offer valuable insights into type theory and functional programming. Its use of rigorous mathematical definitions to establish foundations for mathematics could inform our design of robust and composable software systems. Specifically, I'm intrigued by the potential application of Russell's type theory to improve our understanding of type systems in programming languages, particularly in the context of dependently-typed programming and the development of more expressive, composable, and maintainable codebases.
