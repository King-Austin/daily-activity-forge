# TIL: What's New in Flutter 3.47

_2026-08-13 02:29 UTC_

[Original Source](https://flutter.dev/blog/whats-new-in-flutter-3-47)

### Today I Learned

TIL that Flutter 3.47 introduces improvements to accessibility features, including better support for screen readers and improved keyboard navigation. Specifically, I learned that Flutter 3.47 now includes a `ScreenReaderScope` widget that allows developers to provide alternative text for images and other UI elements, making it easier to create accessible apps. This update suggests that Flutter is prioritizing accessibility and will likely have a positive impact on the development of inclusive and usable mobile and desktop applications.
