# TIL: Ownership of AI Model Outputs May Not Translate to Training Permissions
I came across an interesting thread on Hacker News discussing Claude's stance on model training with user-generated outputs. It appears that even if you own the outputs, the underlying model architecture and training data remain proprietary, restricting your ability to train a similar model. This highlights the complex interplay between model ownership, intellectual property, and the challenges of retraining proprietary AI models. As a developer, this serves as a reminder that even with ownership of generated content, underlying model components may still be subject to licensing or usage restrictions, underscoring the importance of carefully reviewing terms of service and EULAs when working with AI-powered tools.
