**TIL: Finding Flow in Mundane Tasks Can Inform Software Development**

While reading an article on picking berries as a form of meditation, I'm reminded of the concept of 'flow' in software development. Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi's theory of optimal experience suggests that tasks which are challenging yet manageable can induce a state of heightened focus and engagement. This idea can be applied to coding, where developers often struggle with tasks that are too easy or too difficult. By finding the 'sweet spot' of challenge and familiarity, software developers can increase their productivity, creativity, and overall job satisfaction. This perspective can inform approaches to task assignment, code review, and even personal project management, encouraging developers to seek out tasks that allow them to enter a state of flow.
