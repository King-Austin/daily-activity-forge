# TIL - Spreadsheets as a Technical Debt Indicator

TIL from the article "My Rules for Using Spreadsheets" that spreadsheets can serve as a proxy indicator for technical debt in a system. The author's rules for responsible spreadsheet usage highlight the importance of version control, documentation, and modular design. As a software developer, this caught my attention because it underscores the value of applying principles like separation of concerns, modularity, and maintainability to all forms of data processing, not just code. In other words, the more a spreadsheet resembles a miniature application, the higher the likelihood of it becoming a technical debt hotspot that can spread to other parts of the system. This got me thinking about how I can apply these insights to my own work, recognizing that even non-code systems can benefit from software engineering principles to minimize maintenance costs and improve overall system health.
