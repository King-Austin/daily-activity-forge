### TIL: Evolution of Computing Hardware Implications

TIL about the ENIAC, UNIVAC, and Skeduflo machines from the early computing era through the article 'Come for ENIAC, Stay for UNIVAC and Skeduflo'. ENIAC's massive size and complexity, despite its revolutionary ability to perform calculations, highlights the need for future improvements in computing efficiency. UNIVAC's development, which led to the first commercially sold computer, showcases the importance of accessibility and user-friendliness in computing systems. Additionally, Skeduflo, a relatively unknown device, demonstrates the diverse range of experimental projects pursued during this period. As a developer, I should be aware of the historical context and the driving forces behind the evolution of computing hardware, which can inform my design decisions when working on complex systems or optimizing performance.
