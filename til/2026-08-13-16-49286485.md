# TIL: Kubernetes on Oxide: How Customer Needs Shaped Our Integrations

_2026-08-13 16:00 UTC_

[Original Source](https://oxide.computer/blog/kubernetes-on-oxide)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that Oxide is exploring a Kubernetes integration, which could shift the paradigm for sandboxed development and deployment of microservices. The potential implications of this move are twofold: first, it could simplify the process of developing and testing microservices in a controlled environment, similar to how Docker has streamlined containerization. Second, the integration could enable more seamless deployment of Oxide-based services onto Kubernetes clusters, allowing for greater flexibility and scalability in cloud-native applications. This development highlights the ongoing convergence of sandboxed development and container orchestration technologies, and I'll be keeping a close eye on Oxide's progress in this area.
