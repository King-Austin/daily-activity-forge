# TIL: Choose Boring Technology (2015)

_2026-08-13 18:15 UTC_

[Original Source](https://mcfunley.com/choose-boring-technology)

**Today I Learned**
TIL about the "Choose Boring Technology" principle, which reminds me that sometimes the most effective approach is to select familiar, proven tools and frameworks rather than embracing the latest trends. This mindset helps mitigate potential risks and costs associated with adopting new, untested technologies, and allows us to focus on building robust, maintainable software.
