### TIL: The Overemphasis on NP-Completeness in Algorithm Design

Today, I came across the article "NP-Overrated" by [author](https://gruhn.me/blog/2026-08-13/), which challenges the conventional wisdom surrounding NP-completeness in algorithm design. The author's argument is that the emphasis on NP-hard problems has led to an overemphasis on optimization techniques rather than problem reformulation and alternative approaches. This got me thinking about the potential implications of this perspective for software development: it highlights the importance of considering the problem domain and reformulating problems to make them more tractable. This may involve identifying simplifying assumptions or leveraging domain-specific knowledge to design more efficient algorithms, rather than solely relying on generic optimization techniques. This shift in perspective could lead to more effective problem-solving and a more nuanced understanding of computational complexity.
