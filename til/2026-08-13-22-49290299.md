_2026-08-13 22:31 UTC_

**TIL: 'Understanding is the new bottleneck' by Geoffrey Litt**

I came across an insightful article by Geoffrey Litt, highlighting the shift in bottlenecks from computational power to understanding and abstraction. This shift has significant implications for software development. As our systems become increasingly complex and interconnected, the ability to reason about and understand the interactions and implications of our codebase is becoming a major constraint. This means that developers will need to focus on creating high-level abstractions, designing robust and self-explanatory code, and investing time in understanding the underlying systems and technologies, rather than solely focusing on writing efficient code. This shift requires a more holistic approach to software development, prioritizing maintainability, scalability, and ease of understanding over raw performance.
