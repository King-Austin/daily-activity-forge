_2026-08-14 01:31 UTC_

[Original Source](https://sparrowmap.com/)

# Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL about SparrowMap, an initiative that uses publicly available camera feeds to track government vehicles. This project highlights the power of crowdsourced geospatial data and the potential for open-source technologies to monitor government activities. From a technical perspective, I'm intrigued by the use of computer vision and machine learning algorithms to detect and track specific vehicle types. This approach raises questions about the feasibility of deploying similar technologies to monitor other public or private entities, and the potential implications for transparency, accountability, and data protection.
