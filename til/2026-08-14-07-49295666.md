# Today I Learned

TIL that the transmissionist (traditional) approach to knowledge acquisition, often employed in books, may be less effective than the constructivist approach, which emphasizes active learning and experiential knowledge construction. As a software developer, I see parallels with the way we often struggle to apply theoretical concepts to real-world problems. This got me thinking about the importance of hands-on experience and experimentation in software development. By shifting our focus from passively consuming documentation and tutorials to actively building and testing our own projects, we may be able to internalize knowledge more effectively and retain it better. This approach could also be applied to team learning and knowledge sharing, where we encourage experimentation and collaboration over mere knowledge transmission.
