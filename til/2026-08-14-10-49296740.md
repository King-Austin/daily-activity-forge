### TIL: Potential Breakage of Opus 5 Compatibility

I came across an article discussing the perceived degradation in usability of Opus 5, a widely adopted audio codec. Upon reviewing the technical details, I found that the issue is allegedly caused by the codec's new packet structure and its impact on packetization, specifically in relation to the `NAL unit` (Network Abstraction Layer unit) and ` Opus packet headers`. This may result in increased latency, reduced compatibility with older clients, or even incorrect packet loss detection. As a developer, this highlights the importance of testing and validation when working with new or updated dependencies, particularly in real-time applications where compatibility and performance are critical.
