**TIL: 'Differential Heuristics' for Pathfinding**
=====================================================

I just read the insightful article on 'Differential Heuristics' by @redblobgames. In short, I learned that traditional pathfinding heuristics like Manhattan Distance and Euclidean Distance can be improved by incorporating a "differential heuristic" that takes into account the difference between the current node's cost and the target node's cost. This can significantly reduce overestimation and improve the convergence of pathfinding algorithms, especially in scenarios with non-uniform grid sizes and varying node costs. I'm eager to test this technique in my future projects and see how it can enhance the performance of pathfinding-based systems.
