# TIL: Don't classify, hallucinate!

_2026-08-14 12:32 UTC_

[Original Source](https://softwaredoug.com/blog/2026/08/10/hypothetical-classifications)

**TIL (Today I Learned)**

August 14, 2026

TIL about the concept of "hallucination" in software development, as discussed in the article "Don't classify, hallucinate!" by Doug Sutherland. In essence, the author argues that machine learning models often "hallucinate" - producing incorrect or irrelevant outputs - when attempting to classify data that doesn't fit the training distribution. This got me thinking about potential implications for our own projects. Specifically, I realized that overly broad or simplistic classification schemes can lead to misleading results and downstream errors. I'll be keeping this in mind when designing classification pipelines, and considering ways to handle edge cases and unknown inputs to avoid this type of "hallucination".
