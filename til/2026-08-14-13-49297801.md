# TIL: Dear people who work at the airport

_2026-08-14 13:47 UTC_

[Original Source](https://life-after-ssri.bearblog.dev/dear-people-who-work-at-the-airport/)

### TIL

Today I learned about the potential impact of airport worker burnout on system reliability and cybersecurity. The article highlights the high turnover rates and burnout among airport workers, which can lead to a lack of documentation and institutional knowledge. As a software developer, this serves as a reminder of the importance of knowledge management and documentation in complex systems. It's essential to design systems with maintainability and scalability in mind, and to prioritize knowledge sharing and onboarding processes to mitigate the effects of personnel turnover. This is particularly relevant in industries like transportation and logistics, where system failures can have significant consequences.
