### TIL: HashAgent Enables Decentralized AI Model Sharing via WebGPU

TIL that HashAgent, a web-based AI model sharing platform, leverages WebGPU to run AI agents locally on users' machines, enabling decentralized sharing of AI models. This development implies that WebGPU can be used to offload computationally intensive tasks from the cloud, reducing latency and improving model performance. Additionally, the fact that models can be shared as simple URLs suggests new possibilities for peer-to-peer model collaboration and democratization of AI development, allowing developers to share and build upon each other's work without relying on centralized infrastructure.
