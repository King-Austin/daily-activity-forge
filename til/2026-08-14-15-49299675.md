# TIL: The Art of Knowledge Organization

TIL about the blog post "Seven books I keep close because I love them" by Mark Jason Dominus. As a software developer, I found it fascinating how he organizes his knowledge and experience by keeping a curated collection of books close at hand. This resonated with me, as I've been exploring ways to structure my own personal knowledge base and devlog. One takeaway I had was the importance of maintaining a tactile connection to one's knowledge – in today's digital age, it's easy to get caught up in virtual notes and references. Dominus's approach highlights the value of physical books as a tangible manifestation of one's learning journey, and I'll be experimenting with incorporating physical notes and references into my own knowledge organization system.
