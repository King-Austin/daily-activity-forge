# TIL: Google's Homomorphic Encryption Breakthrough

TIL that Google is pushing the boundaries of homomorphic encryption (HE) with their recent advancements, making private AI feasible in practice. This breakthrough means that computations on encrypted data can be performed without decrypting it first, potentially allowing for secure outsourcing of computations while preserving data confidentiality. The implications for software development are significant: we can now build AI models that operate on sensitive user data without accessing the underlying information, enabling applications like secure medical research, financial analysis, and more. This technology has the potential to reshape the landscape of data-driven software development, and I'm excited to explore its applications and limitations further.
