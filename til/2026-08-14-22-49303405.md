# TIL: What You Gain by Building Your Own Game Engine

_2026-08-14 22:18 UTC_

[Original Source](https://eliasfarhan.ch/gamedev/cpp/2026/08/14/srnative-01-why-a-custom-engine.html)

### Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL that building a custom game engine can grant developers fine-grained control over low-level optimizations, allowing for tailored performance and memory management. By not relying on an existing engine, one can optimize specific components, such as rendering and physics, which can lead to significant performance improvements in certain scenarios. This approach also enables developers to integrate custom technologies and features, reducing the need for workarounds or hacks that might compromise the overall game experience. However, it also means assuming the responsibility of maintaining and updating the engine, which can be a significant undertaking.
