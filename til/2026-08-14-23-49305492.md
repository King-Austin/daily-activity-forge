### TIL: RISC-V Open-Source Chip Design Security Risks

TIL that the RISC-V open-source chip design, touted as a more secure alternative to traditional CPU architectures, has potential security risks due to its open nature. As described in the article "RISC-V: They should have known better" by Dmitry Grigoriev, these risks stem from the fact that anyone can modify and distribute RISC-V cores, potentially introducing backdoors or other malicious functionality. This highlights the importance of thorough vetting and auditing of open-source projects, especially those with security implications, to mitigate potential risks and ensure the integrity of the software or hardware being developed.
