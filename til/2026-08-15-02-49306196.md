#### Simplifying and Refactoring Introductory Calculus

TIL about a research paper titled "Simplifying and Refactoring Introductory Calculus" (https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.03459) which attempts to reformulate the foundations of calculus using mathematical notation similar to that used in programming. This approach has implications for software developers working on complex systems, particularly those involving optimization and machine learning. By reimagining calculus in a more programming-friendly notation, the authors aim to make it more accessible and intuitive for developers to reason about and implement optimization techniques, potentially leading to more efficient and scalable solutions.
