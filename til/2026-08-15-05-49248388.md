**TIL: Alternative SSH Port Usage and Single-Packet Authorization**

TIL that closing SSH port 22 is a viable security practice, as demonstrated in the article 'I close SSH port 22 (and what I use instead)' by Michele Bologna. The article highlights the use of a non-standard SSH port (e.g., 9922) and Single-Packet Authorization (SPA) enabled with fwknop, a one-time password system that prevents brute-force attacks on the SSH connection. This approach reduces exposure to potential SSH scanning and brute-force attacks, while also improving compliance with security best practices. As developers, it's essential to consider this alternative approach to SSH configuration to enhance our systems' security posture.
