### TIL: WCAG 2.2 Guidelines Now Apply to ePub and PDF Formats

TIL that the revised WCAG 2.2 guidelines now mandate accessibility requirements for digital publications in ePub and PDF formats. This update means developers working on e-book and document publishing platforms must ensure compliance with 16 new guidelines, including improved navigation, semantic structure, and enhanced visual presentation for users with visual impairments. Specifically, developers will need to implement features like 'ARIA roles and attributes' for semantic structure and provide 'alternative text for images' to ensure seamless accessibility in these formats.
