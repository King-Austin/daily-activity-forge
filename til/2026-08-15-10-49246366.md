# Today I Learned

TIL about Eigendrum, a web application that generates unique drum sounds based on user-input shapes. The technical takeaway from this project is the use of Fourier analysis and the concept of eigenvectors to transform shapes into sound frequencies. This approach could be applied to other domain-specific problems where visual data needs to be translated into audio or vice versa, such as music generation or visualization of complex data.
