I came across an interesting article detailing the use of GCC's nested functions with wide pointers and no trampolines. This technique allows for efficient and compact implementations of callback functions, leveraging GCC's inline assembly capabilities. The implications are significant for systems programming, particularly in the context of embedded systems or other resource-constrained environments where memory and performance are critical. By avoiding the use of trampolines, this approach eliminates the need for additional memory allocations, reducing latency and improving code density.
