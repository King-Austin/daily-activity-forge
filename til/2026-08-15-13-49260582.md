# TIL: The Color of White Light

Today I came across a fascinating visualization of the visible spectrum at [https://ludens.cl/photo/spectra/spectra.html](https://ludens.cl/photo/spectra/spectra.html), which made me realize the limitations of RGB color representation in digital displays. The visible spectrum is actually a continuous range of frequencies, spanning from approximately 380nm (violet) to 780nm (red). However, digital displays typically reproduce colors using a limited set of RGB values, which can lead to inaccuracies and gamut limitations. This got me thinking about the importance of color management and profiling in graphics and UI design, particularly when working with high-fidelity color representations or when targeting specific display technologies (e.g., wide-gamut or HDR displays). I'll have to look into the specifics of color spaces and profiles to better understand the implications of these limitations in software development.
