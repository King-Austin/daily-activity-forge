### TIL: AI Development is More About Leadership Than Coding

Today, I came across the article 'Working with AI Feels More Like Leadership Than Coding' by Allen Bargi, which sheds light on the evolving role of software developers in the AI era. As I read through it, I realized that the rise of AI doesn't necessarily mean more coding, but rather more emphasis on defining the problem, designing the system, and guiding the AI to solve it. This implies that developers will need to adapt their skill sets to prioritize higher-level thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills, making the transition from a purely technical role to a more leadership-oriented one. This shift in focus could lead to more collaboration with other stakeholders, such as product managers and domain experts, to ensure that AI systems are aligned with business objectives and meet user needs.
