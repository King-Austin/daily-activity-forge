### TIL: Inflation-Resilient Pricing for Digital Goods

TIL that the price of a Costco hot dog has increased, sparking a discussion on inflation and pricing strategies. As a developer, I realize that this news can be seen as a real-world example of how to design robust pricing models for digital goods. The fact that Costco's hot dog price has stayed the same for nearly 40 years, despite inflation, is a testament to the power of sticky pricing. This phenomenon can be attributed to the psychological anchor effect, where a perceived value is created through a fixed price point. This concept can be applied to software development by implementing pricing tiers or subscription models that take into account the cost of living and the value perceived by customers.
