### TIL: Challenges in AI-Driven Drug Discovery

TIL that despite the hype around AI-driven drug discovery, the field still faces significant technical hurdles. I learned from the article that most AI models trained on existing datasets struggle to generalize to novel targets and compounds, hindering the discovery of new medications. Moreover, the integration of diverse data sources, such as structural biology and clinical data, poses a challenge. For developers working on similar projects, this highlights the importance of robust data curation, transfer learning techniques, and model explainability to overcome these challenges and unlock the full potential of AI-driven drug discovery.
