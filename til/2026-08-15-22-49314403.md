**TIL: Abdominal Fat May Be a More Accurate Predictor of Heart Disease Risk Than BMI**

I came across a recent study suggesting that abdominal fat is a more reliable indicator of heart disease risk than BMI (Body Mass Index). As a developer, I immediately thought about the potential implications for health and fitness apps. Currently, many apps rely on user-input BMI data to provide insights into their health risks. This new information could lead to a shift towards incorporating more accurate, non-invasive methods to measure body fat distribution, such as bioelectrical impedance analysis or machine learning-powered analysis of body shape data from images or wearables. This could ultimately lead to more effective health risk assessments and potentially even more personalized recommendations for users.
