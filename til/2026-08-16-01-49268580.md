# Today I Learned (TIL)

TIL about the Asus Oxiis Bike Booster, a smart bike accessory that utilizes Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and cellular connectivity to enhance bike safety features. This technology showcases the practical application of IoT and edge computing in a real-world scenario. I take away that BLE can efficiently enable device-to-device communication in resource-constrained environments, while cellular connectivity provides a means for remote data transmission and control. This has implications for developers working on IoT projects, highlighting the importance of balancing device power consumption with the need for reliable and secure data exchange.
