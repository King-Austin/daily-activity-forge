### TIL: "Cascading Knowledge Deficits" in LLMs

TIL that training an LLM on a narrow, fifth-grade level knowledge base can lead to a "cascading knowledge deficit" - a phenomenon where the model's inability to understand higher-level concepts causes it to generate incorrect or incomplete responses to even seemingly basic questions. This highlights the importance of diverse and comprehensive training data in developing reliable LLMs. As a developer, this underscores the need for robust data curation and validation processes when training AI models, lest we inadvertently create "echo chambers" that reinforce our biases and limitations.
