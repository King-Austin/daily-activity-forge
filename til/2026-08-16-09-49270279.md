### TIL: Impulse Tracker - a Potential Paradigm Shift in Music Synthesis

TIL about the Impulse Tracker, a music tracker that leverages the concept of impulse responses to achieve high-quality digital signal processing, as demonstrated by [OVIDEM's implementation](https://ovidem.com/impulsetracker/). The core idea is that by manipulating impulse responses, one can create complex audio effects and sounds without the need for extensive manual scripting or complex audio rendering algorithms. This approach has significant implications for music synthesis and audio processing, potentially enabling developers to create more expressive and dynamic audio experiences with less computational overhead.
