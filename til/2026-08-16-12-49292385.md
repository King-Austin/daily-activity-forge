**TIL: Open-Source eGPU Firmware Implications**

Today, I learned about Chestnut, an eGPU dock with open-source firmware that can be flashed with an RX 9060 GPU. This development suggests that users may be able to bypass proprietary eGPU firmware restrictions, enabling support for a wider range of hardware configurations. As a developer, this raises interesting questions about the potential for community-driven firmware development and the implications for eGPU vendor lock-in. It also highlights the importance of open-source firmware in enabling greater flexibility and customization options for users, which could lead to increased adoption of eGPU technology for various applications, from gaming to professional workstations.
