### TIL: PayPal's Unlocked Screens May Indirectly Leverage Browser Extensions

TIL that PayPal's developers intentionally leave their screens unlocked, potentially as a means of using browser extensions (specifically, browser-based SSH clients) to easily manage their development environments. This approach allows them to bypass the need for a physical keyboard and mouse, which might otherwise be required for tasks such as debugging or code review. While not inherently a security concern, this technique highlights the flexibility of modern web development tools and may inspire alternative workflows for developers looking to streamline their productivity.
