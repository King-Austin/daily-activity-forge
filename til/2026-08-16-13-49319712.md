### TIL: Exploring Self-Managed Root DNS

TIL that building a personal root DNS resolver can grant fine-grained control over DNS resolution, potentially bypassing limitations and costs associated with public DNS services. By running their own root DNS, developers like Anthony can cache and route DNS queries more efficiently, reducing latency and improving performance. This approach also offers more flexibility in handling domain blacklisting, rate limiting, and IP address spoofing, making it an attractive option for developers working with sensitive or high-traffic projects.
