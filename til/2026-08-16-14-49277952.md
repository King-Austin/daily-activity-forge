# TIL: Does anyone run Postgres without PgBouncer?

_2026-08-16 14:07 UTC_

[Original Source](https://brandur.org/fragments/postgres-without-pgbouncer)

**Today I Learned**
TIL that relying solely on Postgres' connection pooling can lead to performance issues and increased resource utilization, as connections may remain idle for extended periods, consuming resources without generating revenue or serving users. This insight highlights the potential benefits of using a dedicated connection pooling solution like PgBouncer, which can help mitigate these issues and improve overall database performance and scalability.
