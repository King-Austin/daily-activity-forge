### TIL: Firefox for iOS now has native adblocker capabilities

TIL that Firefox for iOS has gained native adblocker support. This development highlights the growing importance of content filtering in mobile browsers, particularly on iOS, where ad-blocking has been historically restricted due to Apple's Safari content blockers limitations. As a developer, this shift could imply increased competition for third-party adblocker solutions, potentially forcing them to adapt or innovate beyond traditional blocking methods, such as content filtering and DNS-based solutions. Further, this development might also encourage other mobile browsers to pursue similar ad-blocking capabilities, leading to a more sophisticated mobile browsing experience and a greater emphasis on content filtering in browser development.
