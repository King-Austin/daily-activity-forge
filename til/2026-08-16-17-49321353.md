**TIL: NIH grant cancellation may impact medical data availability**

TIL that the NIH is ending a grant program for budding clinical researchers, which could have significant implications for software developers working on medical data analysis or healthcare-related projects. This grant program likely contributed to the availability of diverse, anonymized medical datasets that developers rely on to train and test AI models. Its cancellation may lead to reduced access to these valuable resources, making it more challenging to develop accurate and generalizable healthcare solutions. Developers should consider preparing for these changes by either seeking alternative data sources or exploring alternative approaches to data acquisition and analysis.
