### TIL: Reevaluating Formal Verification in Software Development

TIL that the traditional approach to formal verification in software development may not be the silver bullet it was once touted to be. The article "The Case Against Formal Verification, 50 Years Later" by Ivan Gavran challenges the notion that formal verification is the ultimate guarantee of software correctness. This raises important questions about the trade-offs between the time and resources invested in formal verification, and the potential benefits it may bring. As a developer, I should be aware that formal verification, while potentially valuable, may not be practical or cost-effective for all projects, and that other verification strategies, such as testing and code reviews, should be considered as complementary approaches to ensure software reliability.
