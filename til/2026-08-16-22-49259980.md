### TIL: Ceramic Water Filter Design Implications for Software Development

TIL that the design of a low-tech ceramic water filter can inform software development in the domain of fault-tolerant system design. The filter's use of multiple ceramic layers with varying pore sizes, which work together to remove contaminants from water, can be seen as analogous to the concept of defense in depth in software design. This approach, where multiple layers of protection work together to prevent a single point of failure, can be applied to software systems to improve their overall reliability and resilience. By considering the ceramic water filter's design principles, software developers can design more robust and fault-tolerant systems that prioritize user safety and system uptime.
