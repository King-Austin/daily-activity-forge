# TIL: AGI-64 Brings Sierra Adventures to the Commodore 64

_2026-08-17 02:39 UTC_

[Original Source](https://meanhamster.com/news/agi-64-brings-sierra-adventures-to-the-commodore-64)

### TIL - Retro-Coding as a Learning Tool

TIL that the AGI-64 project successfully ported the Sierra AGI (Adventure Game Interpreter) engine to the Commodore 64, enabling development of Sierra-style text-based adventure games on this retro platform. This achievement highlights the importance of embracing the diversity of computing architectures in software development, even those considered obsolete today. By exploring the intricacies of retro-coding, developers can gain a deeper understanding of low-level system interactions, data storage, and optimization techniques, all of which remain relevant in modern software engineering.
