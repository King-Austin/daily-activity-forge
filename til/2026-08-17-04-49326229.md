# Today I Learned (TIL) - Gmail's Handling of Sender Rewriting Records (Sender Policy Framework)

TIL that Gmail's use of Sender Policy Framework (SPF) and DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM) might be contributing to the issue of receiving emails from other Sean Conners (https://boston.conman.org/2026/08/11.1). This got me thinking about the potential implications for email authentication. Specifically, the fact that Gmail allows sender rewriting records can create a misaligned sender identity, potentially allowing attackers to spoof legitimate email senders. As a developer, I'll be keeping an eye on this issue and considering how SPF and DKIM can be utilized effectively to prevent email spoofing and ensure sender authenticity.
