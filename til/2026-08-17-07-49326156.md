# TIL: GIMP Development Update

_2026-08-17 07:39 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.gimp.org/news/2026/08/16/dev-update-august-2026/)

TIL that the GIMP development team is actively working on modernizing their UI framework, transitioning from the existing GTK2-based interface to a GTK4-based one, and exploring new technologies such as Vulkan and WebAssembly for improved performance and hardware acceleration. This shift towards more modern and efficient technologies has the potential to significantly impact the future development and maintenance of the project, and could serve as a case study for other open-source projects looking to modernize their architectures and leverage the latest advancements in software development.
