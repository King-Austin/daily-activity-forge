# TIL: Linux HugePages and Transparent HugePage Compaction

_2026-10-15 09:29 UTC_

[Original Source](https://kernel.org/doc/Documentation/vm/hugetlbpage.txt)

HugePages reduce TLB (Translation Lookaside Buffer) misses by increasing page size from 4KB to 2MB or 1GB. For memory-intensive workloads like in-memory databases, this reduces TLB miss overhead by up to 20%.
