# TIL: QUIC Loss Recovery and Congestion Control

_2026-10-17 12:50 UTC_

[Original Source](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc9002)

Today I learned that QUIC eliminates head-of-line blocking by assigning separate sequence spaces to different streams over UDP. Its loss recovery uses monotonic packet numbers rather than byte offsets, removing TCP's retransmission ambiguity problem.
