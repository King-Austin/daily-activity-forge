# TIL: RocksDB Leveled vs Universal Compaction

_2026-10-18 06:25 UTC_

[Original Source](https://github.com/facebook/rocksdb/wiki/Compaction)

Today I learned the trade-offs between Leveled and Universal compaction in LSM-tree key-value stores. Leveled compaction minimizes read amplification and disk space amplification at the cost of higher write amplification, whereas Universal compaction is tailored for write-heavy workloads at the expense of higher temporary disk usage.
