# TIL: Log-Structured File Systems (LFS) and segment cleaning

_2026-10-19 01:18 UTC_

[Original Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Log-structured_file_system)

LFS treats the entire disk as a circular append-only log, converting random writes into sequential writes. A background cleaner thread reclaims free space by compacting live inodes from fragmented segments.
