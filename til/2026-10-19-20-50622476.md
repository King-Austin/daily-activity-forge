# TIL: Network Interface Controller (NIC) Ring Buffers and RX/TX drops

_2026-10-19 20:16 UTC_

[Original Source](https://access.redhat.com/documentation/en-us/red_hat_enterprise_linux/7/html/performance_tuning_guide/sect-red_hat_enterprise_linux-performance_tuning_guide-networking-configuration_tools)

Monitoring NIC drop counters with `ethtool -S` reveals packet discards during bursty traffic. Expanding RX ring buffer size (`ethtool -G eth0 rx 4096`) mitigates packet drops before NAPI polling reclaims descriptors.
