# TIL: TCP BBR Congestion Control Principles

_2026-10-20 02:41 UTC_

[Original Source](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3022184)

Today I learned that Google's BBR congestion control measures the maximum delivery rate and minimum round-trip time instead of relying on packet drops. This achieves significantly higher throughput across lossy transcontinental links compared to loss-based algorithms like CUBIC.
