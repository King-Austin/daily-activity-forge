# TIL: Bloom Filters vs Cuckoo Filters

_2026-10-20 06:15 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dga/papers/cuckoo-conext2014.pdf)

Today I learned that while standard Bloom filters support fast insertion and query without deletion, Cuckoo filters use cuckoo hashing to support dynamic item deletion with lower space overhead for target false-positive rates below 3%.
