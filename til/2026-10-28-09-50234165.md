# TIL: Protobuf Varint Encoding Mechanics

_2026-10-28 09:39 UTC_

[Original Source](https://protobuf.dev/programming-guides/encoding/)

Today I learned how Protocol Buffers use variable-length zig-zag encoding to store signed 64-bit integers in as few as 1 or 2 bytes, where the most significant bit (MSB) of each byte indicates whether further bytes follow.
