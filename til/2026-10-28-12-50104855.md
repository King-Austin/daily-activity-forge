# TIL: V8 Garbage Collection: Orinoco Parallel Scavenger

_2026-10-28 12:43 UTC_

[Original Source](https://v8.dev/blog/orinoco)

Today I learned how V8's modern garbage collector parallelizes nursery collection across worker threads. Young-generation objects that survive two scavenger cycles are promoted to the old generation, reducing pause times to under 2ms in high-throughput Node.js microservices.
