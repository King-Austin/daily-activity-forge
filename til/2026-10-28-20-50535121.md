_2026-10-28 20:07 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html)

Serializable Snapshot Isolation (SSI) tracks dependencies using SIREAD lock tuples rather than locking physical rows, aborting transactions only when dangerous cycle patterns (rw-antidependencies) are detected.
