[Original Source](https://wiki.openjdk.org/display/HotSpot/Safepoints)

The JVM and V8 inject safepoint polls at loop back-edges and method returns. Threads periodically check a guarded memory page or thread-local flag to pause cooperatively for stop-the-world phases.
