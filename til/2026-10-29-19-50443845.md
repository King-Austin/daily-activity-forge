# TIL: TCP keepalive probes and connection cleanup

_2026-10-29 19:07 UTC_

[Original Source](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc1122)

TCP keepalive sends empty ACK packets at configured intervals (`tcp_keepalive_time`, `tcp_keepalive_intvl`, `tcp_keepalive_probes`) to detect unresponsive peers and clean up orphaned socket buffers.
