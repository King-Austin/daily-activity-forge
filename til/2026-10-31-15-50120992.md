# TIL: PostgreSQL MVCC and VACUUM internals

_2026-10-31 15:15 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/routine-vacuuming.html)

Today I learned how PostgreSQL handles dead tuples resulting from UPDATE and DELETE operations. Because rows are immutable under Multi-Version Concurrency Control (MVCC), dead tuples must be periodically cleaned by `autovacuum`. Without sufficient autovacuum workers and cost limits, table bloat occurs and index scans slow down significantly.
