# TIL: Kafka Consumer Group Cooperative Sticky Rebalancing

_2026-11-03 11:15 UTC_

[Original Source](https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/display/KAFKA/KIP-429)

Today I learned that Kafka's `CooperativeStickyAssignor` allows consumer groups to reassign only the partitions that need moving rather than revoking all partitions during a rebalance. This prevents massive lag spikes and keeps data streaming during rolling deployments.
