It’s a wild reminder that "garbage" isn’t actually garbage until you decide to ignore it; the fact that a curse ritual survived in a trash dump highlights how persistent and structurally intact data can remain in uncurated storage environments. For us, this is a stark metaphor for the value of raw telemetry, crash logs, and deprecated feature flags that we usually purge as noise—sometimes the most critical insights into system failures or user behavior are buried in the "trash" of our logging infrastructure, waiting to be excavated by someone who knows what they’re looking for, rather than lost to aggressive retention policies.
