# TIL: Curiosity Is No Solo Act

_2026-11-26 16:20 UTC_

[Original Source](https://thereader.mitpress.mit.edu/curiosity-is-no-solo-act/)

TIL that intrinsic motivation in software development is fundamentally a social construct, not just an individual trait; reading "Curiosity Is No Solo Act" reinforced that our drive to explore new architectures or debug obscure edge cases is heavily mediated by peer interaction and shared context. This suggests that effective engineering culture shouldn't just rely on open-ended "innovation time," but should actively design collaboration surfaces—like pair programming rotations or cross-team tech talks—that leverage collective curiosity to amplify individual problem-solving, rather than assuming that isolation leads to deeper technical insight.
