# TIL: George Whitesides, 1939-2026

_2026-11-26 19:36 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.science.org/content/blog-post/george-whitesides-1939-2026)

**TIL:** George Whitesides, the MIT chemist who pioneered self-assembly and "chemical engineering" solutions to complex manufacturing problems (like his work on microfluidics and soft robotics), has passed away. For software devs, his legacy is a stark reminder of the physical layer's constraints: while we often treat compute as abstract, the future of edge AI and specialized hardware will likely depend on his principles of bottom-up fabrication and low-cost, high-density integration. It’s a good prompt to think about how our abstractions might shift if the cost of specialized, heterogeneous compute drops dramatically due to these chemical engineering breakthroughs, rather than just scaling up general-purpose silicon.
