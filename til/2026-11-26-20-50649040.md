# TIL: The USAID Files

_2026-11-26 20:06 UTC_

[Original Source](https://reclaimthenet.org/usaid-files)

Came across the "USAID Files" leak and it’s a stark reminder that government agencies are running on legacy stacks with zero modern security posture; seeing internal architectures and potentially exposed credentials in the wild is a masterclass in why we can’t rely on "security through obscurity" or assume that non-tech institutions have basic IAM controls, especially when dealing with sensitive diplomatic or humanitarian data. For devs, the takeaway is twofold: first, if you’re building for or integrating with government entities, assume their infrastructure is brittle and audit third-party dependencies rigorously; second, it’s a sobering look at how quickly "classified" or internal data becomes public when the underlying systems aren’t hardened, reinforcing the need for defense-in-depth and regular red-teaming exercises even in low-priority environments.
