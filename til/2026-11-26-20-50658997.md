# TIL: The USA as a Civilizations-esque map

_2026-11-26 20:21 UTC_

[Original Source](https://karpathy.ai/usaciv/)

It’s fascinating to see Andrej Karpathy frame the US through a *Civilization*-style grid, essentially treating national geography as a stateful game board where resources, borders, and "units" (cities/populations) interact under discrete rules. For developers, this is a tangible lesson in **abstraction layers**: by reducing complex, continuous real-world systems to a manageable, turn-based logic grid, you gain a powerful mental model for simulation, optimization, and debugging. It highlights how many large-scale systems—whether logistics networks, cloud infrastructure, or even security domains—can be better understood and engineered when mapped onto simplified, deterministic state machines rather than trying to solve for every variable simultaneously.
