# TIL: Hard Drive pricing has gotten bad

_2026-11-27 02:53 UTC_

[Original Source](https://blog.ktz.me/black-friday-hard-drive-prices-are-messed-up/)

Hard drive price volatility is rarely just a commodity quirk; for developers, it’s a direct signal to audit storage assumptions in our infrastructure. If HDDs are becoming a scarce or expensive resource for local caching, tiered storage strategies, or even just maintaining local dev environments with large datasets, we need to reconsider reliance on cheap local block storage. This might push us further toward object storage, aggressive data lifecycle policies, or re-evaluating whether our local dev setups actually need that much persistent disk space versus ephemeral storage. It’s a reminder that "storage is cheap" is a hallucination that breaks under supply chain stress, so hardcoding assumptions about local disk availability or cost in CI/CD pipelines or local tooling is a risk worth mitigating now.
