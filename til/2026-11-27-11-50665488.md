# TIL: Fields-winning mathematicians worried about eerie silence over AI and encryption

_2026-11-27 11:39 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.thetimes.com/uk/technology-uk/article/ai-making-online-security-useless-smr73d3n8)

**TIL:** The recent silence from Fields Medalists regarding the intersection of AI and cryptography signals a potential paradigm shift beyond just "quantum resistance." If LLMs or advanced neural architectures can extract structural weaknesses in elliptic curve implementations or accelerate lattice-based attacks, the assumption that RSA/ECC are safe for the next 30 years is crumbling faster than predicted. For devs, this means we need to stop treating post-quantum cryptography (PQC) as a future-proofing checkbox and start auditing our key management strategies now; specifically, watch for NIST PQC standardization updates (like Kyber for key encapsulation and Dilithium for signatures) and consider hybrid encryption schemes that combine classical and post-quantum primitives to mitigate the risk of a "harvest now, decrypt later" attack vector becoming viable sooner than expected.
