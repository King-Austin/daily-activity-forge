# TIL: US Air Force's cloud computing platform hacked

_2026-11-27 13:55 UTC_

[Original Source](https://news.sky.com/story/us-air-forces-cloud-computing-platform-hacked-13617163)

TIL that the US Air Force’s cloud platform breach underscores a critical security blind spot: even when infrastructure is outsourced to major providers, the identity and access management layer remains the primary attack vector. For developers, this reinforces that "cloud-native" doesn't automatically mean "secure-by-default"; it’s a stark reminder to treat zero-trust architecture not as a buzzword but as a requirement, specifically ensuring that service-to-service authentication, API gateway protections, and least-privilege IAM policies are rigorously audited, because the perimeter is effectively gone and the data itself is the new surface area.
