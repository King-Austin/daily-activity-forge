# TIL: Counseling for mental health decreases likelihood of protesting by 34%–39%

_2026-11-27 13:25 UTC_

[Original Source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/ssqu.70219)

It’s striking that a 34–39% reduction in protest likelihood correlates with mental health counseling, a finding that challenges the simplistic "unhappy user = high churn" heuristic often baked into product analytics. For us building systems, it’s a reminder that user sentiment data is rarely a direct proxy for behavioral outcomes; psychological support mechanisms can decouple dissatisfaction from adversarial action, meaning our prediction models for churn or community toxicity need to account for non-linear, external interventions rather than assuming a linear relationship between negative feedback and destructive behavior.
