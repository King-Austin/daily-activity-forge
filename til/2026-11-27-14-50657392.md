TIL that ESA’s HERA mission is currently navigating the debris field left by NASA’s DART impact on Dimorphos, offering a rare real-world testbed for autonomous target tracking in high-velocity, non-Keplerian environments. For software devs, this is a stark reminder that deterministic physics simulations break down in chaotic multi-body systems; it highlights the critical need for robust, adaptive control loops and fault-tolerant state estimation algorithms that can handle unexpected perturbations and sensor degradation without human intervention, a principle directly applicable to designing resilient distributed systems or robotics that must operate reliably under uncertain, dynamic conditions.
