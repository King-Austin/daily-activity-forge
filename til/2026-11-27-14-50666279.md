# TIL: New York and California Age Gating Rules Go into Effect in the New Year

_2026-11-27 14:10 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/11/new-york-and-california-age-gating-rules-go-effect-new-year)

NY and CA’s age-gating mandates force a significant pivot from simple client-side date-of-birth prompts to server-side identity verification, likely necessitating integrations with third-party IDV providers or strict, auditable logging of "age attestation" events to satisfy state compliance. For backend engineers, this isn't just a UI toggle; it implies a new class of PII handling where we must securely store verification status without retaining full government ID documents, requiring careful design of data retention policies and clear separation between user-facing authentication state and compliance records to avoid both legal liability and privacy violations under existing frameworks like CCPA.
