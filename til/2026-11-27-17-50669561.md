# TIL: I'm Scared of AI

_2026-11-27 17:56 UTC_

[Original Source](https://thesoapbox.email/archive/3)

Read [this piece](https://thesoapbox.email/archive/3) on the anxiety surrounding AI integration, which highlights a critical tension in modern dev workflows: the shift from writing code to managing probabilistic outputs. The core technical takeaway isn't just about tooling, but about the erosion of deterministic debugging—when an LLM suggests a fix, the "root cause" is often obscured by the model's hallucinations, forcing developers to invest more time in verification than generation. This suggests a near-future competency shift where "prompt engineering" is less about crafting inputs and more about designing robust validation layers and guardrails to ensure that AI-generated artifacts meet the strict correctness standards that traditional CI/CD pipelines enforce.
