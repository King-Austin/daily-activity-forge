# TIL: Google's DeepCrossAttention achieves comparable model quality up to 3x faster

_2026-11-28 00:57 UTC_

[Original Source](https://research.google/blog/supercharging-transformer-residual-connections/)

Google’s DeepCrossAttention (DCA) is a compelling example of how architectural micro-optimizations in the residual stream can yield massive efficiency gains without sacrificing accuracy; by replacing standard skip connections with a lightweight cross-attention mechanism that allows earlier layers to directly attend to later layer representations, DCA effectively mitigates the "highway" problem where gradients vanish or information gets lost in deep stacks. For devs, this suggests that the next wave of inference speedups might come less from hardware-specific quantization and more from fundamental changes to how transformer blocks share information, potentially allowing us to deploy larger context windows or higher model capacities on the same silicon budget.
