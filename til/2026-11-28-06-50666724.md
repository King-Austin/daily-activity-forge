# TIL: Paolo Soleri built a utopian eco-city in the Arizona desert

_2026-11-28 06:43 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www.worldofinteriors.com/story/paolo-soleri-arconsati-arizona)

While debugging a monolithic legacy codebase, I realized Paolo Soleri’s *ArcoSanti* project is the physical equivalent of what we call "big ball of mud" architecture; he designed a self-contained, closed-loop system where every component (water, energy, structure) had to interface perfectly without external dependencies, only for the rigid coupling to eventually fail under real-world entropy. It’s a stark reminder that while building a "utopian" all-in-one platform feels elegant in theory, the lack of modular boundaries and the inability to swap out failing subsystems without tearing down the whole city is exactly why we microservice our APIs and isolate infrastructure components—resilience comes from loose coupling, not from trying to engineer a perfect, hermetically sealed ecosystem.
