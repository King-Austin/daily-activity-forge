# TIL: Knuth: Moratorium on Error Reports

_2026-11-28 09:59 UTC_

[Original Source](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/moratorium.html)

Knuth’s "Moratorium on Error Reports" is a fascinating, albeit controversial, stance where he argues that bugginess is an inherent part of complex software and that stopping the flood of bug reports allows for more productive work on new features rather than endless regression testing. For developers, this challenges the modern DevOps obsession with zero-defect releases; it suggests that at a certain scale, the cost of chasing every minor edge case outweighs the value, pushing teams to prioritize architectural resilience and graceful degradation over exhaustive test coverage for every possible input. It’s a stark reminder that "done" is often a business decision, not just a technical one.
