# TIL: No perfect way to find the love of your life

_2026-11-28 11:44 UTC_

[Original Source](https://yappings.mikeluu.xyz/blogs/the-perfect-partner)

Today I Learned that the search for an ideal partner is fundamentally an unsolvable optimization problem with no global minimum, which serves as a stark reminder that in software engineering, we often mistakenly assume that if we just add enough constraints (filtering by age, location, interests), we can converge on a "perfect" state. In reality, relationship dynamics are a non-convex landscape full of local maxima and shifting variables, meaning that chasing a static, theoretical "perfect" specification leads to overfitting and analysis paralysis. The technical takeaway is that this applies to system design and career choices too: instead of trying to solve for the absolute best option via exhaustive search, we should prioritize heuristics, accept local optima, and build systems (and lives) that are resilient to change and allow for iterative refinement rather than seeking a single, immutable solution.
