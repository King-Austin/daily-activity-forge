# TIL: Farewell

_2026-11-28 11:59 UTC_

[Original Source](https://github.com/p-e-w/heretic/issues/601)

I stumbled upon the "Farewell" issue in the `heretic` repo, which serves as a stark reminder that even niche, long-standing open-source projects can hit a sudden, unannounced EOL without the usual deprecation warnings or migration paths. For developers, this is a critical lesson in supply chain risk assessment: if a dependency—no matter how small or specialized—disappears overnight, your build pipeline and runtime stability are instantly compromised. It underscores the need to audit transitive dependencies not just for security CVEs, but for maintainer burnout or project abandonment, and to proactively vendor critical code or maintain internal forks before you’re left scrambling to patch a dead codebase in production.
