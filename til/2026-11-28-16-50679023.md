# TIL: If AI takes every job who will buy things?

_2026-11-28 16:30 UTC_

[Original Source](https://coryalthoff.com/essays/if-ai-takes-every-job-who-will-buy-things)

TIL that the "who buys?" paradox isn't just an economic abstraction but a hard constraint on AI deployment strategies; if autonomous agents replace human labor, the resulting contraction in consumer purchasing power creates a market failure that no amount of technical optimization can solve, suggesting that future software architectures might need to account for "economic viability" not just as a business metric, but as a systemic risk factor—potentially shifting focus from pure automation to human-in-the-loop models or new forms of value distribution to sustain the very market these tools serve.
