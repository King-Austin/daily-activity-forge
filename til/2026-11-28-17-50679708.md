# TIL: Computer Disease

_2026-11-28 17:46 UTC_

[Original Source](https://blog.adafruit.com/2020/01/13/richard-feynman-on-computer-disease/)

**TIL:** Richard Feynman’s "Computer Disease" warning—popularized in a recent Adafruit blog post—remains a sharp critique of modern software engineering’s tendency to treat complex systems as merely larger versions of simple ones. The core insight is that as system size grows, the cognitive load and emergent behaviors often exceed the capacity of traditional top-down design or unit testing, leading to brittle architectures that fail in ways no one predicted. For developers, this suggests that scaling isn’t just about adding more microservices or lines of code; it requires fundamentally shifting how we model dependencies, prioritize observability over just correctness, and accept that some system behaviors are only discoverable through rigorous, real-world interaction rather than static analysis.
