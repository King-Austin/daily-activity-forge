# TIL: GTA 6 Graphics Rendering: What We Know So Far

_2026-11-28 18:16 UTC_

[Original Source](https://ben3d.ca/blog/gta-6-graphics-rendering-what-we-know-so-far)

**TIL** regarding GTA 6’s rendering tech: The shift from a single global lighting solution to a hybrid approach involves baking complex lightmaps for static urban geometry while reserving real-time ray tracing primarily for dynamic elements like reflections and shadows. For developers, this highlights a pragmatic constraint-driven architecture—rather than chasing full-path tracing on consumer hardware, Rockstar is likely leveraging pre-computed data to offload the heavy lifting, allowing RT cores to focus on high-frequency, visually critical artifacts. It’s a strong case study in how AAA engines balance fidelity with performance budgets by treating lighting as a tiered pipeline rather than a monolithic calculation.
