# TIL: The race is not inevitable

_2026-11-28 18:31 UTC_

[Original Source](https://ryo.lu/journal/the-race-is-not-inevitable)

Read Ryo Lu’s piece on why the AI arms race isn’t a deterministic outcome, which reframed my thinking on how we should approach model training and infrastructure investment. It’s easy to fall into the "compute scaling is the only path" trap, but the argument that strategic choices in data curation, alignment, and open-source collaboration can alter the trajectory is a crucial reminder that engineering decisions are political and ethical levers, not just technical optimizations. For devs, this means we shouldn’t just blindly chase SOTA benchmarks; we need to critically evaluate whether our contributions to the ecosystem actually reinforce concentration of power or enable more distributed, verifiable, and resilient AI systems.
