# TIL: Donald Knuth suspends error bounties due to an influx of AI-generated reports

_2026-11-28 18:01 UTC_

[Original Source](https://cs.stanford.edu/~knuth/moratorium.html)

It’s a stark reminder that the "garbage in, garbage out" problem has moved from data pipelines to the trust model of open-source maintenance; Knuth’s moratorium highlights how LLMs can flood issue trackers with plausible-sounding but contextually blind "bugs," forcing maintainers to spend cycles on triage rather than fixing actual defects. For us developers, this accelerates the need for stricter, automated validation gates in CI/CD pipelines that can distinguish between high-signal human feedback and low-signal AI noise, while also prompting a re-evaluation of how we structure bug reports to be robust against automated misinterpretation.
