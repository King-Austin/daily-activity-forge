# TIL: 5.0 Earthquake West of LA

_2026-11-28 19:31 UTC_

[Original Source](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/ci41581136/executive)

A 5.0 magnitude quake west of LA is a timely reminder that infrastructure resilience isn’t just about cloud provider SLAs but also about the physical cable routes and data center locations that underpin our "always-on" assumptions. For developers, this underscores the value of designing for graceful degradation and eventual consistency, especially in distributed systems where a localized seismic event could temporarily degrade latency or availability in specific regions, potentially triggering cascading failures if retry logic is too aggressive or if health checks are tuned too tightly to normal network conditions.
