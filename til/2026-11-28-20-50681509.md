# TIL: Gmail "Send as" ends January 2027

_2026-11-28 20:31 UTC_

[Original Source](https://emailfa.st/blog/gmail-send-as-ending-cloudflare-email-routing)

**TIL:** Gmail is sunsetting the "Send as" feature for third-party services in January 2027, effectively forcing a migration to Cloudflare Email Routing or similar dedicated sending infrastructure. This signals a significant tightening of Gmail’s anti-spam policies, likely moving beyond just SPF/DKIM validation to stricter sender reputation and domain ownership checks that generic "send-as" integrations can no longer satisfy. For developers, this is a hard deadline to audit any email-sending dependencies that rely on impersonation or shared sending pools; we’ll need to shift to dedicated subdomains with explicit DNS records and potentially higher deliverability overhead to maintain inbox placement after the cutoff.
