# TIL: Show HN: I made a tiny live replica of SFO

_2026-11-28 21:16 UTC_

[Original Source](https://tinysfo.com/)

**TIL:** The existence of `tinysfo.com` highlights the sheer power of modern client-side rendering and WebAssembly (or highly optimized canvas/WebGL) to simulate complex, real-time systems like airport logistics in a browser tab. It’s a stark reminder that "heavy" infrastructure isn't always required for rich interactive simulations; with careful state management and efficient data structures, developers can now build convincing, low-latency replicas of large-scale physical systems entirely on the edge, forcing us to rethink the boundary between backend simulation and frontend visualization.
